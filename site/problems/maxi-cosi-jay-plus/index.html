Maxi-Cosi Jay Plus: known problems

3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Jay Plus feeding booster play arch can break after repeated use and release small parts — French official recall

This is the official French government consumer-recall database (RappelConso), reference 2025-10-0198, voluntary recall by Dorel France — an independent second official confirmation of the same defect.

What to check. Same corrective action as the UK notice: stop using the removable toy tray; the seat and standard tray remain safe. This confirms the defect is EU-wide, not limited to one market.

Reported on: rappel.conso.gouv.fr

Worth checking

Jay Plus Spielbogen (play arch) recall confirmed in Germany — reinforced replacement arch offered

German consumer-warning site confirming the recall and noting Maxi-Cosi offers a reinforced replacement play arch — useful for what the fix is.

What to check. Confirms the recall covers all batches of the Jay Plus and that the manufacturer's remedy is a reinforced replacement arch, obtained via the retailer or eu-consumercare@maxi-cosi.com.

Reported on: produktwarnung.eu

Minor

Maxi-Cosi's own safety-notice page lists the Jay Plus recall (01/07/2025 – 10/10/2025)

The manufacturer's own (Dorel Juvenile Europe) safety-notice page lists the Jay Plus recall with the same sales window, confirming the official status of the recall.

What to check. This is the manufacturer's own confirmation; owners should follow the official instructions on this page or contact Maxi-Cosi consumer care.

Reported on: maxi-cosi.es
