APU power limit hard-locks to ~4-7W on wake from sleep (SteamOS 3.8)

Serious

Owner report on the Valve SteamOS tracker for the RC71L Z1 Extreme (BIOS 342) that after sleep/wake the APU is stuck at the lowest TDP, making the device effectively unusable until reboot.

What to checkIf you run SteamOS/Bazzite on the Z1 Extreme, wake-from-sleep can leave the APU locked at ~4-7W; a reboot is the workaround. Check for a fixed SteamOS build before buying for this use.

Reported ongithub.com

ROG Ally microSD card reader malfunctions under thermal stress (ASUS-confirmed)

Serious

ASUS itself confirmed the reader can malfunction under thermal stress, with some users' cards permanently damaged.

What to checkTreat the microSD slot as unreliable for long sessions; prefer the internal SSD or a larger 2230 SSD for storage. If a card stops being read, test it in another device before assuming the card is dead.

Reported ontech.yahoo.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

microSD card reader malfunctions under thermal stress (official ASUS confirmation)

Serious

News report quoting an ASUS representative confirming the integrated microSD reader can fail under heat; ASUS shipped a fan-speed update to mitigate, but damaged readers need board-level repair.

What to checkDo not rely on the microSD slot for primary storage on the Z1 Extreme; heat from the exhaust-adjacent reader can kill cards. Keep long sessions off the SD card or accept the risk.

Reported onextremetech.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking3

Fatal D3D device-hung crash (DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_HUNG) during games

To check

Microsoft Q&A thread on a ROG Ally RC71L with the Ryzen Z1 Extreme hitting a fatal D3D device-hung error (0x887a0006) during games under Windows 11.

What to checkExpect occasional graphics-driver crashes on the Z1 Extreme under Windows; the DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_HUNG error is a known pattern that driver/BIOS updates may or may not fix. Keep GPU drivers current.

Reported onlearn.microsoft.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

MediaTek MT7922 WiFi disconnects and networks disappear until reboot

To check

Owner report on the Bazzite forum that the MT7922 WiFi card drops all networks minutes after boot and requires a reboot; the card is partially functional (Bluetooth still works).

What to checkWiFi dropouts on the Z1 Extreme are reported with the MediaTek MT7922 card, often under Linux; a reboot is the usual workaround. Treat this as a driver/firmware issue rather than a dead card.

Reported onuniversal-blue.discourse.group

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

ROG Ally APU hard-locks at minimum TDP on wake from sleep (SteamOS 3.8)

To check

On the SteamOS/Linux build, waking from sleep can lock the APU at ~4-7W, causing severe stutter until restart or AC re-plug.

What to checkThis is specific to the SteamOS (Linux) configuration, not stock Windows. If running SteamOS on the Ally, a wake-from-sleep power hang is a known regression; a restart or AC re-plug recovers it.

Reported ongithub.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

Built-in screen flickers when VRR is enabled

Minor

Bazzite issue tracker report that the ROG Ally's built-in screen flickers heavily in-game when VRR is enabled, and stops when VRR is turned off.

What to checkIf you see screen flicker on the Z1 Extreme, try disabling VRR first; the flicker is tied to the VRR toggle rather than a dead panel.

Reported ongithub.com
