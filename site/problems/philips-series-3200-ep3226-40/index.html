Philips EP3226/40 grinder stops turning; machine dispenses water only

Serious

An owner reports the grinder stopped turning and the machine dispenses only water. A forum respondent confirms that if the grinder (moulin) no longer turns, the machine must go to service (SAV). This is a core failure that makes the machine useless for espresso.

What to checkIf the grinder stops and only water comes out, this is not a user-serviceable fix per the forum; the machine needs service. Check warranty status before paying for repair.

Philips EP3226/40 shows three red lights; pump cycle fails after prior repair

Serious

An owner reports three red lights on and no pump cycle engaging, on a machine already repaired once at 3–4 years old. This indicates a recurring electrical/pump failure that required service before.

What to checkThree red lights with no pump cycle is a serious fault. The machine already needed one repair at 3–4 years, suggesting recurring reliability issues. Budget for potential service costs.

Worth checking2

Philips EP3226/40 leaks water underneath; flow meter blocked by limescale

To check

An owner reports water leaking underneath and behind the machine after cleaning, with a wrench (clé plate) error displayed. Another owner resolved the same issue by disassembling the machine and unblocking the flow meter, which was clogged by limescale. This is a maintenance issue tied to scale buildup.

What to checkRegular descaling is critical. If water leaks underneath and the machine shows a wrench error, the flow meter may be blocked by scale. The fix requires disassembly, so consider professional service if not comfortable.

Reported onsav.darty.com

Philips Series 3200 ceramic burrs are fragile; Error 1 signals blocked grinder

To check

A professional repair service states that Philips/Saeco Series 3200 machines use ceramic burrs, which are fragile (высокая хрупкость керамики). Error 1 on the display means the grinder is blocked and cannot rotate. This is a known failure mode requiring service.

What to checkThe ceramic burrs are fragile. If Error 1 appears, the grinder is blocked — do not force it. Professional cleaning or burr replacement may be needed.

Reported oncofferemont.ru
