Hotpoint tumble dryers recalled for fire risk from fluff contacting heating element (models April 2004–September 2015)

SeriousSafety risk

The documented fire risk is fluff contacting the heating element. The recall window ends 30 September 2015, so post-2015 Hotpoint dryers fall outside this recall; the risk applies to 2015 and earlier production.

What to checkIf the dryer was made in 2015 or earlier, check the model/serial against the affected list and unplug if unmodified. Post-2015 units are outside this recall window.

Reported onassets.publishing.service.gov.uk

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

UK government ordered recall of up to 500,000 unmodified Hotpoint/Indesit/Creda tumble dryers (June 2019)

Serious

The Government instructed Whirlpool to recall 500,000 tumble dryers in June 2019, four years after the fault was first flagged. This is the scale of the documented fire risk for Hotpoint-branded dryers.

What to checkConfirms the fire risk is real and regulator-recognised, with hundreds of fires linked to these machines. Check any Hotpoint dryer made up to 2015.

Reported onparliament.uk

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Whirlpool/Hotpoint recall campaign scope: dryers sold April 2004–October 2015 under Hotpoint, Indesit, Creda

Serious

This is the manufacturer's own November 2015 announcement of the corrective campaign, confirming the affected production window (April 2004–October 2015) and brands. It corroborates the recall scope from the manufacturer's side.

What to checkThis confirms the manufacturer-side scope of the recall. Post-2015 Hotpoint dryers are outside this documented campaign window.
