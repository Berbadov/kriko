'OK Garmin' hands-free voice feature cuts Fenix 8 battery life by 35–50%

Serious

The voice wake-word feature, enabled by default in a September update, listens constantly and can halve battery life. Garmin acknowledges the drain but not the magnitude.

What to checkIf your Fenix 8 battery life dropped sharply after a software update, go to Settings > System > Voice and disable 'OK Garmin' to restore expected runtime.

Reported ontech.yahoo.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Fenix 8 display brightness stuck at maximum after sunlight exposure (firmware 23.31), draining battery

Serious

Garmin confirmed the bug after dozens of forum reports; it is a firmware regression (23.31) that also shortens battery life because the screen stays at max brightness.

What to checkIf your Fenix 8 screen stays stuck at full brightness after being in sunlight, touch the screen or press a button to reset it until Garmin ships the fix. Check for the next firmware update.

Reported onnotebookcheck.net

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking4

Fenix 8 GPS acquisition slow (20s–3min) before firmware 12.38 fix

To check

A GPS acquisition bug made satellite lock take up to 3 minutes; Garmin fixed it in firmware 12.38, restoring ~5-second locks.

What to checkIf your Fenix 8 is slow to get a GPS fix, update to firmware 12.38 or later.

Reported onnotebookcheck.net

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Garmin Connect iOS app update broke Fenix 8 smartphone connectivity

To check

A Garmin Connect iOS update broke Bluetooth pairing for Fenix 8 owners; fixed in app version 5.21.1, though some users needed to re-pair manually.

What to checkIf your Fenix 8 won't connect to an iPhone, update Garmin Connect to 5.21.1+; if it still fails, forget and re-pair the watch in Bluetooth settings.

Reported onnotebookcheck.net

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Persistent Fenix 8 battery drain after firmware 22.39; Wi-Fi stays active after Spotify sync

To check

Battery drain persists for some owners even after Garmin's 22.39 fix; a suspected cause is Wi-Fi staying active after a Spotify sync.

What to checkIf battery drain persists after updating, try disabling Wi-Fi manually or re-pairing; check Garmin's forum for the latest workaround.

Reported onnotebookcheck.net

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Turkish owner reports Fenix 8 self-resetting when starting a run; fixed by re-update via Garmin Express

To check

A Turkish owner (Koşu Forum) reports the watch repeatedly resetting when starting a running activity; re-updating via Garmin Express resolved it. This is the only TR-market owner report found.

What to checkIf your Fenix 8 resets when starting an activity, re-update the firmware via Garmin Express on a computer.

Reported onkosuforum.com
