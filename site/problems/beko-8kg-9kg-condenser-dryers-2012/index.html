Beko 8/9kg condenser dryers (DCU9330W/R, DCU8230, DSC85W, TKF8439A) recalled for fire risk — component may overheat

Safety risk

Official UK government recall notice listing exactly the subject models, with recall date 31/05/2017 and production window May–November 2012.

What to check. If you own one of these models manufactured between May and November 2012, stop using it and contact Beko on 0800 917 2018 for a free in-home check and modification.

Reported on: gov.uk

Beko voluntary repair programme for 8/9kg condenser dryers — third party component can fail and overheat

Manufacturer's own safety notice confirming the same affected models and describing the defect as a third-party supplied component that can fail and overheat.

What to check. Beko offers a free in-home check and modification (about 30 minutes). Owners of affected models should register for the repair via Beko's website or call 0800 917 2018.

Reported on: beko.co.uk

Isle of Man Office of Fair Trading confirms Beko 8/9kg condenser dryer fire risk notice

Safety risk

Independent regulator (Isle of Man OFT) confirming the same recall details, providing a second independent source for the fire risk claim.

What to check. This is an independent regulator confirming the manufacturer's recall. Owners should check their model and serial number and contact Beko if affected.

Reported on: gov.im
