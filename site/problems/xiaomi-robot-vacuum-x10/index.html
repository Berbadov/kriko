Xiaomi Robot Vacuum X10+: known problems

4 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Omni/docking station leaks water; mop-wash catch basin overflows

Multiple independent owners report the docking station leaking water around it and the mop-wash basin overflowing, with one owner noting the intake valve itself was not the leak point.

What to check. Before buying, check the station's water plumbing and the mop-wash tray seal; a leak here can damage flooring and is reported to recur even after service. Expect this to be a warranty/repair issue rather than a user-fixable one.

Reported on: alza.cz

Water ingress into robot body oxidises motherboard; warranty refused with ~€187 quote

An Italian owner reports the wall base leaking water at month 23; the service centre refused warranty repair citing water infiltration and motherboard oxidation, quoting €187 — nearly the unit's full value. This documents both the leak and the service/warranty cost of it.

What to check. The leak is not just cosmetic: water reaching the robot's mainboard is treated as out-of-warranty damage, and the quoted repair (~€187) approaches the product's value. Verify warranty terms before relying on coverage for a leak.

Reported on: altroconsumo.it

Worth checking

Dirty water pump damaged by cleaning agents; sealing ring warps and leaks

Xiaomi's own UK FAQ confirms the dirty water pump can be damaged by adding cleaning agents and that a warped sealing ring causes leaks, matching the owner-reported station leaks.

What to check. Use only plain water in the tanks; adding cleaning agents is officially cited as a cause of pump damage. If the pump fails or the seal warps, the station cannot drain dirty water and may leak, which typically needs service.

Reported on: mi.com

Unit stopped working after ~1.5 years and could not return to charging station

Owners report the unit failing to return to its charging station and stopping working after roughly 1.5 years, with one unit needing three repairs. This is a reliability/longevity concern for a flagship model.

What to check. If the robot cannot dock, it cannot recharge or self-clean, effectively ending its usefulness. Several owners report this appearing after a year or more, so consider expected lifespan and warranty length.

Reported on: alza.cz
