AdBlue crystallization and NOx sensor failures in 1.5 Blue dCi engines

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

The 1.5 Blue dCi engines in the Megane 4 suffer from AdBlue crystallization and NOx sensor failures, causing 'starting impossible' warnings, limp mode, and reduced power. This is a chronic issue tied to the SCR system’s sensitivity to AdBlue quality and environmental conditions.

What to checkCheck for 'starting impossible' warnings, verify NOx sensor functionality, and inspect the AdBlue system for crystallization or blockages. Ensure high-quality AdBlue is used.

Reported ondiagnosticgarage.com

EDC dual-clutch transmission sensitivity to heat and software calibration (Megane 4)

Serious

The EDC dual-clutch transmission in the Megane 4 (2016–2022) is prone to jerky gear shifts, delayed take-offs, and warning messages due to heat sensitivity and software calibration issues. This is a chronic reliability flaw in the transmission control system.

What to checkTest for jerky shifts, delayed acceleration, and transmission warning messages. Verify transmission oil levels and condition, and check for software updates.

Reported ondiagnosticgarage.com

Turbocharger failure due to oil neglect in 1.5 dCi engines

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

Turbochargers in the 1.5 dCi engines fail prematurely due to delayed oil changes and low-quality oil, causing loss of acceleration, excessive black smoke, and whistling noises. This is a chronic issue tied to maintenance habits rather than design alone but is exacerbated by Renault’s turbo design sensitivity.

What to checkListen for whistling noises from the engine, check for excessive black smoke, and verify oil change intervals. Inspect turbo shaft play and intercooler hoses for wear or leaks.

Reported ondiagnosticgarage.com

Reported onwhat-breaks.com

Premature suspension wear (stabilizer links, control arm bushings, shock absorbers)

To checkNoticed on a test drive

The Megane 3 and Megane 4 experience early wear of stabilizer links, control arm bushings, and shock absorbers, leading to knocking noises, steering vibrations, and reduced road holding. This is a chronic issue tied to design stress and material choices in the suspension components.

What to checkListen for knocking noises from the front suspension, check for steering wheel vibrations, and inspect suspension components for wear or damage during a road test.

Reported ondiagnosticgarage.com

R-Link multimedia system software bugs and electrical instability (Megane 4)

To checkFound by looking

The Megane 4’s R-Link multimedia system suffers from software bugs causing screen freezes, Bluetooth drops, and rear-view camera malfunctions. Voltage fluctuations in the electrical system further exacerbate these issues, indicating a chronic design flaw in the system’s integration.

What to checkTest the multimedia system for screen freezes, Bluetooth connectivity, and rear-view camera functionality. Check battery and charging system health during a diagnostic scan.

Reported ondiagnosticgarage.com
