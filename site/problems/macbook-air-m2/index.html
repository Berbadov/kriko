MacBook Air M2 image and keyboard faults traced to motherboard, replaced twice without resolution

Serious

Apple's authorized service traced it to the motherboard, replaced it, and said a second motherboard would be needed. The owner was still without a working unit as of 27/05/2026.

What to checkA recurring image/keyboard fault on an M2 Air may be a motherboard problem that survives a screen swap; if you hit repeated repairs under warranty, push for full unit replacement rather than accepting endless part swaps.

Reported onocu.org

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

MacBook Air M2 logic board failure after ~3 years of normal use

Serious

The authorized diagnosis blamed a logic board failure, which the complainant notes is a documented, recurring fault on M2 Macs. The seller/Apple refused free repair under the EU 3-year conformity guarantee.

What to checkExpect the logic board to be a failure point on M2 Airs in the third year; keep proof of purchase and the EU legal guarantee (DL 84/2021) in hand, because the seller may try to charge for a motherboard replacement out of warranty.

Reported ondeco.proteste.pt

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

MacBook Air M2 swollen battery, 170€ charged out of warranty

Serious

By the 3rd year Apple confirmed a swollen battery and charged 170€, claiming out of warranty, while the owner argues the latent defect/safety risk (fire hazard) should be covered by the legal guarantee.

What to checkA swollen battery on an M2 Air is a safety issue that can surface as a chemical smell early; document any smell within the warranty period, because Apple may otherwise charge ~170€ to replace the battery later.

Reported ondeco.proteste.pt

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking1

MacBook Air M2 screen scratched by debris trapped between keyboard and display

To check

This matches the known M2 Air keyboard-marks-on-screen issue.

What to checkOn the M2 Air the keyboard can mark or scratch the display when the lid closes over debris; use a screen/keyboard protector and keep the deck clean if you want to avoid permanent screen damage.

Reported oncomfy.ua
