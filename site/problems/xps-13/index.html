XPS 13 7390 2-in-1 suffers daily blue screens

Serious

An owner of the XPS 13 7390 2-in-1 reports daily blue screens from day one, with Dell diagnostics finding no hardware fault.

What to checkIf you buy a 7390 and see repeated BSODs, push Dell for a resolution early; the owner was told software issues are not covered under basic warranty.

XPS 13 9315 screen cracks from camera/screen design stress point

Serious

A long-time customer describes a design flaw where the camera and screen meet, creating a stress concentration that cracks the screen.

What to checkHandle the lid carefully; a cracked screen from normal use may be denied under warranty, so check coverage before purchase.

Reported ondell.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

XPS 13 display fails via eDP cable/hinge fault when lid opened

Serious

An owner's display blanks as the lid is opened past one-third and then fails entirely while an external monitor works; the diagnosis points to a display cable/hinge fault rather than the GPU.

What to checkIf the screen cuts out when the lid is moved, suspect the eDP cable routed through the hinge; repair involves replacing the cable/panel, typically out of warranty.

Reported ondell.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

XPS 13 overheats and required fan replacement

Serious

An owner of a high-end XPS 13 (€2,518) reports severe overheating that forced a fan replacement, yet the machine still runs excessively hot afterwards.

What to checkCheck thermals early; if the unit runs hot even after a fan swap, expect recurring overheating and treat it as a persistent design weakness.

Reported ondell.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Dell support refuses help on XPS 13 7390 unless extra support purchased

To check

Dell told the owner the BSODs were a software issue and would only help if they bought additional support, since software is not covered under the basic warranty.

What to checkConfirm what the warranty covers for software/firmware faults before relying on Dell support; basic plans may exclude software troubleshooting.

Reported ondell.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

XPS 13 7390 hangs and slow login after BIOS 1.21 update

To check

On the XPS 13 7390, BIOS update 1.21 caused app-opening hangs and ~2-minute logins; the owner could not roll back the BIOS.

What to checkBefore applying a BIOS update on the 7390, check community reports; a bad BIOS can be hard to roll back and may require reflashing.

Reported ondell.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

XPS 13 cooling fan loosens and scrapes against aluminium chassis

To check

A company running Dell XPS units reports the XPS 13's fan loosens and scrapes against the aluminium housing, a recurring hardware noise/vibration defect.

What to checkListen for fan scraping/rattle early; a loose fan rubbing the case can precede fan failure and needs reseating or replacement.

Reported ondell.com
