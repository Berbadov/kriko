Costly or dangerous1

Repeated charging failure, service cannot resolve it

Serious

An owner reports the A9 stops charging and was sent to authorized service 3–4 times, with the service returning it saying 'no problem' while the fault persists. A second complaint on the same site says the charging port was only blocked with dirt and cost 100 TL to fix, while an authorized service quoted ~5000 TL for a case swap.

What to checkCharging faults are common and service outcomes vary widely; before paying for a costly repair, have a second service check the port for debris. A tablet that repeatedly fails to charge is a real cost to discover.

Worth checking2

Slow performance, lag and poor multitasking on the A range

To check

Owners describe the A-range as slow and poor at multitasking, with battery draining quickly; Samsung's own reply acknowledges complaints about battery life and performance versus the S range. A Reddit owner thread on the A9 also calls the screen low-resolution and scrolling laggy.

What to checkFor a budget tablet the A9 is slow under multitasking and its battery life is a common complaint; buy it only if light use is acceptable.

Reported onsamsung.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Wi-Fi dropouts: tablet disconnects and reconnects 10–20s later

To check

A German owner reports the tablet briefly disconnects from any Wi-Fi network and reconnects 10–20 seconds later, annoying in games. Samsung's support reply suggests disabling 'Intelligent Wi-Fi' and resetting network settings, indicating a known software-side Wi-Fi complaint.

What to checkExpect possible Wi-Fi dropouts; a network-settings reset may help, but if it recurs it is a connectivity nuisance to live with.

Reported onsamsung.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

Chronic case/paint peeling and metal deformation

Minor

An owner reports paint dots peeling, metal deformation at the volume area and flaking at the charging-port rear case despite careful use, calling it a chronic case problem. The authorized service quoted ~5000 TL for a case swap and warned the screen might break.

What to checkThe A9's case finish can peel and deform; cosmetic, but a service may quote a costly case replacement, so get a second opinion.

Reported onsikayetvar.com
