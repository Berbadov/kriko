Refrigerant (freon) leak after three years on Beko RCNA406I30W

Serious

An owner of this exact model reports the unit broke after three years of use due to a freon (refrigerant) leak. A refrigerant leak is a sealed-system failure that typically cannot be repaired cheaply and usually means the fridge stops cooling. Evidence is from a Ukrainian buyer of the same model code; no Turkish-specific report was found.

What to checkA freon leak after ~3 years is a costly sealed-system failure (often not worth repairing). If buying, weigh the warranty length against this reported lifespan.

Worth checking1

Freezer ice/frost builds up under the bottom drawer on Beko RCNA406I30W

To check

An owner of this exact model reports that after about a year, ice/frost starts accumulating under the bottom freezer drawer; it does not stop the fridge working but requires periodic manual cleaning of the frost. This matches the pack's ice/frost family concern (frost blocking the drain area), reported on this specific model code. Evidence is from a Ukrainian buyer of the same model; no Turkish-specific report was found.

What to checkExpect to periodically defrost/clean under the bottom freezer drawer even though the unit is No Frost; budget for this maintenance rather than assuming a fully self-defrosting freezer.

Minor1

Fresh-food compartment runs uniformly cold at 3°C with no freshness zone on Beko RCNA406I30W

Minor

An owner of this exact model reports the temperature reads 3°C on every shelf and in both drawers, so there is no separate freshness (zero) zone — the compartment runs uniformly cold rather than having the advertised temperature zones. This is a low-severity behavior/expectation mismatch rather than a food-spoiling failure, but it is documented on this specific model code.

What to checkIf you expect a separate zero/freshness zone, this model reportedly runs everything at ~3°C; verify actual shelf temperatures rather than relying on the zone marketing.
