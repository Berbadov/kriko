Basket closure detection sensor fails on HD9280/70, unit stops detecting basket seating

Serious

Owner report on Trustpilot for the exact subject model HD9280/70: after purchase, the device stopped detecting that the basket is closed, and parts broke. The owner describes a frustrating after-sales experience being bounced between Philips and the retailer. This is a safety-relevant failure because the unit will not operate if it cannot confirm the basket is seated.

What to checkCheck the basket closure mechanism and sensor early in ownership; if it fails, pursue warranty immediately as replacement parts may not be readily available.

Reported onch.trustpilot.com

Contact pin that detects basket seating broke on HD9280/90, preventing operation

Serious

An owner of the HD9280/90 describes the contact pin that signals the basket is correctly seated breaking off; the unit beeps but does nothing. The broken piece had to be fished out. This matches the Trustpilot report of basket-closure detection failure on the /70 — a recurring mechanical weakness in this series.

What to checkHandle the basket insertion gently; if the unit beeps but won't start, inspect the contact pin area for breakage.

Reported onjustanswer.de

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

HD9280/90 stopped turning on after 15 minutes of use; owner had to return it

Serious

A verified purchaser on Alza.at reports the HD9280/90 (the same 5000 Series XL Connected family as the /70) failed completely after 15 minutes of frying: it would not switch on again. The owner had to claim a return and did not buy a replacement. This is a total unit failure shortly into use.

What to checkKeep the receipt and be ready to exercise warranty rights; a sudden total power failure can occur without warning.

Reported onm.alza.at

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking4

HD9280/90 produces limp, nearly black fries and requires much longer cooking times

To check

A detailed Dutch owner review of the HD9280/90 reports significantly worse cooking results than the older Philips Airfryer models: much longer baking times, limp and nearly black fries. The same reviewer also flags safety concerns with the hot pan-and-basket design (no drawer) and a cheap, weak feel. A Google Shopping review of the /70 independently confirms the crispiness problem: 'het word gewoonweg niet knapperig, ja al ik de aangegeven baktijd op de zak minimaal verdubbel.'

What to checkIf crispy results are your priority, be prepared to experiment with longer times; this series underperforms compared to older Philips models.

Reported onbol.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Removable basket rusts and coating peels after ~3 years on HD9280/91

To check

Multiple owners of the HD9280/91 report the removable basket rusting and the coating peeling after about three years. Crucially, the manufacturer does not sell the basket as a spare part, so the only remedy is replacing the whole fryer. This is a long-term durability issue with no affordable repair path.

What to checkIf you plan to keep the unit long-term, be aware the basket is not replaceable; treat it gently and hand-wash to extend its life.

Reported ongalaxus.nl

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Wi-Fi module limited to the 2.4GHz band — connection failures on 5GHz-only routers

To check

Philips confirms the HD9280 carries a 2.4GHz-only Wi-Fi module, so on a 5GHz-only home router the 'Connected' feature fails to pair until the router is switched to dual-band. For a product whose headline feature is app control, this is a real, documented limitation.

What to checkBefore buying, confirm your router broadcasts a 2.4GHz band; if it is 5GHz-only, the app/Wi-Fi features will not work without changing router settings.

Reported onphilips.co.kr

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Wi-Fi module on HD9280/70 only supports 2.4GHz band, causing connection failures

To check

Philips' own support page confirms the HD9280 is a Wi-Fi-capable model but the module is 2.4GHz-only. A user review on Google Shopping for the HD9280/70 confirms the pain: 'De connectie met Philips setup kan "niet gemaakt" worden. Mijn toestel is een PHILIPS, HD9280/70..' and the user had to disable their 5GHz signal to get it to connect. Home routers set to 5GHz-only will not work with this unit.

What to checkBefore buying, confirm your home router broadcasts a 2.4GHz network; if it is 5GHz-only, the app-connect features will not work.

Reported onphilips.pt

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27
