Earbuds stopped connecting after a month of inactivity

To check

An owner reports the earbuds stopped connecting to the phone after a month of inactivity, and that restarting or reinstalling the app did not help.

What to checkBuds left idle can fail to re-pair; if this happens, the official fix is the 10-second factory reset before considering service.

Reported oncomfy.ua

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

One earbud has no sound when paired — acknowledged by Xiaomi

To check

Xiaomi's own FAQ confirms one-side sound loss as a recognized issue, listing poor contact, low battery, dirt, moisture short-circuit and Bluetooth disconnection as causes.

What to checkOne-side sound loss is acknowledged by the maker; the listed fixes are cleaning, re-pairing and a 10-second factory reset, otherwise service center.

Reported onmi.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Right earbud began draining faster than the left after 8 months

To check

An owner reports the right earbud started draining faster after 8 months; the retailer replaced the unit under warranty within 2 days.

What to checkBattery imbalance is a known failure mode; keep proof of purchase since a warranty replacement is the realistic fix.

Reported oncomfy.ua
