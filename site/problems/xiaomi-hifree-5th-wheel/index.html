EU Safety Gate recall SR/02322/26: Xiaomi e-bike battery charger electric-shock risk (36V/42V)

Serious

An EU Safety Gate alert (SR/02322/26, notified by Denmark, product from China) recalls a power charger for Xiaomi e-scooter/e-bike batteries over inadequate creepage distances causing electric-shock risk. Caveat: the recalled charger is rated 36V/42V, while the Hifree 5th Wheel battery is 48V, so this specific alert likely does not cover the 5th Wheel charger — I could not confirm a 5th Wheel-specific charger recall before the budget ran out.

What to checkThere is an active EU recall on a Xiaomi e-bike charger for shock risk, but at 36V/42V it does not match the 48V Hifree 5th Wheel. I could not confirm a charger recall specific to this bike.

Reported onrecalleurope.com

Hifree E-Cycle front wheel came out of the fork during use

Serious

A Hifree E-Cycle (a sibling model in the same Xiaomi Hifree line) lost its front wheel from the fork during use about a week after purchase, which the owner describes as a serious safety risk. Note this is the E-Cycle model, not the 5th Wheel, so it is adjacent rather than identical.

What to checkIf you buy any Hifree folding bike, check the front-wheel retention before every ride; a wheel detaching at speed is a serious hazard. This specific report is on the E-Cycle model.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

EU Safety Gate recall SR/02322/26 covers a 36V/42V Xiaomi scooter charger, not the 5th Wheel's 48V battery

Minor

From the EU Safety Gate recall SR/02322/26 (notified by Denmark, 21 Aug 2026) via recalleurope. This charger recall is for a Xiaomi Electric Scooter E-Bike charger at 36V/42V — it does NOT match the 5th Wheel's 48V battery, so it is not a recall for this product.

What to checkA buyer should not assume this EU recall applies to the 5th Wheel; the recalled charger is a 36V/42V scooter charger, while the 5th Wheel uses a 48V battery. No matching recall for the 5th Wheel's own charger was found.

Reported onrecalleurope.com
