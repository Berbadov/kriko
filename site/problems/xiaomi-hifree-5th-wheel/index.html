Costly or dangerous

EU Safety Gate recall SR/02322/26: Xiaomi e-bike battery charger electric-shock risk (36V/42V)

An EU Safety Gate alert (SR/02322/26, notified by Denmark, product from China) recalls a power charger for Xiaomi e-scooter/e-bike batteries over inadequate creepage distances causing electric-shock risk. Caveat: the recalled charger is rated 36V/42V, while the Hifree 5th Wheel battery is 48V, so this specific alert likely does not cover the 5th Wheel charger — I could not confirm a 5th Wheel-specific charger recall before the budget ran out.

What to check. There is an active EU recall on a Xiaomi e-bike charger for shock risk, but at 36V/42V it does not match the 48V Hifree 5th Wheel. I could not confirm a charger recall specific to this bike.

Reported on: recalleurope.com

Hifree E-Cycle front wheel came out of the fork during use

A Hifree E-Cycle (a sibling model in the same Xiaomi Hifree line) lost its front wheel from the fork during use about a week after purchase, which the owner describes as a serious safety risk. Note this is the E-Cycle model, not the 5th Wheel, so it is adjacent rather than identical.

What to check. If you buy any Hifree folding bike, check the front-wheel retention before every ride; a wheel detaching at speed is a serious hazard. This specific report is on the E-Cycle model.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Minor

EU Safety Gate recall SR/02322/26 covers a 36V/42V Xiaomi scooter charger, not the 5th Wheel's 48V battery

From the EU Safety Gate recall SR/02322/26 (notified by Denmark, 21 Aug 2026) via recalleurope. This charger recall is for a Xiaomi Electric Scooter E-Bike charger at 36V/42V — it does NOT match the 5th Wheel's 48V battery, so it is not a recall for this product.

What to check. A buyer should not assume this EU recall applies to the 5th Wheel; the recalled charger is a 36V/42V scooter charger, while the 5th Wheel uses a 48V battery. No matching recall for the 5th Wheel's own charger was found.

Reported on: recalleurope.com

HiFree 5TH Whell Power Trail carries a 48V 10.4Ah removable lithium-ion battery

From the manufacturer/distributor Mieko's own product page. This anchors the concrete battery part for the 5TH Whell Power Trail: a 48V 10.4Ah removable lithium-ion battery, 4-5 hour charge time.

What to check. This identifies the specific battery spec a buyer should check when buying a replacement or verifying a charger; the 48V rating matters because the EU charger recall below does not apply to it.

Reported on: mieko.com.tr

Hifree 5th Wheel uses a removable 48V 10.4Ah lithium-ion battery

The Turkish distributor Mieko's own product page identifies the battery as a removable 48V 10.4Ah lithium-ion pack (product code EB006). This anchors the battery component for the 5th Wheel line.

What to check. This is the concrete battery spec to check against any recall or replacement claim; the owner complaint above shows service struggled to supply a matching pack.

Reported on: mieko.com.tr
