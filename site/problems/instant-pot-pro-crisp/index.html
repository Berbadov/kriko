Instant Pot Pro Crisp: known problems

3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Pro Crisp AF 8 pressure cooking lid can be opened while pressurized, ejecting scalding contents (lawsuit)

A product-liability lawsuit (Ulloa v. Instant Brands, Eastern District of Tennessee, filed July 21) names the Pro Crisp AF 8 specifically and alleges the lid could be rotated and opened while the unit remained pressurized, so the scalding contents were ejected onto the user, causing severe burn injuries.

What to check. Treat the lid lock as the critical safety part. If the lid can be turned or opened before pressure has fully released, stop using the unit immediately and do not attempt to open it under pressure; this is the failure mode the lawsuit says causes catastrophic burns.

Reported on: aboutlawsuits.com

Worth checking

Owner reports defective pressure sensor — timer runs before pressure is generated and lid can be opened

An owner on the Galaxus Q&A reports that on the Pro Crisp the timer starts before pressure is generated and the lid can be opened, which they attribute to a defective sensor; they note two other identical units did not behave this way.

What to check. If the timer starts before the unit reaches pressure and the lid opens early, the pressure sensor/lock may be faulty — a safety-relevant defect. This is an individual owner report, not a recall; verify against the unit's behavior and contact support if it occurs.

Reported on: galaxus.ch

Steam escaping from the side of the lid — manufacturer instructs checking the sealing ring

The manufacturer's own EU safety manual (German) documents that steam escaping from the side of the lid is a known condition and instructs the user to turn the appliance off and verify the sealing ring is seated correctly.

What to check. Steam leaking from the side of the lid means the seal has failed (ring missing, worn, or mis-seated). Check and replace the silicone sealing ring before pressurizing; the manual treats side steam as a condition to stop cooking for.

Reported on: instantpot.de
