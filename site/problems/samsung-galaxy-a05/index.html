Galaxy A05 after One UI 7 update: touchscreen intermittently dead and unexpected restarts

Serious

The owner reports that after the One UI 7 update in late July 2025, the touchscreen intermittently stops working when unlocking and the phone restarts unexpectedly, including at night while charging.

What to checkA One UI 7 update can introduce touchscreen and random-restart bugs on the A05; check for a newer software version before assuming hardware failure.

Reported onsamsung.com

Galaxy A05 touchscreen/LCD stops responding — multiple owners report the same

Serious

A buyer review states the LCD is bad and the screen cannot be touched, and that all users complain about the same thing, asking Samsung to pull the phone from the market.

What to checkIf you buy an A05, test the touchscreen thoroughly within the return window; this is the most repeated failure across sources.

Reported onsamsung.com

Galaxy A05 battery drains fast, shuts down at 10-30%, and heats while charging

To check

It lists as classic signs of a worn battery: faster discharge than before, sudden shutdowns at 10-30%, heating while charging, and a jumping percentage indicator.

What to checkIf the A05 starts shutting down at 10-30% or heating on charge, the battery is the likely cause; a replacement is the standard fix.

Reported onsamsung-centrum.pl

Galaxy A05 battery drains very fast and case is slightly bent — Turkish owner complaint

To check

A buyer of a Galaxy A05 in Turkey reports the battery drains very fast since purchase and the case has a slight bend, with no impact or user error, and that Samsung asked for about 3,500 TL for service despite the warranty.

What to checkIn Turkey, fast battery drain is reported on the A05; if under warranty, push for free service rather than paying the quoted fee.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Galaxy A05 green line appears on screen after about a month, no damage

To check

An owner reports a green line down the screen appearing randomly after a little over a month, with no damage; a Samsung helper replies a green line usually suggests a physical fault needing a screen or cable replacement.

What to checkA green line on a month-old unit is a hardware fault, not software; expect a screen replacement rather than a fix.

Reported oneu.community.samsung.com

Galaxy A05 overheats and suddenly drains the battery under multitasking

To check

The owner reports the phone overheats and suddenly drains the battery when running multiple tabs, and crashes.

What to checkHeavy multitasking on the A05 can trigger overheating and sudden battery drain; expect this in demanding use.

Reported onmakro.co.za

Galaxy A05 screen freezes repeatedly, needs power-button reset

To check

The owner reports the screen began freezing often after about five months, requiring a power-button reset each time, and that the problem persisted after clearing storage and resetting internet settings.

What to checkFrequent screen freezing that needs a hard reset may not be fixed by clearing storage; it can require service-center attention.

Reported onsamsung.com
