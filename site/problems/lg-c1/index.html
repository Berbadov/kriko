After a system update LG OLED48C1 developed horizontal lines and repeated shutdowns

Serious

Reclame Aqui consumer complaint: an OLED48C1 that worked until November 2025 developed horizontal black lines across the image and sudden continuous shutdowns after a system update; the authorized LG repair quote (R$ 5,790) exceeded the TV's original price (R$ 4,107).

What to checkA C1 that develops horizontal lines and repeated shutdowns after an update can be expensive to repair — one owner's authorized repair quote exceeded the TV's purchase price.

Reported onreclameaqui.com.br

LG C1 burn-in after long static image; Pixel Cleaning did not remove it

Serious

Hardware.com.br article: a 55" LG C1 owner left a Fire TV static home screen on overnight and found a large visible mark; Pixel Cleaning did not remove it. The article cites RTINGS' accelerated long-duration test in which a C1 showed permanent retention in static-element regions after thousands of hours of repetitive content.

What to checkBurn-in on the C1's OLED panel is cumulative and permanent; a single static image left on can reveal it, and the built-in Pixel Cleaning may not remove it. Static content should be avoided for long periods.

Reported onhardware.com.br

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

LG C1 random restarts under gaming load — LG cites possible mainboard/power-supply overheating

Serious

German owner forum (EU market): the C1 restarts by itself under high load during gaming on both PS5 and Xbox Series X; LG's own reply to the owner suggested a possible overheating problem on the mainboard or power supply. A forum member notes the LG exchange program for power-supply faults covers only 2016-2019 models, so the C1 is not covered by it.

What to checkIf a C1 restarts only under gaming load, suspect the mainboard or power supply rather than the cable or firmware; this is a hardware fault not covered by LG's older power-supply exchange program.

Reported oncomputerbase.de

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Firmware 03.41.05 broke Wi-Fi streaming apps, power control and Bluetooth on C1

To check

Russian webOS forum aggregating Reddit reports on firmware 03.41.05: after the update the C1's Wi-Fi connects but all streaming apps stop working, power on/off becomes unreliable, and Bluetooth pairing fails; users advise not installing the update.

What to checkFirmware 03.41.05 is reported to break Wi-Fi streaming apps, power control and Bluetooth on the C1; owners advise against installing it.

Reported onwebos-forums.ru

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Firmware 03.53.45 update caused black-screen startup loop on LG C1 65"

To check

Spanish forum (EU market), LG C1 65" 2021 on firmware 03.53.45: about 28 seconds after power-on the TV shows an update screen and then goes fully black, requiring a power-off/on; the owner solved it with a factory reset, which also added new settings options.

What to checkOn firmware 03.53.45 a C1 can fall into a black-screen update loop at startup; a factory reset (advanced reset) is reported to resolve it, at the cost of reconfiguring the TV.

Reported onforocoches.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

LG C1 power cycles when a bright game flash appears on screen

To check

TechRadar/Yahoo news report: a bright muzzle flash in Resident Evil Requiem caused an LG C1 to power-cycle (turn off and back on); the report notes the cause is unclear, either a power unit failure or a panel problem, and that no obvious fix exists yet.

What to checkA C1 that shuts off and restarts on bright on-screen flashes points to a power-supply or panel fault; owners report no reliable fix, only workarounds like dimming or changing HDMI cable.

Reported ontech.yahoo.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

OLED65C1 screen goes black with an update logo seconds after power-on

To check

French Darty SAV community (EU market), OLED65C1: seconds after power-on the screen goes black and shows an update logo; a second certified owner confirms the same problem and says a factory reset did not fix it ("J'ai réinitialisé la tv").

What to checkA C1 that shows an update logo and black screen shortly after power-on may not be cured by a reset; owners report it recurring after every power cycle.

Reported onsav.darty.com
