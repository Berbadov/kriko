C10 error code: WiFi module failure on Instant Pot Plus

To check

Official Instant Pot troubleshooting table (Spanish market) lists C10 as a WiFi module failure requiring customer service contact. This is model-specific to the Plus WiFi, which is the only Instant Pot variant with built-in WiFi.

What to checkIf the unit displays C10, the WiFi module has failed and requires service; the unit may still cook but remote/app features are lost.

Reported oninstantpot.es

Minor2

No recall found for Instant Pot Plus WiFi; only Smart/Smart-60 (2015) recall identified

Minor

The only Instant Pot recall identified covers Smart/Smart-60 models (2015, electrical shock hazard). No recall was found for the Plus WiFi (RPPC61-BK). Later burn lawsuits concern lid-safety/pressure-retention failures but are not specific to the Plus WiFi model.

What to checkThe Plus WiFi is not under any identified recall, but buyers should be aware of the broader pattern of lid-safety burn lawsuits against Instant Pot brand cookers.

Reported onschmidtandclark.com

WiFi function not working on Instant Pot Plus WiFi (customer report)

Minor

An Amazon.it customer review of the Instant Pot Plus WiFi reports the WiFi function does not work, alongside missing accessories and used-looking packaging. This is a single-owner report, not an official defect notice.

What to checkBuyers should verify WiFi connectivity immediately upon receipt, as a non-functional WiFi module may require return or service.

Reported onamazon.it
