Dishes come out dirty on the 3.5-hour program despite normal settings and different detergents

Serious

A 1.5-year-old unit stopped cleaning dishes on the long program despite normal salt/rinseaid and several detergent brands; Samsung called it user error and refused warranty help.

What to checkPoor wash performance is a recurring reported failure; if it appears, test with multiple detergents before assuming user error, and be prepared that Samsung may reject the claim.

Machine leaked 2-3 litres of clean water from underneath while switched off

Serious

A unit leaked roughly 2-3 litres of clean water from underneath even while switched off; the owner was quoted a 2200 TL service fee due to technician distance.

What to checkA leak while off points to a water-path or seal fault, not user loading; this can be costly to diagnose if the nearest authorized service is far.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Rinse failure leaves detergent residue and film on glassware from the second wash

Serious

A new unit left detergent residue and film on glassware from the second wash on every program; Samsung service refused to accept it as a fault.

What to checkIf you buy this model, verify rinse performance early and keep records; the owner reports Samsung service disputed the defect and offered only a paid follow-up visit.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking2

Abnormal noise and an obvious manufacturing defect within the first week

To check

A brand-new unit showed abnormal running noise and an obvious interior manufacturing defect within a week; Samsung offered a paid replacement instead of a free one.

What to checkInspect a new unit immediately and report noise/defects within the warranty window; the owner reports Samsung proposed a paid exchange for a factory-defective machine.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Internal rust appeared inside the tub while under warranty

To check

The same owner reported rust forming inside the tub; the authorized service only surface-cleaned it and Samsung rejected the warranty claim as user error.

What to checkInterior rust on a machine still under warranty is a defect worth documenting with photos, as the owner reports Samsung disputed it.
