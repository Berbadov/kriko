China SAMR recall of 2.98M Teslas including Model Y over emergency door-release handles

The Chinese regulator (SAMR) recalled 2.98M Teslas, including China-built Model Y, because the emergency manual door release is hard to find and operate, which can trap occupants in a crash or fire.

What to check. A Model Y owner in TR/EU should know the manual release locations (front lever, rear cable under the door-pocket mat on 2020-2024 cars) and confirm their specific car has the rear release fitted, since the recall fix is a label plus an OTA window-lowering update.

Reported on: thepeterboroughexaminer.com

Fatal 2024 Anseong Model Y fire: witnesses could not open the doors

Safety risk

In a fatal October 2024 Model Y fire in Anseong, witnesses could not open the doors; Tesla's manual says the front release is a lever and the rear release is a cable under the door-pocket mat, and that not all Model Ys have the rear manual release.

What to check. This is the concrete failure behind the recall: the electronic door release can be unusable after a crash, and the manual backup is hard to find, especially for rear passengers; an owner should locate and practice both releases on their own car.

Reported on: motormag.co.kr

Turkey: Model Y crashed into a truck and caught fire, firefighters took ~3 hours to extinguish

Safety risk

A documented Model Y battery fire in Turkey (Aydın/Efeler) after a collision with a truck: the lithium pack entered thermal runaway, required about three hours of cooling to extinguish, and carried a re-ignition risk.

What to check. This is a crash-induced battery fire, not a spontaneous one; the practical cost for an owner is that a Model Y fire is very hard to put out (tens of thousands of gallons of water) and can reignite, so parking/charging near structures carries risk.

Reported on: d1ev.com

Worth checking

NHTSA investigating 2021 Model Y external door-opening failure tied to 12V battery voltage drop

NHTSA is investigating 2021 Model Y external door-opening failure: 16 complaints of the exterior handle not working due to low 12V battery voltage, some cases requiring breaking a window to enter.

What to check. This is an open investigation, not a final defect finding; an owner should keep the 12V battery healthy because a weak low-voltage battery can leave the exterior door handle inoperative.

Reported on: motormag.co.kr
