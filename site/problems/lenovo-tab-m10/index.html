Costly or dangerous

Tab M10 3rd gen freezes, apps won't open, random shutdowns and won't charge (Lenovo official forum, TR)

A Turkish owner of the M10 3rd gen reports severe freezing, apps not opening, random shutdowns, refusal to charge, and the system UI repeatedly stopping. This is the strongest single claim: it is on Lenovo's own official forum, from a Turkey-located user, and matches the pack's 'severe lag/freezing' and 'mainboard failures' concerns. A buyer should expect a 3rd-gen unit to need frequent restarts and possibly warranty service.

Reported on: forums.lenovo.com

Worth checking

Tab M10 (TB311FU) lags and freezes, owner blames insufficient RAM

A Hepsiburada (Turkish retailer) buyer of the M10 4/64GB (model TB311FU) reports lag and freezing and attributes it to insufficient RAM. Another review on the same page notes 'kasma oluyor bir süre sonra' (lag develops after a while). This is a recurring performance complaint on the Turkish market units.

Reported on: hepsiburada.com

Tab M10 3rd gen battery drains fast after about one year of use

An owner of the M10 3rd gen (ZAAE0027UA) lists fast discharge as the main drawback, especially after roughly one year of use. This matches the pack's 'short battery life' concern and tells a buyer the battery degrades noticeably within the first year.

Reported on: comfy.ua

Tab M10 3rd gen started glitching and kicking user out of apps within months

An owner of the M10 3rd gen reports the tablet began glitching and kicking her out of apps within months of purchase, and later the camera failed inside apps until a reboot. This is a software-stability complaint that recurs across the M10 3rd-gen reports.

Reported on: comfy.ua

Tab M10 Military Edition power button stopped responding after 8 months

An owner of the M10 Military Edition (ZAAH0046UA) reports the power button stopped waking the device after sleep at 8 months, plus random reboots and content stutter. This is a concrete physical-button failure matching the pack's 'faulty physical buttons' concern.

Reported on: comfy.ua

Minor

Tab M10 1st gen display not responding to touch (iFixit repair thread)

An iFixit repair thread on the M10 1st gen describes a display that is not responding to touch. The suggested fixes are a forced restart and charging, pointing to a software/battery-related display fault rather than a guaranteed hardware defect. Lower severity because it is a troubleshooting thread, not a confirmed failure rate.

Reported on: de.ifixit.com

Tab M10 3rd gen camera stopped working after 5 months

A short owner note on the M10 3rd gen says the camera stopped working after 5 months and the device lags. Lower severity because it is a single brief report and cameras are secondary on this class of tablet.

Reported on: comfy.ua
