POCO X6 5G green lines appeared on screen after ~2 months

Serious

A Yandex Market owner reports the screen went dark and showed green lines after ~2 months, though it did not recur.

What to checkA display defect (green lines / screen going dark) is the highest-cost failure here; buyers should keep the unit within warranty in case it recurs, since a panel replacement is expensive.

Reported onmarket.yandex.ru

Worth checking3

POCO X6 5G battery drains fast within ~6 months of use (TR market)

To check

A Turkish owner reports the battery and fast-charge life dropped noticeably within about 6 months of ownership.

What to checkA TR buyer should treat battery longevity as a known weak point and check whether the unit is still under warranty before the ~6-month mark if drain becomes severe.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

POCO X6 5G battery drains very fast in some situations (EU market)

To check

Polish owners report the battery drains very quickly in some situations, while charging itself works fine.

What to checkEU buyers should expect variable battery life; if drain is severe, verify warranty coverage early since the battery is a common complaint.

Reported onmgsm.pl

POCO X6 5G heats up strongly near the cameras under demanding apps (EU market)

To check

Same Polish EU-market source as above: the phone's body heats up noticeably near the cameras when running demanding software.

What to checkEU buyers who game or run heavy apps should expect noticeable heat near the camera module; this is a comfort concern more than a failure.

Reported onmgsm.pl

Minor1

Xiaomi: POCO X6 red/green tint at viewing angles is normal OLED behavior

Minor

Xiaomi's official FAQ states that a slight red/green tint when viewing the OLED at an angle is normal, not a defect.

What to checkNot every color shift is a defect; a mild tint at an angle is expected OLED behavior, while persistent green lines or a full green screen (as in other reports) are not.

Reported onmi.com
