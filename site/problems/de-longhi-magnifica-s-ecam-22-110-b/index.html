Bean grinder is a repeated failure point on the Magnifica S

Serious

The owner reports the grinder caused the machine to break down four times, and that after-sales service was hard to reach.

What to checkThe grinder is the most-reported hard failure on this machine. If you buy one, expect that a grinder failure may require repair or replacement, and check local service availability before purchase.

Worth checking5

Brew unit can jam against the grounds container (design change made)

To check

The owner found the grounds container stuck and impossible to remove, and a forum repairer notes DeLonghi changed the brew unit and grounds container design for this issue.

What to checkThe brew unit can hang on the rear edge of the grounds container and jam it, blocking removal. This is a known design weakness on this model, and DeLonghi revised the parts.

Reported onkaffee-welt.net

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Brew-unit second press stops, producing weak coffee and broken pucks

To check

After about 10 weeks the machine stopped doing the second press of the coffee puck, giving weak, less foamy coffee and pucks that broke apart when dropped.

What to checkThe 'Nachpressung' (second tamp) failing is a symptom of the brew unit needing maintenance or lubrication. If your shots turn weak and pucks crumble, the brew group is the part to inspect.

Reported onamazon.de

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Descale light triggers very early; no sensor, just a litre counter

To check

The descale light came on twice in three weeks; the machine has no hardness sensor and just counts litres, with the factory hardness set to the hardest level 4.

What to checkExpect frequent descale prompts. Setting the water-hardness level to match your local water can reduce how often it asks, but the counter-based logic means it will still prompt regularly.

Reported onwebsite-g7g.amazon.co.uk

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Plastic chassis screw-support breaks, stopping puck ejection

To check

The plastic chassis piece that anchors the ejection lever's pivot broke, so used coffee pucks stopped ejecting into the tray.

What to checkThe replacement part is cheap (~12€) but fixing it requires removing the whole body and disassembling the machine. A misaligned brew-unit piston was the root cause, so this can recur.

Reported onblog.demees.net

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Water reservoir leaks when inserted

To check

One owner reports the water tank leaks as soon as it is fitted, and got no response from DeLonghi customer service.

What to checkA leaking reservoir is a reported fault on this model, and the same review notes poor after-sales response. Check the tank seal early if you notice water around the base.

Reported onfnac.com
