Galaxy A15 fast charging drops to normal charging and charging takes hours (TR market)

Serious

The Şikayetvar complaint page lists ~30 charging complaints for the A15, several describing fast charging dropping to normal charging within a minute and full charges taking 2-7+ hours, some within warranty.

What to checkIf buying new, this is the most-reported TR-market fault; test fast charging within the return window. If owned, a slow-charge unit is a warranty claim rather than a DIY fix.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Galaxy A15 touchscreen becomes unresponsive, blocking calls and photos

Serious

An owner review (collected on Google Shopping, reviewer 'Bradgh') reports the touchscreen suddenly becoming unresponsive on multiple occasions, making calls and photos impossible, and requiring a full restart. A separate Turkish complaint on Şikayetvar ('dokunmatik') reports the same touchscreen trouble.

What to checkUnresponsive touchscreen is a serious usability fault; a buyer should test the touchscreen thoroughly in the return window, and an owner should treat it as a hardware/warranty claim.

Reported ongoogle.co.in

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking4

Galaxy A15 overheats after ~20 minutes and camera shuts down from overheating

To check

An owner review on Samsung's official product page (German review) reports the device getting hot after ~20 minutes and the camera switching off due to overheating; Samsung's own reply acknowledges abnormal warmth and recommends an authorized service check.

What to checkOverheating that disables the camera is a hardware/thermal fault worth a service-centre check; a buyer should verify sustained camera use in the return window.

Reported onsamsung.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Galaxy A15 screen intermittently appears and disappears (flickering)

To check

An owner review on Samsung's official page reports the screen frequently appearing and disappearing intermittently, a display fault consistent with the flickering/panel issues repair guides describe for the A15.

What to checkIntermittent screen dropouts point to the AMOLED panel or its connection; a buyer should check the display during the return window and treat a flickering unit as a warranty/return case.

Reported onsamsung.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Green line on screen reported after an update (5G variant, same A15 family)

To check

A Samsung Community thread reports a green line appearing after a December update — but explicitly on the Galaxy A15 5G (SM-A156E), not the 4G model in this subject. I could not confirm this specific fault on the 4G unit, so treat it as a same-family signal rather than a confirmed 4G defect.

What to checkThe green-line fault is documented on the A15 5G sibling; for the 4G model this remains unconfirmed. A buyer should check the display for vertical lines during the return window regardless.

Reported onr2.community.samsung.com:443

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

One UI 7 update makes Galaxy A15 overheat and drain battery faster

To check

An owner review (Spanish, reposted on Samsung's Finnish page) reports that after installing One UI 7 the phone overheats and the battery drains faster; the same reviewer also reports missing autofocus.

What to checkA software-update regression is the most likely cause here; a buyer should check whether the unit ships with or has received One UI 7 before judging battery/thermal behaviour.

Reported onsamsung.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

Galaxy A15 wired-headphone audio stutters, unfixed for over a year

Minor

An owner review on Samsung's official page reports audio stutter with wired TRS headphones that persisted for over a year across updates; Samsung's reply blames dust/headphone compatibility.

What to checkLower-severity issue: if the buyer relies on wired headphones, test the jack early; otherwise it is a nuisance rather than a dealbreaker.

Reported onsamsung.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27
