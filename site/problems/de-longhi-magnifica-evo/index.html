De'Longhi Magnifica Evo: known problems

3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Made watery coffee then stopped working within a month (ECAM290.81)

The buyer states the machine started making watery coffee and stopped working within a month of purchase, and the repair was not completed after two months.

What to check. A formal consumer complaint records a brew-unit failure shortly after purchase; check warranty/service response time before relying on after-sales support.

Reported on: altroconsumo.it

Worth checking

Milk frothing stops after months despite cleaning (ECAM290.61.B)

Owner reports the machine stopped frothing milk after ~3 months; cleaning the system and disassembling the nozzle gave zero result, and a replacement carafe failed the same way within a month.

What to check. The milk carafe is repeatedly named as the weak point; expect frothing to degrade and plan for replacement or manual frothing.

Reported on: comfy.ua

Minor

Grounds container reports full when nearly empty

Owner reports the grounds-container-full warning triggers with only three coffees' worth of grounds; the same review notes the machine runs out of water or beans mid-brew.

What to check. The grounds sensor can false-trigger; empty the tray when prompted rather than trusting the count.

Reported on: m.alza.cz
