Costly or dangerous

WF-1000XM5 left earbud failed within a month with static/buzzing/popping

A verified owner reports the left earbud failed within a month of purchase, with very low volume and static/buzzing/popping.

What to check. A buyer should test both earbuds immediately on arrival and keep proof of purchase, because one owner's unit failed within the first month with static/buzzing/popping and very low volume. This is a unit-level failure, so check the warranty/return window early.

Reported on: sony.com.ar

Worth checking

Sony acknowledges white noise/static under Active Noise Canceling on WF-1000XM5

Sony's own documentation confirms that a continuous static/white noise is heard whenever the Noise Canceling function is on, and the only remedy it gives is to turn ANC off.

What to check. Expect a faint hiss/static whenever ANC is enabled; Sony documents this as inherent to the ANC implementation rather than a per-unit defect. If that hiss bothers you, the only remedy Sony offers is to disable ANC, which defeats the main selling point.

Reported on: sony.com.au

WF-1000XM5 charging case intermittent charging failure reported

The same long-term owner reports an intermittent charging problem that a service-centre claim did not resolve.

What to check. One owner reports intermittent charging problems where the service-centre claim came back unresolved and dirty. Verify the charging case actually tops up the earbuds before relying on it, since a failed case leaves the buds unusable.

Reported on: m.alza.cz

WF-1000XM5 right earbud stopped working completely after ~2.5 years

A long-term owner reports the right earbud stopped working completely after about 2.5 years of use.

What to check. A long-term owner reports one earbud dying completely after ~2.5 years, which is past the typical warranty period, so the replacement cost falls on the buyer. Treat the earbuds as a consumable with a limited lifespan rather than a permanent purchase.

Reported on: m.alza.cz
