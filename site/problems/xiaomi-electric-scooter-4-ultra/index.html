Xiaomi Electric Scooter 4 Ultra: known problems

5 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

At a glance

  • Handlebar folding joint wears out after ~200 km and can fold while riding (owner report, Hungary)
  • Throttle fault code 14 means the throttle must be replaced (Xiaomi official FAQ)
  • Real-world range far below the advertised 70 km, degrading after a month
  • Scooter auto powers off on slopes / overload due to motor overheating (official FAQ)
  • Steering column develops play / wobble (owner forum, Russia)

Costly or dangerous

Handlebar folding joint wears out after ~200 km and can fold while riding (owner report, Hungary)

After only ~200 km the handlebar folding joint wore out so badly the scooter could fold while riding, causing personal injury and ~80,000 HUF in parts damage. This matches the pack's known handlebar-play issue.

What to check. Check the folding joint for play early; a worn joint can fold mid-ride and cause a crash, and the replacement part is costly.

Reported on: m.alza.hu

Throttle fault code 14 means the throttle must be replaced (Xiaomi official FAQ)

The maker itself documents that fault code 14 (or one long + four short beeps) means the throttle has failed and must be replaced at a service center.

What to check. If the dashboard shows code 14, the throttle is faulty and needs a service-center replacement; do not keep riding on a failing throttle.

Reported on: mi.com

Worth checking

Real-world range far below the advertised 70 km, degrading after a month

A 80 kg rider got at most 35 km per charge (advertised 70 km) and only 25 km after about a month. This matches the pack's known overstated-range issue.

What to check. Expect roughly half the advertised range at typical adult weight, and budget for further degradation within the first month.

Reported on: m.alza.cz

Scooter auto powers off on slopes / overload due to motor overheating (official FAQ)

The scooter has a self-protection cut-off that shuts it down when the motor overheats on slopes or under overload. This is a documented behavior riders should know about.

What to check. On long or steep climbs the scooter can shut down mid-ride; avoid sustained full-power climbs and exceeding the payload.

Reported on: mi.com

Steering column develops play / wobble (owner forum, Russia)

An owner reports the steering column started to develop play/wobble and had to fabricate a new clamp because no replacement existed yet. This corroborates the handlebar-play issue from an independent owner source.

What to check. Watch for steering-column play; owners report having to improvise a fix because official replacement parts were unavailable early on.

Reported on: 4pda.to
