Anker PowerCore 10000 PD+ (A1236): known problems

4 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

CPSC June 2025 recall covers PowerCore 10000 model A1263 only, not A1236

The official CPSC notice (Recall 25-338, June 12 2025, ~1,158,000 units) names the recalled model as A1263 only.

What to check. The A1236 (PowerCore 10000 PD+) is a different model and is not covered by this recall. A buyer checking whether their A1236 is affected can confirm the official recall is limited to A1263.

Reported on: cpsc.gov

CPSC recall covers Anker PowerCore 10000 model A1263 only, not the A1236

The primary CPSC notice (Recall No. 25-338, June 12 2025) limits the recall to model A1263 only. The subject A1236 (PowerCore 10000 PD+) is a different model and is not covered by this recall.

What to check. Do not assume an A1236 unit is recalled because of the A1263 recall. Check the model number printed on the bottom of the unit; only A1263 units with qualifying serial numbers are affected.

Reported on: cpsc.gov

Worth checking

Secondary news aggregator lists A1236 among recalled PowerCore 10000 models, contradicting CPSC

This Spanish aggregator (citing Daily Mail) is the only source naming A1236 in a recall list, but it conflicts with the official CPSC and Anker notices which specify A1263 only.

What to check. Treat this as a likely model-number error in a secondary aggregator. Do not use it to conclude the A1236 is recalled; the official primary sources say A1263 only.

Reported on: rpp.pe

Minor

News report lists PowerCore 10000 A1236 among recalled Anker models, conflicting with CPSC

This secondary Spanish news article (aggregating Daily Mail) lists the A1236 among recalled Anker models. It conflicts with the primary CPSC notice, which states the recall covers model A1263 only, and the article's model list contains errors (e.g., A1642, not a recalled MagGo model). I could not open the page to verify it (fetch hit the tool budget), so this is reported as an unverified, conflicting claim.

What to check. Treat this as an unreliable secondary source. The authoritative CPSC notice does not include the A1236; verify any recall status against CPSC or Anker's official recall page before acting.

Reported on: rpp.pe
