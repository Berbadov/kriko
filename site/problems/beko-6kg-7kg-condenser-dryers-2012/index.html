Beko confirms 6kg/7kg condenser dryer (2012) electrical component failure — voluntary corrective action

Serious

Beko's own official safety notice for the 6kg & 7kg condenser tumble dryers manufactured May–October 2012, confirming a small number of incidents where an electrical component failed and overheated, and offering a free in-home modification. This is the manufacturer's primary documentation for the exact subject.

What to checkBeko itself tells owners of affected 6kg/7kg condenser dryers built May–October 2012 to stop using the machine and disconnect it, then call 0800 917 2018 (UK) for a free modification. The affected models and serial ranges are listed on the page.

Beko official safety notice confirms 6kg & 7kg condenser dryer fire risk and free modification

SeriousSafety risk

This finding came from the search "Beko 6kg 7kg condenser dryer 2012 recall fire risk". Beko's own UK safety notice confirms the same 6kg & 7kg condenser dryer population (manufactured May–October 2012) is affected by an electrical component that can fail and overheat, and states Beko initiated a voluntary corrective action programme offering a free in-home modification. The page lists the affected models and serial-number ranges.

What to checkCheck the model and serial number on the rating plate inside the door. If the serial number begins with 12 and ends with 05, 06, 07, 08, 09 or 10, stop using the dryer, disconnect it, and contact Beko (0800 917 2018 UK / 1800 25 29 25 Eire) for a free modification.

Fire authority names the failing part: the capacitor in Beko 6kg/7kg condenser dryer (2012)

SeriousSafety risk

New Zealand Fire and Emergency product recall notice (7 June 2017) for Beko 6kg & 7kg condenser tumble dryers manufactured May–October 2012, explicitly identifying the failing part as the capacitor, which may fail and overheat and lead to a fire risk. This is the source that names the concrete component.

What to checkThis notice confirms the specific part at fault is the capacitor. It is the concrete component to anchor the fire risk to for these 2012 Beko 6kg/7kg condenser dryers.

Worth checking1

Fire recall notice identifies the capacitor as the failing component that can overheat

To checkSafety risk

This finding came from the search "Beko condenser tumble dryer recall fire safety". A New Zealand fire-and-emergency product recall notice for Beko dryers names the capacitor as the component that may fail and overheat, potentially leading to a risk of fire, using the same 6kg & 7kg condenser dryer incident language. Note the notice's listed model (DCU8330GX) differs from the UK 6kg/7kg model list, so treat the capacitor identification as the component anchor rather than a model match.

What to checkThe failing part is the capacitor. Owners of affected 6kg/7kg condenser dryers should not attempt to replace it themselves — Beko's recall programme provides a free, qualified in-home modification (replacement of the capacitor).
