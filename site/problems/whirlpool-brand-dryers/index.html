Costly or dangerous

Electrical Safety First: Whirlpool recall of Hotpoint/Indesit/Swan/Creda/Proline dryers due to fire risk from fluff build-up

Safety risk

The UK electrical safety charity documents the formal recall programme for Whirlpool-owned brands (Hotpoint, Indesit, Swan, Creda, Proline) sold 2004–2015, with fire risk from fluff build-up, and gives the consumer remedy: unplug, free replacement/repair/refund.

What to check. Owners of affected machines should unplug them immediately and contact Whirlpool's recall line (0800 151 0905 in UK) for free replacement, repair, or refund.

Reported on: electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk

Lint build-up on heating element causes fire risk in Whirlpool-owned tumble dryers (2004–2015)

Safety risk

The UK Parliament BEIS Select Committee report documents the core defect: excess fluff contacting the heating element, with at least 750 fires since 2004 and 5.3 million affected machines in the UK alone. Whirlpool alerted UK and European authorities.

What to check. If the reader owns a Whirlpool, Hotpoint, Indesit, Creda, Proline or Swan tumble dryer made between April 2004 and September 2015, they should check the model against the affected list and stop using an unmodified machine — the documented failure is a fire risk.

Reported on: publications.parliament.uk

Produktwarnung (Germany): Whirlpool safety notice for Hotpoint and Indesit dryers — lint contact with heating element fire risk

Safety risk

The German product warning site documents Whirlpool's official safety notice for Hotpoint and Indesit dryers made April 2004–September 2015: residual lint can contact the heating element and create a fire risk. Initially Whirlpool said use under supervision was OK; later advice changed to unplug.

What to check. German owners of Hotpoint or Indesit dryers from 2004–2015 should check the model list and, per later UK guidance, unplug until modified.

Reported on: produktwarnung.eu

The Argus: Whirlpool recalls 800,000 tumble dryers after government intervention; 5.3 million units affected overall

News report confirming the scale: 800,000 dryers recalled after government intervention, with 5.3 million units manufactured April 2004–September 2015 across Hotpoint, Indesit, Creda, Swan and Proline brands. Green sticker indicates non-affected machines.

What to check. Check for a green sticker inside the door or on the back of the machine. No green sticker means check the model against the recall list.

Reported on: theargus.co.uk

UK Government ordered recall of 500,000 Whirlpool tumble dryers in 2019

In June 2019 the UK Government instructed Whirlpool to recall 500,000 tumble dryers, four years after the first warning, with up to 750 fires linked to the fault in an 11-year period.

What to check. Documents the escalation to a formal recall, confirming the severity and the scale of machines still unmodified.

Reported on: parliament.uk

Which?: campaigned for recall of 500,000+ fire-risk Whirlpool tumble dryers after tragic deaths

Safety risk

The UK consumer group Which? confirms it campaigned for a recall of more than 500,000 fire-risk Whirlpool tumble dryers, and links the deaths of Sohaib Ahmed and his daughter Manahal to ongoing tumble dryer fire concerns.

What to check. Consumer advocacy groups consider the fire risk serious enough to have demanded a full recall. Owners should take the recall seriously.

Reported on: which.co.uk

Worth checking

Digital Trends: Whirlpool-branded tumble dryers NOT affected by the safety notice — only Hotpoint/Indesit/Creda

This is a critical clarification: the major fire recall applies to dryers sold under Hotpoint, Indesit, and Creda brands (Whirlpool-owned), NOT to dryers sold under the Whirlpool brand itself. The pack's subject is 'Whirlpool-brand dryers', so this distinction matters.

What to check. If you are buying or own a dryer actually branded 'Whirlpool', it is not covered by this recall. If it is Hotpoint, Indesit, Creda, Swan or Proline, check the recall list.

Reported on: digitaltrends.com

Enterprise Singapore safety alert: joins UK OPSS in advising stop-use for Whirlpool/Hotpoint/Indesit/Creda/Swan/Proline dryers

Singapore's consumer product safety regulator joined the UK OPSS in advising consumers to stop using affected dryers from Whirlpool, Hotpoint, Indesit, Creda, Swan and Proline brands manufactured 2004–2015, citing the same lint-to-heating-element fire risk.

What to check. This confirms the recall has international reach and that regulators outside the EU/UK also advise stop-use.

Reported on: consumerproductsafety.gov.sg

IFS (Germany): Whirlpool dryers among brands still causing fires investigated by German insurance institute

Safety risk

The German Institute for Damage Prevention and Damage Research (IFS) of public insurers reports it still investigates fires in dryers from brands including Bauknecht and Whirlpool, linked to an older pattern of damage accumulation. This is independent German evidence that Whirlpool dryers appear in fire investigations.

What to check. German evidence shows Whirlpool dryers appear in fire investigations; owners should follow the recall guidance for affected models.

Reported on: ifs-ev.org

UK Commons Library: Whirlpool identified fault in two dryer models; consumers waited up to 16 months for repair

The UK House of Commons Library briefing confirms Whirlpool identified the fault in two dryer models (April 2004–September 2015) under Hotpoint, Indesit, Creda brands, and documents that consumers waited up to 16 months for repairs despite being promised 6–8 weeks.

What to check. If you own an affected machine, expect possible long delays for repair; consider demanding replacement or refund instead.

Reported on: commonslibrary.parliament.uk
