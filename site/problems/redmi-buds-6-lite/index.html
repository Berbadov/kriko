Redmi Buds 6 Lite one earbud stopped charging after a month (owner, Slovak retailer review)

Serious

A buyer reports one earbud stopped charging after a month on a replacement unit, and the first unit lasted about a year.

What to checkA single earbud refusing to charge is a known failure mode; clean the charging contacts and try the reset, but budget for a replacement or warranty claim if it persists.

Redmi Buds 6 Lite unpair mid-use while watching YouTube or listening to music (owner, German forum)

To check

A German owner reports the earbuds unpair mid-use (YouTube, music) and audio falls back to the phone speaker, happening twice within 5 minutes.

What to checkExpect occasional mid-session disconnects; a factory reset (10s button hold in case) is the manufacturer's suggested fix, but if it recurs the unit may need after-sales service.

Reported oncomputerbase.de

Minor2

Redmi Buds 6 Lite bass drops out when ANC is on at higher volume (owner, Slovak retailer review)

Minor

Owners report the bass drops out when ANC is enabled at higher volume; the workaround is turning ANC off, and one owner expects a firmware fix.

What to checkThis is a firmware/ANC quirk, not a hardware defect; if you listen loud with ANC on, expect bass dropouts until a firmware update lands.

Reported onm.alza.sk

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Xiaomi official FAQ acknowledges intermittent sound or noise on Redmi Buds 6 Lite

Minor

Xiaomi's official FAQ addresses intermittent sound/noise, attributing it to battery, distance, obstacles, or interference, and ends with after-sales inspection if unresolved.

What to checkIntermittent audio is acknowledged by Xiaomi; try full charge, closer proximity, and a reset before concluding it is a defect.
