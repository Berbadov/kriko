Sage/Breville Barista Express Impress: known problems

5 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Grinder failed 2 months after purchase in Turkey; service/parts delay

A Turkish owner's SES876 Impress grinder failed only 2 months after purchase. The seller (Enplus) first called it user error and charged 3,000 TL, then said the replacement part had to come from abroad, leaving the machine unrepaired for 2 months.

What to check. In Turkey the grinder failure is not only a mechanical risk but a service risk: owners report being charged for a warranty-age fault and waiting months for a part shipped from overseas. Check the seller's service/parts commitment before buying in TR.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Worth checking

Grinder arrived broken on a new unit

One buyer's unit arrived with a dead grinder, which had to be returned. Combined with the two in-service grinder failures above, the grinder is the most frequently reported failure point on this model.

What to check. Test the grinder immediately on delivery (before the return window closes), since a non-working grinder has been reported on a new unit and the grinder is the most common failure point overall.

Reported on: google.com.do

No low-water protection for the boiler

An owner flags that the Impress has no low-water sensor, so running it dry risks the boiler.

What to check. If you are forgetful about refilling the reservoir, the lack of a low-water cut-off is a real risk of expensive boiler damage. Treat keeping the tank full as a hard habit with this machine.

Reported on: google.com.do

Pressure release valve prevents high-pressure shots (Impress design)

An owner of the Express Impress reports a built-in pressure release valve that caps brew pressure around 50% and dumps pressure when the basket is slightly overfilled, making high-pressure shots impossible.

What to check. This is a design behaviour rather than a breakdown, but it affects whether the machine can make the shots you want. If you habitually overfill or want full-pressure extractions, this model's pressure-limiting valve may frustrate you from day one.

Reported on: google.co.zw

Minor

Drip tray fills with water quickly / high water waste

An owner reports the drip tray fills after only 3–4 coffees due to high water waste, requiring frequent emptying. (This review is on the Impress product page but does not name the exact model, so treat it as a lower-confidence signal.)

What to check. Expect to empty the drip tray often — roughly every few coffees — which is a routine maintenance cost rather than a fault. If water loss seems excessive, check the group seal and reservoir seating.

Reported on: google.com.do
