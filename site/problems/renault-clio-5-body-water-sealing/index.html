Catalyst and DPF failures in emissions

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

Renault Clio models often develop exhaust emissions problems, especially catalytic converter inefficiency and Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) blockage. These issues can lead to MOT failure and expensive repairs.

What to checkCheck for a valid MOT, look for warning lights, and perform an OBD scan for emissions-related codes such as P0420 (catalyst) or DPF blockage codes. Ask if the catalyst or DPF has been replaced recently.

Reported oncarcostcheck.co.uk

Clio 5 AEB system failure triggers warning light

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

The Renault Clio 5 can experience a failure in its autonomous emergency braking system, which may illuminate the master warning light without an obvious mechanical or electrical cause. This suggests a possible design or software flaw that could affect braking performance in critical situations. A buyer cannot easily detect this issue during a standard pre-purchase inspection, so it represents an unpredictable safety risk.

What to checkDuring the viewing, ask the seller whether the master warning light has ever appeared. If possible, have a diagnostic scan performed for AEB-related DTCs (e.g., in the ABS or electronic brake control module). A test drive in a safe, open area may reveal unexpected braking interventions or warning lights.

Reported onaracustasi.com

Coil spring breakage

SeriousFound by looking

Broken coil springs can cause severe handling issues, vehicle instability, and pose a safety risk.

What to checkVisually inspect coil springs for cracks or breaks. Check for uneven ride height or vehicle sagging on one side.

Reported oncarcostcheck.co.uk

Fuel injector leaks cause delayed starts in Clio 5

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

This evidence points to a chronic fuel injector issue in the 1.3 TCe engine, where small leaks cause delayed starts and poor throttle response. The problem persists even after cleaning or replacement, indicating a design or component weakness. Buyers should be aware as this is not easily caught in a routine inspection and can be costly to fix.

What to checkCheck for engine codes like P0171/P0172 during a diagnostic scan. Listen for long cranking times on cold start, smell fuel near the engine bay, and ask if injectors have been replaced or cleaned (and if the problem returned).

Reported onyoldakiusta.com

structural corrosion, chassis weakness

SeriousFound by looking

The Clio5 body shell and subframe can suffer from structural corrosion, affecting body mountings and chassis strength. This is a known chronic issue in Renault Clio models and can compromise safety and lead to expensive repairs.

What to checkDuring inspection, check the underbody for rust, especially body mountings, subframe, sills, and floor pan. Look for bubbling paint or corroded spots. A professional underbody inspection on a lift is necessary to assess structural integrity.

Reported oncarcostcheck.co.uk

Ball joint excessive play

To checkFound by looking

Excessive play in ball joints can lead to loss of wheel alignment, uneven tire wear, and potential wheel detachment.

What to checkCheck for excessive movement in ball joints by applying force to the suspension components. Listen for clunking noises when driving over rough terrain.

Chronic catalytic converter clogging causing loss of power and poor throttle response

To checkFound with a diagnostic scan

The text describes a scenario where the Renault Clio 5 exhibits a loss of power and poor throttle response, which is attributed to potential catalytic converter clogging. This is a known chronic issue in turbocharged engines where exhaust backpressure builds up, restricting exhaust flow and reducing engine performance. The mention of 'katalitik konvertör tıkalı olabilir' (catalytic converter may be clogged) supports this defect.

What to checkCheck for excessive backpressure in the exhaust system using a manifold vacuum gauge or exhaust flow tester. Inspect the catalytic converter for physical damage or excessive heat buildup. Listen for unusual exhaust noises (e.g., rattling or hissing) that may indicate a clogged converter.

Reported onyoldakiusta.com

Crankshaft/camshaft sensor failure on 1.3 TCe

To checkFound with a diagnostic scan

The crankshaft and camshaft position sensors tell the engine computer when to spark and inject fuel. On this 1.3 TCe engine, they can fail chronically, causing delayed cranking, rough idle, and weak throttle response even after other parts are replaced. A buyer cannot see this without a diagnostic scan because it may not illuminate a warning light until the sensor completely fails.

What to checkCheck for DTC codes P0335/P0340 (crank/cam sensor circuit). Try a cold start; note if it cranks long before firing. If the car has service records for repeated sensor or injection-related repairs, suspect it. A scan tool showing intermittent sensor faults confirms the risk.

Reported onyoldakiusta.com
