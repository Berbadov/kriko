Anker PowerCore III 10000 (A1247): known problems

4 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

At a glance

  • CPSC June 2025 recall covers model A1263 only, not the A1247
  • CPSC recall of Anker PowerCore 10000 covers model A1263 only, not the A1247 subject
  • Global Anker recall (Sept 2025) covers A1257/A1647/A1652/A1681/A1689, none of which is the A1247 subject
  • September 2025 global recall models exclude the A1247

Costly or dangerous

CPSC June 2025 recall covers model A1263 only, not the A1247

The official CPSC notice names model A1263 as the only recalled PowerCore 10000. The subject of this research, the PowerCore III 10000 A1247, is a different model and is NOT part of this recall.

What to check. If you own an A1247, this recall does not apply to it. Do not assume the headline 'Anker PowerCore 10000 recall' covers your unit — check the model number printed on the bottom; only A1263 units with qualifying serial numbers are affected.

Reported on: cpsc.gov

CPSC recall of Anker PowerCore 10000 covers model A1263 only, not the A1247 subject

The official CPSC notice states the recall covers only model A1263 (PowerCore 10000), not the A1247 (PowerCore III 10000) subject.

What to check. If you own an A1247, this recall does not apply to it. The recall is only for model A1263 units sold in the U.S. with qualifying serial numbers; check your serial number at anker.com/product-recalls if you have an A1263.

Reported on: cpsc.gov

Worth checking

Global Anker recall (Sept 2025) covers A1257/A1647/A1652/A1681/A1689, none of which is the A1247 subject

A second, global recall covers five other Anker models; none is the A1247 subject.

What to check. This global recall likewise does not apply to the A1247. The A1247 subject appears in no recall notice found in this run.

Reported on: schmidtandclark.com

September 2025 global recall models exclude the A1247

The second, worldwide Anker recall (September 2025) covers models A1257, A1647, A1652, A1681, A1689. The A1247 is not on this list either.

What to check. The A1247 appears in neither the June 2025 A1263 recall nor the September 2025 global recall. No recall or documented fire/overheating event found in this run is tied specifically to the A1247 model.

Reported on: schmidtandclark.com
