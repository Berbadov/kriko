Sony WH-1000XM3: known problems

4 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

At a glance

  • Left ear cup swivel point breaks, requiring full headband replacement at $198
  • Side sliders crack and break during normal use — chronic, widely reported issue
  • Ear pad material flaking/tearing after ~2 years of use
  • Erratic behavior in cold temperatures — official Sony acknowledgement

Costly or dangerous

Left ear cup swivel point breaks, requiring full headband replacement at $198

Owner reports the plastic swivel joint broke about a week after the 1-year warranty expired. Sony's authorized repair center quoted $198 to replace the entire headband because the swivel is not sold as a separate part. This makes the repair cost nearly the price of a new pair.

What to check. Buyers should check the swivel joints early and consider that a broken swivel effectively totals the headphones since Sony only offers a full headband replacement at ~$198.

Reported on: bestbuy.com

Side sliders crack and break during normal use — chronic, widely reported issue

A reviewer describes the side sliders as a chronic problem with hundreds of user posts about the same breakage point during normal use. The plastic piece is described as structural but designed as if non-structural, breaking from fatigue. Users resort to DIY fixes like super glue, zip ties, or 3D-printed replacements.

What to check. This is a known design weakness. Buyers should expect the side sliders to potentially crack with normal use and budget for aftermarket reinforcement or replacement parts.

Reported on: google.com.sa

Minor

Ear pad material flaking/tearing after ~2 years of use

An owner review on SoundGuys reports the soft parts (ear pads) started tearing after about 2 years of careful daily use. Another reviewer on Google Shopping also reported the material on the ear cups started flaking off. This is a consumable-wear issue rather than a structural failure.

What to check. Ear pads are a wear item expected to degrade after roughly 2 years. Buyers should budget for replacement ear pads as a normal maintenance cost.

Reported on: soundguys.com

Erratic behavior in cold temperatures — official Sony acknowledgement

Sony's official Turkish support page (dated 14.05.2025) acknowledges the WH-1000XM3 can behave unpredictably when subjected to sudden, significant temperature changes — symptoms include sudden volume changes and track skipping. Sony states the device is designed for optimal operation between 0°C and 40°C and recommends a power cycle as a temporary fix.

What to check. This is an official Sony acknowledgement of a known quirk. Buyers in cold climates (e.g., TR/EU winters) may experience erratic behavior when moving between warm indoors and cold outdoors; a restart is the official workaround.

Reported on: sony.com.tr
