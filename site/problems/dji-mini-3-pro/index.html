Gimbal high-frequency vibration/shaking in footage, DJI suggests calibration and prop inspection

To check

On the official DJI forum, a support reply attributes heatwave-like video distortion to high-frequency gimbal vibration, listing bent/damaged props, debris, and gimbal motor issues as causes, and recommends calibration and prop replacement.

What to checkIf footage shows heatwave-like distortion, check props for damage and calibrate the gimbal before assuming a camera fault; the fix may be as cheap as new props.

Reported onforum.dji.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Mini 3 Pro gimbal produces shuddering/stuttering video, fixed by DJI warranty swap

To check

An owner on the DPReview forum reports the gimbal producing shuddering video on playback about half the time; cleaning the gimbal only partly helped, and DJI replaced the whole drone under warranty.

What to checkIf video playback shudders, try cleaning and calibrating the gimbal first, but be prepared that this can require a warranty repair or replacement unit.

Reported ondpreview.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

Firmware vulnerability lets an attacker exhaust storage and block firmware updates (CVE-2026-78251)

Minor

An official INCIBE-CERT early-warning notice lists the Mini 3 Pro (firmware up to 01.00.0900) among affected models for a hardcoded-credential FTP flaw that can exhaust storage and block firmware updates.

What to checkThis requires an attacker with network/USB access, so practical impact for a typical owner is low, but keep firmware updated to the patched version.

Reported onincibe.es
