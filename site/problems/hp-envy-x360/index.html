Costly or dangerous

Envy x360 battery fails within the first year, needing paid replacement

A Best Buy owner review reports battery failure right out of the box and a paid replacement within the first year, alongside a hinge break. This is an owner-reported reliability claim on a retail review page.

What to check. Test the battery early in the warranty period; if it randomly shuts off or fails to power on, file a claim before the warranty lapses because out-of-warranty replacement is paid.

Reported on: bestbuy.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Envy x360 hinge assembly separates from chassis, cracking the display

An owner on HP's own community forum reports the hinge separating under normal use and destroying the display, calling it a documented design flaw. A Best Buy owner review in the same run independently reports the hinge breaking and the screen separating from the lid.

What to check. Check the hinge area on any used unit and treat a cracked/separating screen as hinge damage, not accidental damage; HP has not recalled it.

Reported on: h30434.www3.hp.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking

Envy x360 keyboard stops responding; welded into palmrest makes repair costly

An iFixit thread documents the built-in keyboard stopping responding after updates, with the fixer noting the keyboard is welded into the palmrest so the whole upper case must be replaced at roughly $100+ in parts. A Czech Alza review in the same run reports the keyboard randomly disconnecting twice, requiring two warranty claims.

What to check. If keys stop responding, a USB keyboard confirms it is the internal unit; budget for a full upper-case replacement since the keyboard is not separately replaceable on newer models.

Reported on: it.ifixit.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Envy x360 locks up with a loud buzzing noise during voice chat

A Best Buy owner describes a lockup with a loud buzzing noise during voice chat, calling it a known issue HP had not fixed. This matches the widely reported 'Envy x360 buzzing' audio problem.

What to check. If you rely on Zoom/Discord calls, verify the audio driver/firmware is current before buying; a lockup mid-call requiring a full restart is disruptive.

Reported on: bestbuy.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Envy x360 runs hot to the touch even on light tasks and while charging

A brand-new 16-ac0000 owner reports the chassis getting hot even with only Chrome running and while charging. A German computerbase.de forum thread in the same run reports constant high fan noise and thermal throttling on the 13" 2018 model, and a Best Buy owner reports overheating followed by a no-power failure.

What to check. Expect a warm chassis under load and during charging; a cooling pad and balanced power mode help, but severe throttling or no-power after overheating is a repair-worthy fault.

Reported on: h30434.www3.hp.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Envy x360 storage drive fails within days, with repeated warranty claims

A verified Czech Alza review reports the HDD failing the second day and being sent for warranty repair, and another verified reviewer reports the HDD failing twice and losing data. This is an EU-market owner report.

What to check. Back up immediately and confirm the storage warranty; an HDD that fails within days means data loss and a warranty claim right after purchase.

Reported on: m.alza.cz

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27
