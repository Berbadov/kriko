Redmi Buds 6: known problems

4 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Left earbud goes silent after ~3 months of use (Redmi Buds 6)

Owner review on a Ukrainian retailer page. Two separate buyers report the left earbud losing sound after about 3 months of normal use, one noting it is a repeat occurrence with this brand.

What to check. If you buy these, treat one-side sound loss after a few months as a realistic failure mode; check warranty/return terms before purchase since this appears after the typical return window.

Reported on: comfy.ua

Worth checking

Battery holds ~2.5 hours instead of claimed 10 hours (Redmi Buds 6)

Owner review on the same Ukrainian retailer page. The buyer reports the earbuds hold only about 2.5 hours of charge versus the advertised 10 hours without the case.

What to check. Expect real-world battery life to be well under the advertised figure; if long single-session battery life matters, this is a shortfall to weigh.

Reported on: comfy.ua

Xiaomi acknowledges audio cut-off at start of playback (firmware quirk)

Xiaomi confirms the first word of playback can be cut off in non-noise-canceling mode and that a firmware fix was planned for November 2024.

What to check. If you hear the start of words/audio clipped, switching to ANC or transparency mode is the documented workaround; a firmware update was promised to fix it.

Reported on: mi.com

Minor

Xiaomi acknowledges battery-level difference between left and right buds (firmware quirk)

Xiaomi confirms the app can show a large battery difference between the left and right buds and that a firmware fix was planned for November 2024.

What to check. A mismatched left/right battery reading is a known, acknowledged quirk rather than a defect; a firmware update may resolve it, but verify your unit has the latest firmware.

Reported on: mi.com
