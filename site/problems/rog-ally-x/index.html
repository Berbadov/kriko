ASUS UK reports 'high repair volume' on ROG Xbox Ally X; USB/button/motherboard complaints

Serious

This is about the newer ROG Xbox Ally X variant, not the 2024 ROG Ally X. ASUS UK told a Windows Central writer its repair centers were overloaded and diverted repairs overseas; anecdotal Reddit reports point to USB-port, sticky-button and motherboard issues.

What to checkFor the Xbox-branded Ally X, expect slow warranty turnaround (repairs sent overseas) and a higher chance of board-level faults. Buy with a retailer that handles warranty locally, and log any fault early.

Reported ontechpowerup.com

ROG Xbox Ally X unit died in sleep, refused to charge (no LED) after ~5 months

Serious

A Windows Central journalist's £800 ROG Xbox Ally X (again the Xbox variant) failed to wake from sleep and then refused to charge, with no charging LED, after about five months of light use. The charger was verified working on another device.

What to checkFor the Xbox-branded Ally X, a sleep-to-dead power failure is documented, and recovery (power-button holds) failed in this case. Keep the unit charged and be ready to use warranty if it won't wake or charge.

Reported ontech.yahoo.com

Worth checking1

ROG Ally X crashes/freezes/BSODs traced to drivers, heat, sleep resume and storage

To check

An owner-facing troubleshooting guide lists the recurring crash causes for the ROG Ally X: Windows updates swapping in a generic AMD driver, BIOS issues, unstable sleep resume, heat, and storage corruption.

What to checkIf the unit crashes or fails to wake, the first suspects are a Windows-update driver swap and sleep-resume bugs, not hardware. Roll back/reinstall the AMD driver and update BIOS before assuming a faulty unit.

Reported onevezone.evetech.co.za
