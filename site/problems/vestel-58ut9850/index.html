58ut9850 rechargeable remote ignores most button presses within 2 months

To check

An owner of the 58ut9850 reports the bundled rechargeable remote stops registering most button presses within about two months of purchase, despite a full battery; only the Netflix and power keys respond.

What to checkBefore buying, budget for a possible remote replacement or service visit; the remote is the most-reported problem specific to this model. Verify the remote works fully during the return window.

Minor
