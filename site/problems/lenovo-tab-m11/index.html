Lenovo Tab M11: known problems

4 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

At a glance

  • Mainboard replaced under warranty after heavy freezing
  • Tablet slows down and cannot handle two apps at once
  • Tablet stops connecting to Wi-Fi after powering off
  • Tablet freezes after a complete discharge

Costly or dangerous

Mainboard replaced under warranty after heavy freezing

Found via 'Lenovo Tab M11 problems review' (Comfy.ua customer review).

What to check. A buyer reports the tablet started freezing heavily a few months after purchase and needed boards replaced under warranty, so a hardware fault is possible within the first year.

Reported on: comfy.ua

Tablet slows down and cannot handle two apps at once

Found via 'Lenovo Tab M11 problems review' (Alza.cz customer review, Czech market).

What to check. A verified Czech buyer reports the tablet slows down a lot and cannot handle two apps at once, so multitasking performance is a real limitation for this entry-tier model.

Reported on: m.alza.cz

Tablet stops connecting to Wi-Fi after powering off

Found via 'Lenovo Tab M11 sorun şikayet' (Comfy.ua customer review).

What to check. An owner reports the tablet stops connecting to Wi-Fi after it powers off; a factory reset fixes it temporarily but the problem recurs, pointing to a Wi-Fi module fault.

Reported on: comfy.ua

Minor

Tablet freezes after a complete discharge

Found via 'Lenovo Tab M11 reliability review forum' (Galaxus.de customer review, German/Swiss market).

What to check. A Galaxus buyer notes the tablet freezes and takes very long to restart after a complete discharge, so letting the battery fully drain should be avoided.

Reported on: galaxus.de
