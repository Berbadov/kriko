Mainboard replaced, drive-wheel frame broke, then 'Unit temperature protection' error — Estonian consumer regulator upheld refund

Serious

An official Estonian Consumer Complaints Commission decision (case 19-1/25-07524, 25.08.2025).

What to checkThis is a regulator decision, the strongest source type. The unit needed a mainboard replacement, then the drive-wheel frame broke, then the battery drained fast and the robot threw a 'Unit temperature protection' error and disconnected from the dock/app. The commission ordered the seller to refund the full €1219. Expect multi-week service turnaround (five weeks each visit) and recurring faults that can end in full refund rather than repair.

Reported onjvis.ttja.ee

Recurring water leakage from the bottom, plus charging error — Turkish owner complaint

Serious

Turkish complaint site Şikayetvar, S8 Pro Ultra water-leakage category.

What to checkA Turkish owner reports water leaking from the unit two weeks after purchase, followed by a charging error, and four service visits that returned the unit unrepaired. This is a documented water-leakage failure mode in the TR market with poor service resolution.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Worth checking1

Error 1 tied to the LDS laser unit — German owner forum

To check

Galaxus (German) question-and-answer thread for the S8 Pro Ultra.

What to checkA German owner got Error 1 within about a month of ownership; the fix discussed is manually rotating the top laser unit and restarting. This is a known, usually recoverable fault, but it can recur and points to the LDS turret as a weak spot.
