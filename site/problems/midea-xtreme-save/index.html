Costly or dangerous

Xtreme Save unit fails to cool within weeks of purchase

Turkish buyer of three Xtreme Save units reports one arrived faulty and would not cool for ~1.5 months; dealer closed and Midea service unresponsive.

What to check. A cooling failure that appears immediately means the unit is unusable from day one and warranty service may be slow; verify the seller and service network before purchase.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Worth checking

Xtreme Save chronic heating failure with DF error and cracked line set

Turkish owner reports persistent heating failure with a 'df' error; service found the line set cracked during installation causing loss of cooling, and a switch-like part faulting.

What to check. Heating failures with a 'df' error can trace to installation damage (cracked pipe) or a control part; repeated service visits without a lasting fix are a pattern in these reports.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Xtreme Save control-board error codes E7/F4 rated critical

French error-code reference lists Xtreme Save among models affected by control-board faults E7 (indoor/display board communication) and F4 (outdoor EEPROM), both rated 'critique' and requiring a qualified technician.

What to check. Control-board faults are electrical and not user-serviceable; an E7/F4 error means a board replacement, which is a significant repair cost and typically needs an authorised service visit.

Reported on: code-erreur-pompe-a-chaleur.fr

Xtreme Save outdoor unit emits constant noise and vibration within three months

Turkish owner complaint, Izmir, 18.000 BTU unit, noise/vibration reported within ~3 months of purchase.

What to check. Before buying, confirm the outdoor unit is firmly mounted on proper anti-vibration mounts and check the unit's sound level in person; noise/vibration complaints appear early in ownership.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Xtreme Save stops with gas-release sound and QF error code

Turkish forum owner of a new Xtreme Save reports the unit stopped with a gas-release sound and a QF error; forum replies attribute it to defrost (DF) but the owner experienced it as a fault.

What to check. A gas-release sound and an error code are worth having a technician verify; if it is defrost it is normal, but a refrigerant leak or compressor fault is costly to diagnose.

Reported on: inceleriz.com

Minor

Xtreme Save outdoor-unit hum transmits through the wall at low fan speed

Turkish forum owner reports a wall-transmitted hum when the inverter drops frequency at set temperature; replies advise checking/replacing the outdoor unit's rubber vibration mounts.

What to check. A low-frequency hum through the wall is often the outdoor unit's vibration mounts; it is fixable but may require a service visit, so factor that in if the unit sits against a bedroom wall.

Reported on: inceleriz.com
