Electronic control board fails after only five uses

Serious

An owner reports the unit would not power on after only five uses, and the authorised service diagnosed a failed electronic board and ordered a spare part.

What to checkControl-board failure can occur very early, leaving the unit dead and requiring a service visit and spare part. This is a high-severity electrical failure; a buyer should keep warranty documents and expect possible service delays.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Inner basket and grill non-stick coating peels within 3 months

Serious

An owner reports the internal basket and grill coating peeling and changing colour at month 3 despite following cleaning instructions, and stopped using the unit over health concerns.

What to checkBuyer should treat the non-stick coating as the main wear risk: it can peel within the first months, making the unit unusable and raising health worries. Check warranty/service response before purchase, since some owners report service refusing replacement.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Unit goes completely dead after a power cut, recurring fault

Serious

An owner reports the unit died completely after a power cut during use, showing no light or warning even after unplugging; the same fault had happened before and required a replacement unit from service.

What to checkA power cut during operation can leave the unit completely dead with no indicator, and this fault recurred on the same owner. This points to fragile power/control electronics; a buyer should ensure surge protection and warranty coverage.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Oil collector part lifts/peels, raising health concern

To check

An owner reports the oil collector part lifting/peeling on the HD9243 while still under warranty, and describes it as a possible health risk.

What to checkThe oil collector's coating can lift over time, which the owner saw as a health risk. A buyer should inspect this part and note that service may treat it as normal wear rather than a defect.
