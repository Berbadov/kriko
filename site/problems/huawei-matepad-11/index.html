Firmware update on MatePad 11 reportedly loses sound and battery autonomy

To check

A buyer review on Technodom for the MatePad 11 (Debussy-W09CS).

What to checkAn owner warns not to update past firmware 2.0.0.221 because the update degrades sound and battery life; another reports installed apps disappearing on their own. Treat OS updates as risky and keep backups.

Reported ontechnodom.kz

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

MatePad 11 Bluetooth keyboard drops connection when detached from the tablet

To check

A Turkish owner on the DonanımHaber MatePad 11 (HarmonyOS) owner thread.

What to checkA Turkish owner reports the official keyboard case drops its Bluetooth link shortly after being detached from the tablet, and that resets (network reset, re-pair, reboot) did not fix it. Relevant to TR buyers using the keyboard case.

Reported onmini.donanimhaber.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

MatePad 11 OS updates are inconsistent and newer HarmonyOS versions break some apps

To check

A GSMarena user review comparing MatePad 11 (2021) and (2023).

What to checkOwners report inconsistent OS updates and that moving to newer HarmonyOS versions can break preinstalled Huawei apps (e.g. WPS PC Software Engine) because the OS is based on older Android. Check app compatibility before updating.

Reported onm.gsmarena.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

MatePad 11 battery drains fast even at 99% max capacity; one owner stopped using it

To check

Kakaku.com (価格.com) owner forum thread 'バッテリーの消費' for the MatePad 11.

What to checkOwners report heavy battery consumption even with battery health at 99%, with background apps restarting after screen-on; one owner says the tablet became unusable. Expect short battery life and check battery health before buying used.

Reported onbbs.kakaku.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27
