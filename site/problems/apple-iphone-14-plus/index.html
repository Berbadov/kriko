Apple iPhone 14 Plus: known problems

3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

iPhone 14 Plus continuous panic reboot loop (SMC BSC failure 0x500000)

Owner/repair thread on iFixit describing a specific hardware panic signature.

What to check. A 14 Plus stuck in a 3-minute reboot loop may be a charging-port flex/back-glass sensor hardware fault, not a dead battery — a battery swap may not fix it. Get a proper panic-log diagnosis before paying for parts.

Reported on: ts.ifixit.com

Worth checking

iPhone 14 Plus battery health drops quickly (Turkish owner reports)

Turkish complaint site (Şikayetvar) — owner reports battery health at 95% after 7 months.

What to check. A Turkish owner reports unusually fast battery-health loss (95% in 7 months) despite original charger and not charging while in use. Warranty battery claims may be contested by Apple, so check coverage early.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

iPhone 14 Plus green line is an OLED hardware failure software cannot fix

Repair-shop guide describing the green-line defect across iPhone X–14.

What to check. A green line is a display-panel hardware fault — no software fix exists; it requires a screen replacement. Not specific to the 14 Plus but documented on it.

Reported on: sdindianaphones.com
