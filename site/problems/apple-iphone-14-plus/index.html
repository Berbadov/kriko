iPhone 14 Plus continuous panic reboot loop (SMC BSC failure 0x500000)

Serious

Owner/repair thread on iFixit describing a specific hardware panic signature.

What to checkA 14 Plus stuck in a 3-minute reboot loop may be a charging-port flex/back-glass sensor hardware fault, not a dead battery — a battery swap may not fix it. Get a proper panic-log diagnosis before paying for parts.

Reported onts.ifixit.com

Worth checking2

iPhone 14 Plus battery health drops quickly (Turkish owner reports)

To check

Turkish complaint site (Şikayetvar) — owner reports battery health at 95% after 7 months.

What to checkA Turkish owner reports unusually fast battery-health loss (95% in 7 months) despite original charger and not charging while in use. Warranty battery claims may be contested by Apple, so check coverage early.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

iPhone 14 Plus green line is an OLED hardware failure software cannot fix

To check

Repair-shop guide describing the green-line defect across iPhone X–14.

What to checkA green line is a display-panel hardware fault — no software fix exists; it requires a screen replacement. Not specific to the 14 Plus but documented on it.

Reported onsdindianaphones.com
