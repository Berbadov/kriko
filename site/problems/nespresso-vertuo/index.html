Vertuo Pop broke three times in one year, leaks and stops dispensing coffee

Serious

Came from the Italian consumer-complaint board Altroconsumo (Vertuo Pop, subscription machine).

What to checkA Vertuo Pop reported to fail three times within a year, returning from service still leaking and not dispensing coffee. The owner's cost is repeated service trips and wasted coffee, with no working machine in between.

Reported onaltroconsumo.it

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Vertuo button blinks red and capsule is wasted after starting a brew

Serious

Came from the Portuguese consumer-complaint board Deco Proteste (Vertuo, under warranty).

What to checkThe machine starts a brew then aborts with a blinking red button, wasting a capsule each time; the owner reports the fault persists after a service visit and an online reset. Cost is wasted capsules plus service time with no resolution.

Reported ondeco.proteste.pt

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking1

Vertuo Next defective pump, repaired at Repair Café

To check

Came from the Dutch Repair Café repair forum (Vertuo Next, DeLonghi/other OEM).

What to checkA repair volunteer identifies a defective pump in a Vertuo Next and gets it running again. This shows the pump is a replaceable/repairable part, though the repair requires disassembly skill.

Reported onrepaircafe-forum.org
