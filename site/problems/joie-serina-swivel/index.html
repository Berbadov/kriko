Joie Serina Swivel: known problems

3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

InfoCons consumer alert: Serina Swivel seat can separate, non-compliant with GPSR

This is a consumer-protection alert (InfoCons) reflecting the EU Safety Gate notification for the same product, confirming the defect and the GPSR non-compliance.

What to check. Independent confirmation that the recall is EU-wide (notifying country Ireland, origin China). The swing was sold via Smyths stores and online, so check any unit bought from Smyths.

Reported on: infocons.org

Joie Serina Swivel (Elephant Duo) recalled — steel bolt securing seat unit to frame can fail

This is the Irish regulator CCPC's official product-safety notification (published 12 March 2026).

What to check. If you own a Serina Swivel (Elephant Duo), check the batch number on the identification sticker on the underside of the swing arm/seat. If it is 190225, 200225, 210225 or 220225, stop using it immediately; CCPC says Joie/Smyths offer free collection, a full refund, or an upgrade to the Joie Serina 2 in 1.

Reported on: ccpc.ie

Joie official safety notice: Serina Swivel seat can separate from base on affected batch

This is the manufacturer Joie's own product safety notice (published 2 March 2026), the primary source for the defect.

What to check. The manufacturer's notice restricts the defect to units manufactured 19, 21 or 22 February 2025 (model W1306DAELD000) and says units outside those dates are safe to continue using. Check the date on the label under the base before using.

Reported on: joiebaby.com
