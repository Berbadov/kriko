Green vertical line appears on A15 5G display after a software update

Serious

An owner on Samsung's own community reports a green line appearing after the December 2024 update; Samsung's reply directs to a service center for replacement/repair.

What to checkA green line is typically a panel-level defect, not fixable by software; expect a screen replacement cost if it occurs. Check warranty/service-center coverage before paying.

Reported onr2.community.samsung.com:443

Worth checking3

Battery drains too fast and screen stays dark on incoming calls

To check

A verified buyer review on Samsung's Finnish store page reports the battery drains too quickly and the screen does not wake when a call comes in.

What to checkOwners report noticeably fast drain despite the 5000 mAh cell, plus a screen-not-waking quirk on calls. If battery life is a priority, verify the unit's behavior early in the return window.

Reported onsamsung.com

Coloured vertical lines on the A15 indicate a damaged AMOLED panel needing replacement

To check

A UK repair-parts shop's diagnostic guide states coloured vertical lines on the A15 mean the AMOLED panel is damaged and the whole display assembly must be swapped.

What to checkIf a green/pink/white line appears, software fixes won't help; the display assembly must be replaced. Budget for a screen replacement cost if this happens outside warranty.

Reported onsupremephoneparts.com

Phone is slow, lags, and freezes from first use

To check

A buyer review on Samsung's Finnish page describes the phone as slow, laggy, and freezing from first setup, and says other reviewers report the same.

What to checkMultiple EU owners describe sluggishness and input lag even for basic use. If you need a snappy budget phone, this model's performance is a common complaint to weigh.

Reported onsamsung.com
