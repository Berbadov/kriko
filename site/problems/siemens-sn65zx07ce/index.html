Pump failure after one year on Siemens SN65ZX07CE

Serious

A Czech user review on Alza.cz (rated 4.0/5 from 6 users) reports the pump died after one year. The reviewer notes that newer pumps from Siemens and Bosch are designed for low consumption and noise but are more sensitive and fail faster. This is a verified-purchase owner report of a major component failure.

What to checkThe circulation pump is a critical, expensive part. If it fails after the warranty period, expect a significant repair bill. Consider extended warranty coverage for this model.

Siemens official: E31/E15/E3100 = water protection system activated

Serious

Siemens' own German support page states E31 (along with E15, F15, E3100) means the water protection system is activated. This corroborates the owner-reported E31 on the SN65ZX07CE and confirms it is a water-leak safety trigger, not a software glitch.

What to checkTreat E31 as a potential leak. Siemens explicitly advises against self-repair for health/safety reasons: close the water tap, switch the unit off, and book a service visit. Expect a technician diagnosis rather than a user fix.

Reported onsiemens-home.bsh-group.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Detergent dosing leaves tablets undissolved; touch control delays; cutlery basket fixation

Minor

Onliner.by's AI-generated summary of verified reviews for the exact SN65ZX07CE lists recurring minor complaints: the detergent dosing system sometimes leaves tablets undissolved, the touch control can respond with delay or error, the cutlery basket fixation is poorly designed, some programs run long, and plastic items stay wet even after intensive drying.

What to checkThese are low-severity usability annoyances rather than failures. If tablets come out undissolved, check loading and the detergent dispenser flap; if the touch panel misbehaves, it may be a sensor sensitivity issue. None of these should stop a purchase, but they set expectations on drying of plastics and program length.
