Health Canada recall of Instant Pot Smart-60 for electrical hazard

Serious

Health Canada joint recall notice (RA-54204, July 15, 2015) confirming the same Smart-60 electrical recall: UPC 853084004132, UL certification file E214884, and date codes 1410 to 1503. Independent confirmation of the CPSC recall details.

What to checkConfirms the affected model and date-code range from a second regulator. Same check applies: verify the date code under the base before use.

Reported onrecalls-rappels.canada.ca

Instant Pot Smart/Smart-60 recalled for electric-shock hazard from thermal probe in base

Serious

This finding came from the recall search. The U.S. CPSC and Health Canada jointly recalled the Instant Pot Smart and Smart-60 in July 2015 because the thermal probe in the base can conduct electricity through the cooker, posing an electric-shock risk. The recall covers serial numbers 1410-1503 and manufacture dates 12/1/2014-6/1/2015; three consumers reported being shocked. A buyer should check the serial number on the label under the base; an affected unit should be stopped and the base replaced free.

What to checkIf you own or are buying a Smart/Smart-60, check the serial number and manufacture date on the label under the base. Units in the 1410-1503 range are recalled for an electric-shock hazard and should not be used; contact Instant Pot for a free replacement base. The current Turkish-market 'Akıllı Pişirici' (Smart Cooker) is a different, newer model and is not covered by this recall.

Reported oncpsc.gov

Instant Pot Smart-60 child burn lawsuit alleges lid opening ejected scalding contents

To check

April 2025 Florida lawsuit (Case 6:25-cv-00618) alleges a 2-year-old was burned in May 2023 when the Smart-60 lid was opened and scalding contents ejected. Another allegation in litigation, consistent with the Wilder complaint's lid-under-pressure theory.

What to checkSecond independent source alleging the same failure mode (lid opening under pressure) on the Smart-60. Both are pending lawsuits, so treat as reported risk rather than confirmed defect. Caution with lid opening is warranted.

Reported ontheclarkfirmtexas.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Instant Pot Smart-60 lid removable under pressure, per court complaint

To check

Federal complaint (Wilder v. Instant Brands, Docket 1:23-cv-1964) alleges the Smart-60 lid can be removed while pressure, heat and steam remain inside, causing scalding burn injuries. This is an allegation in litigation, not an adjudicated finding.

What to checkThis is a plaintiff's allegation, not a proven defect. If you own a Smart-60, never force the lid open and always confirm the float valve has dropped before opening. Treat this as a reported risk, not a confirmed failure.

Reported onjohnsonbecker.com
