Cam follower wear destroys camshaft in EA888

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

In some EA888 engines the cam follower under the high-pressure fuel pump wears through its coating. Once the metal is exposed it quickly damages the follower and the camshaft. This is a known failure that can be checked by a mechanic and typically appears around 30,000 miles.

What to checkDuring a pre-purchase inspection, require the fuel pump to be removed so the cam follower's coating can be inspected for wear. Watch for fuel pressure loss or a check engine light. If the coating is worn, budget for replacement of the follower and possibly the camshaft.

Reported ontorquecars.com

Chronic oil consumption in EA888 Gen 2 engine

SeriousFound by looking

The second-generation EA888 2.0L engine has a known piston ring design defect causing excessive oil burning, often 1 quart per 600–1,200 miles. This can lead to engine damage and was addressed by a class-action settlement. Buyers of older EA888-equipped cars (2008–2013) should be aware.

What to checkCheck oil level and dipstick for low level, look for blue smoke from exhaust on cold start, ask for service records showing piston/ring replacement or oil consumption complaints.

Reported onampauto.io
