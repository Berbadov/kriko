Costly or dangerous2

Burning chemicals/rubber smell persists after repeated burn-off runs — fills the house

Serious

This is a single-owner report but severe if it happens; the manufacturer instructs a burn-off, and this owner says it did not clear.

What to checkIf the unit smells strongly of chemicals/rubber on first use, run the burn-off as instructed; if it persists after several runs, this owner's experience suggests it may not clear and the unit may be unusable indoors.

Reported onbestbuy.com

Error code 2 (Err2) on Flip Toaster Oven — thermal sensor fault, unit dead within months

Serious

The manual (manualslib SP150 Series) corroborates that Err2/Err3/Err4 are not user-serviceable and require calling customer service.

What to checkIf you buy this unit and it throws Err2, it is not a DIY fix — the manual directs you to call Ninja service, so the failure typically means a warranty claim or replacement. Confirm the warranty terms before buying.

Reported onbestbuy.com

Worth checking1

EU model SP101UK: interior heating bars and bottom plate impossible to clean; flimsy build

To check

This is the EU/UK model (SP101UK), directly relevant to the TR/EU market. Cleaning difficulty and a 'tinny' build are the recurring complaints.

What to checkFor the EU/UK market, expect the interior (heating bars, bottom plate) to be very hard to clean and the build to feel light/tinny; the drip rack and roasting tin are not dishwasher-safe. Cleaning effort is the main ongoing cost.

Reported onamazon.ae
