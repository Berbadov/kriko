Battery failure on GT 5 46mm and 2400 TL repair quote (TR)

Serious

A Turkish owner reports battery failure on the GT 5 46mm and a 2400 TL repair quote for the battery on a watch that cost 4000 TL.

What to checkIf buying in TR, factor in that a battery replacement can cost over half the watch's price and that the owner reports Huawei charging for it even when the watch was never submerged.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

GT 5 mainboard failure within first year, watch dead (TR)

Serious

A Turkish owner reports the GT 5 dying completely from a mainboard fault within its first year, requiring warranty service.

What to checkTotal failure from a mainboard fault inside the first year is reported; the owner had to send it to an authorised service under warranty.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

GT 5 overheated and never turned on again, no spare parts (Ukrainian owner)

Serious

A Ukrainian owner reports the GT 5 41mm suddenly overheating and never powering on again, with no repair parts available.

What to checkSudden overheating followed by total failure is reported, and the owner says no spare parts exist for repair, so the watch is a total loss.

Reported oncomfy.ua

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

GT 5 stuck at 0% and stuck in reboot loop overnight (TR forum)

Serious

An owner reports the GT 5 waking dead at 0% and endlessly rebooting even on a fast-charge powerbank, concluding the battery died.

What to checkA GT 5 can fail to charge and loop-reboot overnight even when the battery was not low; the owner had to send it to service under warranty.

Reported onforum.donanimhaber.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

GT 5 screen freezes and stops responding (TR)

To check

A Turkish owner reports the GT 5 screen freezing and not responding, persisting through restart, reset and update.

What to checkA frozen, unresponsive screen may not be fixable by the owner; the complaint asks for hardware/software repair under warranty.

To check

A Ukrainian owner reports screen interference/lines on the GT 5 41mm after ~9 months and sent it to service.

What to checkDisplay faults appearing around 9 months are reported by owners; the owner faced possible non-repairability and replacement.

Minor1

Official Huawei TR bulletin on Bluetooth connection drops on wearables incl. GT 5

Minor

Huawei's own Turkish support bulletin lists the GT 5 among applicable products and documents common Bluetooth connection-drop causes and fixes.

What to checkConnection drops are a recognised, documented issue on the GT 5; the bulletin's fixes are mostly environmental (distance, interference, app backgrounding) rather than hardware defects.

Reported onconsumer.huawei.com
