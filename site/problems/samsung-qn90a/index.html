50QN90A boot loop with blinking red light; owner reports chronic panel/power design defect

Serious

A 50-inch QE50QN90AATXTK owner describes the same boot-loop symptom (red light blinks twice, stays on black screen) after ~4 years, and states the authorized service and his own research attribute it to a chronic panel/power-supply design defect in the series.

What to checkThis corroborates the boot-loop pattern across screen sizes in the series. The owner reports the panel replacement quote was near the price of a new TV, making out-of-warranty repair uneconomical.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

QE55QN90AATXK enters boot loop after warranty; service quotes 35,750 TL panel replacement

Serious

Turkish owner of a 55-inch QE55QN90AATXK reports spontaneous boot loop shortly after warranty expiry; authorized service diagnosed 'panel arızası' (panel failure) without any inspection and quoted 35,750 TL — more than the TV's second-hand value.

What to checkA buyer should treat the QN90A panel as a known failure point after the warranty window. The repair quote is near the cost of a new TV, so an extended warranty or a different series is worth considering.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

QN90A 85-inch owner reports bootloop after 3 years; another panel failure after 2.5 years

Serious

On Samsung's own Danish product page for the 85-inch QN90A, a German customer review reports a bootloop after 3 years, and another review reports the panel defective after 2.5 years with repair uneconomical. These are on the manufacturer's official page, so they are first-party customer reports.

What to checkThis is independent corroboration from the manufacturer's own site, in the EU market, of the boot-loop and panel-failure pattern at 2.5–3 years.

Reported onsamsung.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking1

QN90A CEC control with receiver stops working after firmware update, later resolves itself

To check

An AVS Forum owner reports CEC (HDMI-CEC) control between the QN90A and an Onkyo receiver stopped working, possibly after a firmware update, but resolved itself without any changes. This is a transient connectivity annoyance, not a persistent defect.

What to checkCEC glitches after firmware updates are common and here self-resolved. Low concern for buyers; a firmware update or power cycle usually restores it.

Reported onavsforum.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

QN90A from 2021 emits loud cracking noises from the TV after 2.5 years

Minor

A German owner of a 2021 QN90A reports loud cracking noises coming from the TV after ~2.5 years, with no picture faults. This is a low-severity cosmetic/mechanical concern, not a functional failure.

What to checkCracking noises from the chassis are annoying but not a functional failure; the owner reports no picture issues. Low priority for a buyer.

Reported oncomputerbase.de
