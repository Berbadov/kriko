Bosch/Siemens/Neff/Constructa 60 cm glass ceramic hob: electronic component failure can switch the rear cooking zones on by themselves (fire risk)

SeriousSafety risk

Austrian consumer-protection notice (konsument.at) describing the official recall of 60 cm glass ceramic hobs under Bosch, Siemens, Neff and Constructa, bought from January 2006, with two induction and two electric zones.

What to checkThis is exactly the subject product (60 cm, 2006, four brands). Before buying or using a 2006 60 cm Bosch/Siemens/Neff/Constructa glass ceramic hob, check whether it is part of the recall; the manufacturer committed to repairing it. Do not leave objects on the hob unattended and switch off the circuit when not in use until it is checked.

Reported onkonsument.at:443

Bosch/Siemens/Neff/Constructa glass ceramic hob with two induction plates recalled for fire risk (RAPEX)

SeriousSafety risk

Official notice from the Cyprus Department of Electromechanical Services (Ministry of Communications) listing the RAPEX entry for the Bosch/Siemens/Neff/Constructa/Balay/Lynx glass ceramic hob with two induction plates, origin Germany and Spain from September 2006, reason: fire.

What to checkThis is the primary regulator source for the recall. It confirms the product identity (glass ceramic hob with two induction plates, four target brands, 2006 origin) and the fire risk. It is the strongest anchor for the claim.

Reported onmcw.gov.cy

Bosch glass cooktop shattered spontaneously during normal cooking

To check

Owner report (ComplaintsBoard) of a Bosch glass cooktop shattering spontaneously during normal cooking; Bosch Türkiye classified it as user-related (invisible micro-crack) and offered only a discounted paid glass replacement.

What to checkThis documents a recurring reported failure mode for Bosch glass hobs (spontaneous shattering with flying glass), but the report does not identify the model, so it is not confirmed for the specific 2006 60 cm unit. Treat it as evidence of a general pattern rather than a finding specific to this model.

Reported oncomplaintsboard.com
