CVE-2026-78321: HTTP media server DoS blocks QuickTransfer (Neo)

To check

INCIBE's advisory CVE-2026-78321 documents a denial-of-service vulnerability in the Neo's HTTP media server (firmware up to 01.00.0400) that blocks QuickTransfer photo/video retrieval, fixed only by a firmware update.

What to checkKeep the Neo's firmware current; the documented fix for this vulnerability is a vendor firmware update, and an unpatched unit can have its media transfer disrupted.

Reported onincibe.es

DJI bulletin: gimbal moves erratically (Neo series)

To check

DJI's official support bulletin lists the Neo series as an applicable product for erratic gimbal movement and gimbal hardware errors, with gimbal calibration in DJI Fly as the first fix.

What to checkIf the gimbal moves erratically or the app warns, try gimbal auto-calibration in DJI Fly first; if it persists the bulletin points to a gimbal hardware error needing service.

Reported onsupport.dji.com

Gimbal rubber bumpers break after a crash

To check

On the iFixit teardown page an owner reports the gimbal's rubber bumpers break in a crash; another owner notes the frame, connectors, motors and gyroscope are glued in place, making DIY repair extremely difficult.

What to checkThe Neo is not user-serviceable: internal parts are glued, so a crash that breaks the gimbal bumpers or frame effectively means replacement or a service-center repair, which can cost close to a new unit.

Reported onzh.ifixit.com
