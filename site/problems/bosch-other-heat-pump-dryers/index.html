Bosch WQG244C1TR Serie 6 heat pump dryer emitted smoke after service cleaning (Turkey)

Serious

Turkish owner complaint (Şikayetvar) for a Bosch Serie 6 heat pump dryer (WQG244C1TR). After a paid cleaning by a Bosch-directed service, the machine emitted smoke at night, filling the home with a burnt smell; the owner demanded replacement. Single-owner report, not an official recall.

What to checkIf smoke or burning smell appears from the dryer, unplug it immediately and do not resume use until an independent inspection; consider replacement if the unit has been serviced.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Bosch WQG244C1TR/05 heat pump dryer keeps heating after cycle ends — owner reports fire risk (Turkey)

SeriousSafety risk

Turkish owner complaint (Şikayetvar) for a Bosch WQG244C1TR/05 purchased 23.10.2024. The owner reports the machine continued running with the heat pump engaged at high heat after the programme had finished, creating serious overheating and a fire risk; the fault recurred 11 times over six months and Bosch service twice said there was no problem.

What to checkIf a Bosch heat pump dryer keeps heating after the cycle ends, unplug it and insist on a detailed inspection; do not accept a 'no fault' finding without testing the programme-termination behaviour.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Bosch WQG244C1TR/05 heat pump dryer keeps heating after programme end; owner reports fire risk (Turkey)

SeriousSafety risk

Finding came from the query 'Bosch WQG244C1TR kurutma makinesi yangın'. A verified owner complaint (Şikayetvar, 29 May 2025) about a Bosch WQG244C1TR/05 heat pump dryer bought 23 Oct 2024 in Turkey: the machine kept running the heat pump with intense heat after the programme ended, creating serious overheating and a fire risk. A technician said there was no problem; the fault recurred 11 times over 6 months. This is a TR-market, non-WDT67 Bosch heat pump dryer, matching the subject.

What to checkAn owner reports a fire risk on this model: the dryer continued heating after the programme ended. If a Bosch heat pump dryer runs hot after the cycle finishes, treat it as a safety concern, stop using it, and escalate beyond a single service visit (the owner reports the fault was dismissed and recurred 11 times).

Bosch WQG24500BY heat pump dryer withdrawn from Serbian market — UMAC compressor wiring electric-shock risk

Serious

Official CEFTA market-surveillance record (economy: Serbia) for Bosch model WQG24500BY, barcode 4242005303526, made in Poland. The compressor's electrical connection was not made to specification during production, creating an electric-shock risk if installed in a household whose electrical installation does not meet safety guidelines. This is the only official recall/withdrawal record I found for a Bosch heat pump dryer in the European region; note the market is Serbia, not EU or Turkey.

What to checkIf you own a WQG24500BY, check the serial number against the Serbian ministry list and contact BSH/Bosch service for inspection; the risk depends on the household's electrical installation.

Reported ontransparency.cefta.int

Bosch WTY 88931 heat pump dryer tripped house circuit breaker — electrical fault (Germany)

To check

German owner complaint (ReclaBox) for a Bosch WTY 88931 heat pump dryer, 31 months old. The house circuit breaker tripped during operation and the machine would not restart; a technician could not locate the fault. Single-owner report of an electrical defect.

What to checkIf a heat pump dryer trips the circuit breaker or RCD during operation, stop using it and have the insulation/electrical system checked before further use.
