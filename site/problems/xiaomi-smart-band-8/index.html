Xiaomi Mi Band 8 stops charging after 3 days of use

Serious

An owner reports the band worked for 3 days then stopped charging entirely.

What to checkEarly charging failure means the band is unusable within days of purchase; a buyer should test charging immediately and keep proof for warranty.

Reported ontechnodom.kz

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Xiaomi Smart Band 8 case comes unglued and water kills it after ~1 year

Serious

An owner reports the case delaminated after a year and two weeks, water entered and the band died; another review on the same page says the sealing gasket leaked and the watch burned out.

What to checkWater-resistance appears to degrade over roughly a year; once the case unglues, water kills the band. A buyer should not rely on the 5 ATM rating past the first year.

Reported ondns-shop.kg

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Xiaomi Smart Band 8 stops accepting charge near empty battery after ~2 years

Serious

A Turkish owner reports the band stopped taking a charge at ~5% battery after two years; the cable and pogo pins were verified to deliver 5V, so the fault is internal to the band.

What to checkA band that stops charging near empty is effectively dead after warranty. The owner had to troubleshoot cable, contacts and power source before concluding the fault was internal. Cost is a full replacement.

Reported onteknoseyir.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking2

Xiaomi Smart Band 8 strap breaks after ~8 months

To check

An owner reports the strap broke after 8 months and the store said it was not a warranty case.

What to checkThe strap is a consumable that fails within the first year and is not covered by warranty; the user must buy a replacement strap.

Reported ondns-shop.kg

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Xiaomi Smart Band 8 unresponsive and drops tracking when paired with iPhone

To check

A Reddit user reports the band becomes unresponsive, is hard to connect, and stops tracking mid-run when paired with an iPhone, while it worked with a Xiaomi phone.

What to checkBluetooth reliability is worse with non-Xiaomi phones, especially iPhone. A buyer using an iPhone may face connection drops and lost workout tracking.

Reported onredditrecs.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

Xiaomi Smart Band 8 black screen and touchscreen errors acknowledged by manufacturer

Minor

Xiaomi's own FAQ states hardware failure can cause screen problems, and lists troubleshooting for black screen and touchscreen errors.

What to checkThe manufacturer acknowledges hardware failure can cause display problems. This is troubleshooting guidance rather than an owner failure report, so it is lower confidence.

Reported onmi.com
