Costly or dangerous1

Right earbud stops charging entirely after ~3.5 months of use

Serious

Found via Best Buy customer review (rated 2/5). Owner reports the right earbud progressively failed to charge and then stopped charging entirely within 3.5 months of purchase, with no fix from re-seating or re-pairing.

What to checkIf buying, verify the charging behavior of both earbuds within the return window. If the right earbud fails to charge, this is a documented hardware failure, not user error.

Worth checking1

Right earbud randomly turns off and frequent re-pairing required

To check

Found via Best Buy customer review (rated 2/5, owned 3 months). Owner reports the right earbud randomly powers off or fails to activate when removed from the case, and the buds require frequent re-pairing with phone and laptop.

What to checkExpect possible intermittent right-bud dropouts and re-pairing hassle. This is a reliability issue that may require warranty support.

Minor2

Semi-open design leaks more ambient noise than competitors' flagship ANC earbuds

Minor

Found via Norwegian professional review (Lyd & Bilde). The reviewer notes the Elite 10 Gen 2's semi-open, ventilated design lets more ambient sound through than the top ANC models from Sony, Sennheiser, Technics, and Bose — a design trade-off for comfort.

What to checkIf maximum noise isolation is critical, this model's semi-open design will leak more ambient sound than competitors' sealed designs. Comfort comes at the cost of ANC effectiveness.

Reported onlydogbilde.no

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Software bug: bass automatically reduced in very quiet environments; no fix expected

Minor

Found via Galaxus customer Q&A. An owner reports a software bug where bass is automatically reduced in very quiet environments, noticeably changing sound in spaces like elevators. The owner notes that since Jabra discontinued the product line, a fix is unlikely.

What to checkIf you listen in very quiet environments, expect the sound signature to change (bass drop). This is a firmware behavior unlikely to be patched given Jabra's exit from the earbud market.

Reported ongalaxus.at
