iPad 10. Nesil charging port contact failure — warranty claim denied

To check

nesil şikayet sorun şarj'. A Turkish owner reports intermittent charging on an iPad 10 under warranty; Apple's authorized service said the charging port had contact failure due to frame deformation and denied warranty coverage, quoting 15,300 TL for a replacement unit.

What to checkIntermittent charging on an iPad 10 can be diagnosed by Apple as frame damage causing charging-port contact failure, and warranty coverage may be denied. If you buy one, check the frame/port area early; a charging fault may not be covered if any deformation is found.

iPad 10th gen battery does not last a day and needs daily charging

To check

An eBay buyer review of the iPad 10th gen reports the battery does not last a full day and requires daily charging.

What to checkOwners report the battery drains quickly enough to need daily charging; a buyer should check battery health and expect that a heavily used unit may not last a full day.

iPad 10 heats up in the right corner during app updates

Minor

On the Russian forum 4pda, a user reports the iPad 10 heats up in the right corner (where the processor and board sit) during app updates, and asks about weak battery life.

What to checkA forum user reports noticeable heating in the right corner of the iPad 10 during app updates, consistent with the processor/board location. This is a minor comfort concern rather than a failure, but combined with battery complaints it may indicate thermal/power behaviour worth watching.

Reported on4pda.to
