CPSC recall 16-211: Swagway self-balancing scooters/hoverboards battery pack can overheat, smoke, catch fire or explode

SeriousSafety risk

This is the official CPSC recall (16-211) of Swagway self-balancing scooters/hoverboards, 267,000 units, with 42 reported incidents of battery packs smoking/catching fire/exploding and 16 injuries. Caveat: the companion 10-firm recall table identifies the recalled Swagway model as 'Swagway X1', so this official recall covers the X1, not the X2.

What to checkIf considering a Swagway X1, this is a documented, officially recalled fire hazard — do not buy one that was not remedied. For the X2, this recall does not directly name it, so treat it as context, not proof about the X2.

Reported oncpsc.gov

Swagway hoverboards reported catching fire; Amazon and Target stopped selling Swagway; class action alleges fire

SeriousSafety risk

Local news (WSBT 22, South Bend) reporting multiple Swagway hoverboard fire claims, retailers dropping the brand, and a class action alleging a Swagway Hoverboard caught fire. This is brand-level, not X2-specific.

What to checkEstablishes that Swagway hoverboards as a brand have documented fire incidents and were pulled by major retailers; it does not single out the X2 model.

Reported onwsbt.com

Worth checking1

Swagway hoverboard ignited while charging in Chappaqua, NY home

To check

A campus fire-safety alert documenting a Swagway-brand hoverboard that ignited while charging in a home. Brand-level, not X2-specific.

What to checkReinforces the charging-related fire risk for Swagway hoverboards; the alert's own advice is to never charge unattended and to verify a UL listing label.

Reported oncampusfiresafety.org

Swagway X2 owner reports: unit quit working out of nowhere; unit spun and threw rider; one unit vibrates

Minor

X2-specific owner reports of the board quitting, coming apart, one unit spinning and throwing a rider, and another vibrating. These are quality/function complaints, not documented fires.

What to checkThis is the only X2-specific evidence found; it points to reliability/quality issues and poor customer-service response, not a documented battery fire specific to the X2.

Reported onpissedconsumer.com

iFixit teardown: Swagway battery protection board has skimpy solder joints on charge/discharge MOSFETs

Minor

Independent teardown found the only notable defect was thin solder joints on the battery protection board's charge/discharge MOSFETs, which carry high power. This is a general Swagway teardown, not X2-specific.

What to checkA manufacturing-quality concern tied to the battery circuit that could relate to overheating; it is a single teardown observation, not a documented failure.

Reported oncnet.com
