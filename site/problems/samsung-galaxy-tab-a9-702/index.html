Worth checking3

Charging board (Ladeplatine) replacement needed for slow/unreliable charging

To check

Repair-record source confirming the charging port is a known replaceable failure point on SM-X210.

What to checkThe USB charging port is a documented weak point — slow charging, intermittent connection, and a loose cable point to the charging board. It is replaceable but the repair is not trivial (needs heat, new adhesive).

Reported onde.ifixit.com

Display started freezing within 18 months of purchase

To check

Single owner report; Samsung's response routed to service center.

What to checkOne owner reports the display freezing within 18 months despite light use. This is a single report, so treat it as a risk to verify rather than a widespread fault.

Reported onsamsung.com

Two-finger touch stops registering during gaming (multi-touch failure)

To check

A second UK review on the same product line also noted touch sensing problems holding a finger on screen blocks other touches.

What to checkIf you game or use two-finger gestures, the touchscreen drops touches when a second finger is held down — Samsung's own support verified it. Not a fixable software toggle per the reviewer.

Reported onsamsung.com
