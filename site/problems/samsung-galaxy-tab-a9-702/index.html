Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+: known problems

3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Worth checking

Charging board (Ladeplatine) replacement needed for slow/unreliable charging

Repair-record source confirming the charging port is a known replaceable failure point on SM-X210.

What to check. The USB charging port is a documented weak point — slow charging, intermittent connection, and a loose cable point to the charging board. It is replaceable but the repair is not trivial (needs heat, new adhesive).

Reported on: de.ifixit.com

Display started freezing within 18 months of purchase

Single owner report; Samsung's response routed to service center.

What to check. One owner reports the display freezing within 18 months despite light use. This is a single report, so treat it as a risk to verify rather than a widespread fault.

Reported on: samsung.com

Two-finger touch stops registering during gaming (multi-touch failure)

A second UK review on the same product line also noted touch sensing problems holding a finger on screen blocks other touches.

What to check. If you game or use two-finger gestures, the touchscreen drops touches when a second finger is held down — Samsung's own support verified it. Not a fixable software toggle per the reviewer.

Reported on: samsung.com
