ZenBook 13 OLED: known problems

3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Motherboard failure — laptop stopped suddenly and will not power on (UM325UA)

A repair-forum user reports their UM325UA-KG501TS stopped suddenly while in use and will not power on, with no LED when the charger is plugged in. The user suspects a short circuit on the motherboard. This is a single owner report on a repair forum, so it is anecdotal but points to a possible sudden-death failure mode.

What to check. If the machine dies suddenly with no power LED, this may be a motherboard-level failure requiring board repair or replacement; check warranty coverage before paying for out-of-warranty repair.

Reported on: diy-laptoprepair.com

Worth checking

Brightness bug after screen lock — firmware bug suspected on UM325SA

A German owner of a UM325SA reports that after locking the screen, the display reports maximum brightness but is visibly dimmer; lowering brightness by one step makes it brighter again. A forum respondent calls it a suspected Asus firmware bug.

What to check. If you see this, try updating firmware/drivers first; if it persists, it is a known firmware-level quirk, not a hardware failure — but verify with Asus support before assuming it will be fixed.

Reported on: computerbase.de

Minor

OLED screen flickering at low brightness

TechSpot's review aggregation lists OLED flickering at low brightness as a reviewer complaint for the UM325S. This is a secondary/aggregated source, lower weight than owner forums, but it corroborates a display-level quirk.

What to check. If you are sensitive to flicker, keep brightness above the low end or check for driver updates; this is a known OLED characteristic rather than a defect unique to this unit.

Reported on: techspot.com
