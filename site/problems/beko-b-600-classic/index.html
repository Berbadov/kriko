Beko B 600 Classic: known problems

Costly or dangerous

Dishes come out dirty despite four service visits (Beko b600 b)

A Beko b600 b owner reports dishes and the tub interior come out dirty/uncleaned; after four authorized service visits (salt setting, software update, glass-wash test, water test) the service blamed the water and refused a replacement. This is the most consequential failure reported on this model — cleaning performance not resolved by service.

What to check. There are reports of this model leaving dishes visibly dirty, with the authorized service unable to resolve it after multiple visits and refusing replacement; budget for the possibility of a persistent cleaning defect.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Worth checking

Strong plastic smell on every wash cycle (B600B Classic 4 programlı)

A B600B Classic 4-program owner reports a strong, unpleasant plastic smell on every wash from day one, persisting after repeated use; Beko support said it was normal and would fade, but it did not. This is a comfort/health concern specific to this model's materials.

What to check. If you are sensitive to chemical smells, know this model has been reported to emit a strong plastic odor that did not fade with use, and that Beko may treat it as normal.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Upper spray arm drips water onto clean dishes after the cycle ends (B 600 b Classic)

A new B 600 b Classic owner reports that after the door auto-opens at cycle end, water drips from the upper spray arm onto the lower basket and re-wets clean dishes; the authorized service called it normal for this series. It is a recurring, user-visible flaw on this exact model, though the owner could not get a replacement.

What to check. Expect water to drip from the upper arm and re-wet the lower load after the cycle, and know the authorized service may dismiss it as normal for this series rather than fix or replace it.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Minor

Retail reviews mostly positive (4.8/5) but some note noise and no child lock

The Hepsiburada retail review summary for the B 600 bClassic shows a 4.8/5 average across 63 reviews with praise for cleaning and value, but notes some users say it is not quiet and has no child lock. This gives balance: the model is generally well rated, with the specific complaints above being minority reports.

What to check. Overall retail sentiment is positive (4.8/5), so weigh the specific complaints against a generally well-reviewed, good-value model; the main recurring negatives are noise and the lack of a child lock.

Reported on: hepsiburada.com
