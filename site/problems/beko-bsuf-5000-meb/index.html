Costly or dangerous1

Heavy smoke from the rear of the oven during cooking; owner demanded refund on safety grounds

Serious

An owner on Şikayetvar (complaint 'Mini Fırında Aşırı Duman Ve Güvenlik Riski, İade Ve Ücret Geri Ödemesi Talebi', 28 March) reports thick smoke pouring from the rear of the oven during cooking, filling the kitchen, and asked for a refund citing both safety and health concerns.

What to checkA reported heavy-smoke event during normal cooking is a fire and inhalation hazard. If you own or buy this oven, run the first use in a ventilated space and watch the rear of the unit; if dense smoke appears, stop use and pursue a refund or exchange rather than accepting a repair.

Worth checking1

Oven-door glass cracked while cooking; service charged for the part

To check

An owner on Şikayetvar (complaint 'Mini Fırın Camı Çatladı, Servis Ücreti İsteniyor, Çözüm Yok', 12 July 2025) reports the oven-door glass cracked while cooking, and that Beko's service asked to be paid for the service and part before even visiting.

What to checkIf you buy this oven, check the door glass early and document any crack immediately; the owner's report indicates Beko treated the cracked glass as a chargeable repair rather than a warranty failure, so a glass failure could cost you the door assembly out of pocket.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

Interior enamel coating flaking/bubbling after about four months of use

Minor

An owner (complaint 'Garanti Kapsamındaki Beko Fırın Emaye Çatlaması İçin Ücretsiz Onarım İsteği', 25 March) reports the interior enamel flaking/bubbling in the lower part of the oven after about four months, while still under warranty.

What to checkThis is a quality/reliability defect rather than a safety hazard, but it means the interior finish can degrade within months. If you buy this oven, inspect the interior enamel early and file any flaking claim while the warranty is still active, since the owner's report shows it appears within the first year.

Reported onsikayetvar.com
