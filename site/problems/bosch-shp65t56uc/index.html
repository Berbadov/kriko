Degraded o-rings on middle-drawer spray arm supply tube cause leaks

To check

Owner of a SHP65T56UC/01 reports the beige o-rings at the middle-drawer spray arm connection are badly degraded; a repair tech confirms they are not sold separately and the spray arm supply tube (11018097) is the replacement.

What to checkIf you see leaks at the back of the middle drawer, the o-rings cannot be bought alone; budget for the spray arm supply tube (11018097).

Reported onforum.appliancepartspros.com

Dishwasher stops randomly with no error code - door switch or control board

To check

A repair blog documents a SHP65T56UC that stops mid-cycle with no error code; the cause is the door switch or control board, and parts were under $100 versus a $350 shop quote.

What to checkIf the unit stops randomly with no code, suspect the door switch/control board; it is a DIY-able ~$100 parts fix.

Reported ontitam.ca

E-18 / E-08 low-water-level errors persist after inlet valve and drain filter replacement

To check

The owner replaced the water inlet valve and drain filter and the constant beeping with E-18/E-08 codes continued, so the low-water-fill diagnosis is not a simple valve swap.

What to checkA constant-beep/E-18 unit may not be fixed by replacing the inlet valve; check actual water fill level and the inlet/outlet plastic assembly before spending.

Reported onforum.appliancepartspros.com

Minor1

Spray arm plug into water source wears out and is difficult to engage

Minor

An owner repair story on PartSelect reports the spray arm plug into the water source wore out and became hard to engage, requiring spray arm replacement.

What to checkA worn spray arm plug is a straightforward, low-cost replacement that resolves engagement/leak issues at the drawer connection.

Reported onpartselect.com
