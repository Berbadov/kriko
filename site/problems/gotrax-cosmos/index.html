Worth checking

General Gotrax battery issues documented but not Cosmos-specific

Troubleshooting guides document common Gotrax battery failures including BMS cutoffs and charging issues. These are brand-wide patterns, not specific to the Cosmos hoverboard.

What to check. If buying a Gotrax Cosmos, be aware that Gotrax batteries have documented BMS/charging failure patterns across the brand, though no Cosmos-specific incidents were found.

Reported on: facebook.com

Owner reports Gotrax battery problem and poor customer service on replacement unit

An owner reports a replacement Gotrax unit with battery failure and unhelpful customer service. This is a scooter review, not the Cosmos hoverboard specifically, but reflects brand-wide reliability and support concerns.

What to check. Gotrax customer service and battery reliability have documented complaints across products; factor this into purchase decisions even though no Cosmos-specific failure was found.

Reported on: sport.woot.com

Minor

Gotrax Cosmos manufacturer page confirms UL2272 certification and 144Wh battery

Identifies the product as the Gotrax Cosmos with a 144Wh battery and manufacturer-claimed UL2272 certification. This establishes product identity but is a marketing claim, not independent verification.

What to check. UL2272 certification is a meaningful safety standard, but verify it independently rather than relying solely on the manufacturer's page.

Reported on: gotraxpro.com

No CPSC recall found for Gotrax Cosmos; 2016 recalls cover other hoverboard brands

CPSC recall records for hoverboards date from 2016 and cover other brands (Hovertrax, Swagway). No Gotrax Cosmos recall appears in CPSC-related results.

What to check. The absence of a recall is not proof of safety, but it means no official recall action has been taken for this product.

Reported on: safe.cpb.or.kr
