No CPSC recall found for Gotrax Cosmos; 2016 recalls cover other hoverboard brands

Minor

CPSC recall records for hoverboards date from 2016 and cover other brands (Hovertrax, Swagway). No Gotrax Cosmos recall appears in CPSC-related results.

What to checkThe absence of a recall is not proof of safety, but it means no official recall action has been taken for this product.
