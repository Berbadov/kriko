Costly or dangerous

MatePad SE 11 Bluetooth completely non-functional (error on connect, pairs with nothing)

A buyer reports the Bluetooth never worked: it throws an error on connect and pairs with no device, leading to a return/refund dispute.

What to check. If Bluetooth is essential (keyboard, audio, stylus), test it immediately on delivery; a dead Bluetooth unit may require a return fight.

Reported on: technodom.kz

MatePad SE 11 recurring screen fault (lines, then black screen) plus charging-socket failure, twice through service

A Turkish owner reports the screen developed a line then went black/refused to power on, and the charging socket failed; after two service visits the fault was not permanently fixed.

What to check. Buyer should verify warranty/service terms before purchase; screen and charging faults are the most serious reported failure and may require repeat service visits.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Worth checking

MatePad SE 11 runs HarmonyOS 2.0 (Android 10 base) with no Google services and Bluetooth restrictions

A buyer reports HarmonyOS 2.0 is an Android 10 base with no Google services, Bluetooth restrictions, and other system limitations.

What to check. If the buyer relies on Google Play apps, they must sideload APKs or use a GBox workaround; some apps will not work.

Reported on: dns-shop.ge

Minor

MatePad SE 11 battery part: HB27G3B1EGW-12, 7600 mAh, AGS6 family

Identifies the replacement battery part number and the AGS6 model family for the SE 11; the seller lists it for fast discharge, unexpected shutdowns and irregular charging.

What to check. If the battery fails, this is the part number to search for; the AGS6 code confirms the SE 11 model identity.

Reported on: spainsellers.com
