Costly or dangerous

Redmi Pad freezes with 'system not responding' popups for up to 8 minutes

An owner reports the tablet repeatedly freezes with 'system not responding'/'app not responding' popups, sometimes for up to 8 minutes, and calls it unusable.

What to check. Expect the 4GB/entry config to stutter and freeze under normal use; budget for a higher-RAM variant or a different tablet if smooth multitasking matters.

Reported on: dns-shop.kg

Redmi Pad mainboard died after 1.5 years in endless reboot loop; reballing did not fix it

An owner reports the tablet died after ~1.5 years in an endless reboot loop that ignores the power button; a shop reballed the CPU and it failed again, then the shop declared it a brick/factory defect.

What to check. A mainboard failure is a total-loss event with no cheap fix; if you buy one, keep it within warranty and back up data, as the failure can appear after a year or more.

Reported on: dns-shop.kg

Worth checking

Redmi Pad Helio G99 + 4GB RAM lags out of the box

An owner says the G99/4GB config lags badly even out of the box, though it remains usable for light reading and undemanding games.

What to check. The 4GB RAM variant is the weak point; choose the 6GB/8GB version if you want tolerable responsiveness.

Reported on: dns-shop.kg

Redmi Pad battery drains suspiciously fast, losing 1% within a minute of a full charge

An owner reports the battery drains suspiciously fast, losing 1% within a minute of a full charge.

What to check. Battery life can be inconsistent between units; a fast-draining unit may need a battery replacement, so check battery health early in the return window.

Reported on: market.yandex.ru

Minor

Redmi Pad lags once charge drops below 20%

An owner reports the tablet starts to lag once the charge drops below 20%.

What to check. Keep the tablet charged above ~20% to avoid sluggish behaviour; this is a nuisance rather than a defect.

Reported on: market.yandex.ru
