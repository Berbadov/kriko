MAXTEMP E1 error on Ender 3 hotend after hotend swap

Serious

An owner reports MAXTEMP E1 printer-halted at 200 °C on an Ender 3 with a new hotend; the reply identifies a shorted/open temp sensor or thermal runaway as the cause.

What to checkA MAXTEMP E1 halt on the Ender 3 usually means the thermistor circuit is faulty (short or open) — verify the thermistor reading and wiring before assuming the board is dead.

Reported onlinustechtips.com

Mainboard fails after about 10 models on Ender 3

Serious

A verified buyer review on the Austrian retailer Alza reports the Ender 3's mainboard probably failed after printing only about 10 models.

What to checkA small share of owners report the stock mainboard dying early (within a handful of prints); this is a low-probability but high-cost failure that is hard to predict before it happens.

Flashing custom firmware bricks Ender 3 S1 to splash screen

To check

On the Ender 3 S1 (STM32F4 SoC), flashing a custom firmware build left the printer stuck on the Creality splash screen; recovery required downgrading to an older Creality build first. This is a variant-specific brick path, not the original Ender 3's 8-bit board.

What to checkOn Ender 3 S1/V2-style STM32F4 boards, a failed custom-firmware flash can leave the machine bricked on the splash screen; keep the official firmware image on an SD card to recover before re-flashing.

Reported ongithub.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Stock Bowden extruder motor skips and clogs on Ender 3

To check

Polymaker's wiki describes the stock original Ender 3's non-geared Bowden extruder as prone to motor skips (clicking) and under-extrusion because the stepper lacks torque; the plastic extruder also cannot have its tension adjusted.

What to checkExpect extruder clicking and clogs on the stock Ender 3 extruder; a geared or direct-drive extruder upgrade is the standard fix, and print speeds should be kept low (about 40 mm/s with a 0.4 mm nozzle).

Reported onwiki.polymaker.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Stock plastic extruder lever breaks on Ender 3

To check

A verified buyer reports the stock plastic extruder lever broke after ~1700 hours of printing, and that the metal replacement lever over-tightened the filament, so the replacement is not a drop-in fix.

What to checkThe original Ender 3's plastic extruder lever is a known wear part that cracks over time; if replacing it, the metal lever needs tension adjustment or it will grind the filament.

Reported onm.alza.at

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Weak bed springs force frequent re-leveling on Ender 3

Minor

A verified buyer notes the print bed periodically needs re-calibration because of poor spring quality — a first-layer calibration failure mode the pack tracks.

What to checkBudget for better bed springs or a leveling upgrade; the stock springs let the bed drift out of level, causing first-layer failures that are mistaken for bigger problems.

Reported onm.alza.at
