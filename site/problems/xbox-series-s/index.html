Microsoft global replacement program for Xbox Series X/S AC power cords (model 1948-000001) due to overheating

Serious

Reports a voluntary global replacement program for Series X/S AC power cords (model 1948-000001) that can overheat at the C13 connector, with surface temperatures exceeding safety thresholds.

What to checkThis is the most consequential finding: affected power cords can overheat or melt at the plug, and owners in the EU market should check their cord's model number (1948-000001) and use Microsoft's replacement program rather than keep using the cord.

Reported onthegadgetdigest.com

Xbox Series S internal SSD failure leaves console stuck at dashboard and E109 error after cloning

Serious

A repair thread describes a Series S that boots games to a blackscreen and returns to the dashboard; the suspected cause is a failing SSD, and a self-repair clone produced an E109 error because the drive key did not transfer.

What to checkA failing internal SSD makes the console drop back to the dashboard when launching games; DIY cloning is risky (E109 error from the missing key), so this is an expensive repair that most buyers will not attempt.

Reported onzh.ifixit.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking1

Xbox Series S left analog stick drift reported by owners

To check

An owner reports severe left-stick drift on a Series S controller that cleaning with alcohol did not fix.

What to checkStick drift is a common owner-reported fault on the Series S controller; the poster's cleaning attempt failed, so a buyer should budget for a replacement controller (roughly €50-60) rather than expect a cheap fix.

Reported onelotrolado.net
