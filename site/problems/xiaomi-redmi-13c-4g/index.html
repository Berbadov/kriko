Costly or dangerous

Turkish owner: new Redmi 13C charges slowly and overheats

A Turkish owner on iFixit reports a new, out-of-the-box Redmi 13C that dropped from a 30-minute charge to a 2-hour charge and overheats severely within a few weeks of purchase.

What to check. A Turkish-market buyer should watch for slow charging and overheating early in the device's life; this owner reports paying repair shops repeatedly without a fix, so a warranty claim early on matters.

Reported on: tr.ifixit.com

Worth checking

Redmi 13C freezes and crashes with games

An iFixit owner describes the Redmi 13C freezing often, failing to hold internet, and crashing during games.

What to check. Expect general lag and app crashes under load; the suggested fix path is safe mode to isolate third-party apps before blaming hardware.

Reported on: it.ifixit.com

Redmi 13C shuts down when unplugged during charging (battery degraded)

Official Xiaomi support FAQ for the Redmi 13C lists a degraded/low battery as a cause of the device shutting down the moment it is unplugged from charging.

What to check. If the phone dies right after being unplugged, the battery is the first suspect; Xiaomi recommends an authorized service center check/replacement rather than a DIY fix.

Reported on: mi.com

Redmi 13C stuck in bootloop after flashing an official ROM

An XDA owner thread documents the Redmi 13C (gale) entering a persistent bootloop after a failed official-ROM flash, with the device then locked and hard to recover.

What to check. Flashing or updating this model carries a real risk of a bootloop if the bootloader state is wrong; a buyer should not attempt unofficial flashing without confirming the device is unlocked.

Reported on: xdaforums.com

Minor

Redmi 13C camera app freezes

Official Xiaomi support acknowledges the Redmi 13C camera app freezing, with a note that if troubleshooting fails there could be a hardware issue.

What to check. Camera freezes are common enough that Xiaomi ships a dedicated FAQ; most are fixed by cache clears and updates, but persistent freezes point to hardware and a service visit.

Reported on: mi.com
