Costly or dangerous

Ninja Foodi DualZone fan failed after 6 months, causing loud noise and improper air frying

An owner of the Ninja Foodi 7-in-1 DualZone (same DualZone family, different model code) reports the fan failed after 6 months, producing a very loud noise and making the unit unable to air fry properly. This is a known failure mode in the DualZone family and should be watched for on the R-AD300CO.

What to check. If the unit suddenly becomes very loud or stops circulating air properly, the fan motor has likely failed; seek warranty service immediately rather than continuing to use it.

Reported on: sellout.woot.com

R-AD300CO stopped working within a year

An owner reports the unit failed completely in under a year despite being loved while it worked. This is a total-loss failure within the typical warranty window, meaning the buyer should verify warranty coverage and keep proof of purchase.

What to check. Confirm the warranty term (typically 1–2 years) and register the product at purchase; keep the receipt in case of early total failure.

Reported on: sellout.woot.com

Worth checking

R-AD300CO left basket arrived damaged with burnt-rubber smell

A verified Amazon purchaser reports the left side arrived damaged and emits a strong burnt-rubber smell. This suggests possible QC issues in the left chamber specifically, and a smell that may indicate overheating insulation or wiring.

What to check. Inspect both baskets and the left chamber immediately on delivery; a burnt-rubber smell should trigger an immediate return, not a wait-and-see.

Reported on: sellout.woot.com

R-AD300CO produces heavy smoke within first uses

A verified Amazon purchaser reports heavy smoke after only a couple of uses. The HomeAndTome long-term review notes this may be factory residue burning off, but the owner was considering a return, so the buyer should expect smoke on early runs and decide whether that is acceptable.

What to check. Run several empty preheat cycles in a ventilated area before first real use; if smoke persists beyond the first few cycles, treat it as a defect and return.

Reported on: sellout.woot.com

Minor

EU-market Ninja Foodi MAX Dual Zone (AF400EUCP) emitted strong chemical smell on first heating

A German Amazon reviewer of the EU-market AF400EUCP (same Foodi Dual Zone family) reports a strong chemical smell on first heating that took about five 20-minute cycles to dissipate and ruined the first batch of food. This is relevant to EU buyers of the same product family, though the exact model code differs from R-AD300CO.

What to check. EU buyers should plan for several empty preheat cycles with windows open before first use, and discard the first cooked batch if the chemical smell persists.

Reported on: amazon.de

R-AD300CO emits high-pitched noise when powered off but plugged in

An owner reports a persistent high-pitched noise even when the unit is off but still plugged in. This indicates standby power draw and possibly a failing transformer or capacitor; it is a nuisance rather than a functional failure.

What to check. Unplug the unit when not in use to eliminate the noise and standby power draw; if the noise becomes louder or the unit fails, it points to a power-supply issue.

Reported on: sellout.woot.com
