Costly or dangerous

K1C 2025 repeatedly malfunctioned — three service visits, returned still faulty

Customer review on Alza.cz for the K1C 2025, from seed 'Creality K1C reliability forum'. A verified buyer reports the printer repeatedly malfunctioned from purchase, needed three service visits, and kept coming back faulty.

What to check. A verified owner reports a K1C 2025 that never worked reliably despite three service visits. Reliability can be a lottery; if you buy one, buy from a seller with a solid return/warranty path and test it immediately.

Reported on: m.alza.cz

Worth checking

K1C X-axis graphite sleeve wears into black sticky residue causing layer lines and lost steps

Creality official maintenance guide for the K1C, from seed 'Creality K1C common problems'. Creality states the X-axis graphite copper sleeve builds a black sticky substance over time, increasing drag and causing layer lines, misalignment, and lost steps.

What to check. This is a wear item the manufacturer says needs cleaning every ~300 print hours. If you buy a K1C, plan for regular X-axis rod cleaning and lubrication, or you may see layer lines and skipped steps as the machine ages.

Reported on: wiki.creality.com

K1C extruder jams at filament entry on a new unit — replacement extruder needed

Owner forum thread (lesimprimantes3d.fr), from seed 'Creality K1C extruder clog'. A new K1C could not feed the supplied PLA filament, which blocked at the extruder entry; the owner ended up getting a replacement extruder from the seller.

What to check. Filament can jam at the extruder entry even on a new machine, and the fix can mean a full extruder swap. Budget for the possibility of an early extruder failure and check the PTFE tube and lever before assuming it is a slicer issue.

Reported on: lesimprimantes3d.fr

K1C nozzle clogs after printing ABS — filament blocked in hotend/Teflon pipe

Creality official troubleshooting page for the K1 series, from seed 'Creality K1C extruder clog'. Creality documents that after printing ABS, switching filament can fail because the filament is blocked in the extrusion kit, the Teflon pipe, or the nozzle throat, requiring disassembly to clear.

What to check. If you print ABS on a K1C, expect hotend clogging when switching materials; the fix involves disassembling the extrusion kit and possibly replacing the blue Teflon tube. Factor in this maintenance when choosing materials.

Reported on: wiki.creality.com

Minor

K1C filament end detector sits 30cm from nozzle — runout leaves filament stuck in tube

Customer review on Alza.sk for the K1C, from seed 'Creality K1C reliability forum'. A long-term owner describes the filament end detector being 30cm from the nozzle, making runout removal require disassembling the tube, and reports the failed-print detection never worked.

What to check. The runout sensor placement can make filament changes awkward (disassembling the tube), and failed-print detection reportedly did not work for this owner. Minor but worth knowing if you rely on unattended printing.

Reported on: m.alza.sk
