Continuous vibration drains battery then unit fails at ~3 months (battery)

Serious

Owner review on Amazon.co.jp (query 'Xiaomi Smart Band 11 Active pil sorunu'): at 3 months the vibration ran continuously, the battery drained rapidly, then the band lost connectivity. Warranty reportedly not honored.

What to checkA runaway vibration motor that drains the battery and kills the unit is a reported failure mode; buy from a channel where warranty is enforceable.

Reported onamazon.co.jp

Screen goes black and unit stops charging at ~4 months (display/early failure)

Serious

Owner review on Amazon.co.jp (from query 'Xiaomi Smart Band 11 Active common problems'): the screen went black at 4 months and the band would no longer charge or pair. This is an early hardware/display death, not a settings issue.

What to checkExpect a meaningful chance the display dies within the first year; keep the receipt and check warranty coverage before relying on this band long-term.

Reported onamazon.co.jp

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Touch panel stops responding, alarm cannot be dismissed (display/input)

Serious

Owner review on Amazon.co.jp (query 'Xiaomi Smart Band 11 Active common problems'): one of two units bought died within a year when the touch panel stopped responding, so an active alarm could not be dismissed.

What to checkA dead touch panel makes the band unusable and can trap an alarm; this is a hardware failure, not fixable by settings.

Reported onamazon.co.jp

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Battery life drops below 4 days when extra functions are used

To check

Official statement that heavy use cuts battery life below 4 days.

What to checkExpect to charge more than twice a week if you run many features; the 21-day figure only applies to the ultra-long battery mode.

Bluetooth drops frequently, requires re-pairing (connectivity)

To check

Korean marketplace review on danawa (query 'Xiaomi Smart Band 11 Active şikayetvar şikayet'): Bluetooth connection drops often and must be re-entered each time.

What to checkExpect to re-pair the band periodically; if notifications matter to you, verify pairing stability before relying on it.

Charging contacts oxidize; band fails to power on (battery/charging)

To check

Xiaomi's official FAQ (query 'Xiaomi Smart Band 11 Active pil sorunu') lists depleted battery and oxidized charging contacts as the primary reasons the band fails to power on, and warns power-bank charging may not reach full capacity.

What to checkKeep the charging contacts clean and avoid power-bank charging; if the band won't power on, cleaning the contacts is the first fix to try.

Reported onmi.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Included strap breaks quickly / rubber degrades (durability)

To check

Amazon.co.jp aggregate review summary (query 'Xiaomi Smart Band 11 Active common problems'): the included strap breaks quickly and the rubber degrades/tears with short use.

What to checkPlan to buy a spare strap; the stock strap is a reported weak point.
