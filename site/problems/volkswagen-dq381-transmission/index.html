Clutch debris contaminates mechatronics (DQ381)

Found with a diagnostic scan

The DQ381 transmission uses a single oil circuit, so clutch wear debris can circulate into the mechatronics (control unit). This debris accelerates valve wear, leading to expensive repairs. Frequent oil changes are required to delay or prevent this damage.

What to check. Ask for proof of transmission oil changes at recommended intervals; test drive for jerky or delayed shifts; scan for mechatronics fault codes (e.g., P17xx).
