Arçelik A 710 B Prestige: known problems

3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Arçelik A 710 B — leaks water from underneath and does not wash properly

The replacement unit is identified as 'A710b'. It leaked water from underneath and washed poorly for about a month.

What to check. A buyer should check for water pooling under the machine early; combined with poor washing this forced the owner to hand-wash for a month while the warranty dispute dragged on.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Worth checking

Arçelik A 710 B — dishes come out dirty and wet even after the program finishes

The unit is identified as 'A710B'. From the first day the dishes came out both not fully clean and wet.

What to check. A buyer should not assume a full program means clean, dry dishes; this recurring complaint means the wash and dry performance should be verified early, and it is grounds for a return request.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Arçelik A 710 B — rust stains leaking from the salt compartment on a new unit

The unit is identified as 'a 710 b'. Rust leaking from the salt compartment appeared within days of a replacement unit arriving.

What to check. A buyer should watch the salt compartment area for rust staining early on; it appeared on a brand-new unit and signals a corrosion/water-seal problem rather than normal use.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com
