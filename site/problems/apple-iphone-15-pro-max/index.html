Random reboots traced to NAND storage fault on iPhone 15 Pro Max

Serious

An owner describes random reboots that escalate to failure to boot past the Apple logo; a repair answer points to NAND storage failure.

What to checkIf a 15 Pro Max starts randomly rebooting and then fails to boot, treat it as a hardware fault, not a software glitch. Back up data early, because a NAND failure can make data unrecoverable and the fix is board-level.

Total failure after ~18 months diagnosed as logic board defect

Serious

A German owner's device failed outright after ~18 months of light use; an Apple Authorized Service Provider diagnosed a logic board defect and quoted ~€700 for a board replacement, with data unrecoverable.

What to checkA sudden no-charge/dead state after 1–2 years can be a logic board failure, not the battery. The fix is a costly board swap and data is typically lost, so keep backups and be aware the repair can approach €700.

Reported onifone.de

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Turkish owner: freezing, faulty touchscreen, and self-restarts from first month

Serious

A Turkish owner of a 1 TB unit reports freezing, a faulty touchscreen from day one, self-restarts, and camera focus failure; the service report listed multiple faulty components and the device was returned with a new IMEI.

What to checkIn the Turkish market, some units show combined hardware faults (freezing, touchscreen, camera) within the first months. If you buy one, test touch, camera focus, and stability during the return window, and keep the service report.

Worth checking4

Battery drain on iPhone 15 Pro Max after iOS 27 update

To check

A news report on iOS 27 complaints quotes an iPhone 15 Pro Max owner reporting higher battery consumption after the update.

What to checkBattery drain on the 15 Pro Max is often tied to a recent iOS release rather than a worn battery. Before paying for a battery swap, wait for a point update and check battery usage in Settings.

Reported onenglish.mathrubhumi.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Green/pink line on iPhone 15 Pro Max display indicates damaged row driver

To check

A UK repair guide states a persistent green/pink line on the 15 Pro Max panel means a damaged row driver and requires a screen replacement.

What to checkA permanent green or pink line on the display is a panel fault, not a software glitch, and needs a screen replacement. If the line is intermittent, try a forced restart first, but a fixed line will not clear on its own.

Reported onphonerepair.co.uk

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Random restarts reproducible by tapping the back — loose hardware contact

To check

A German owner reports the phone restarting on its own and can reproduce a restart by tapping the back, pointing to an internal loose contact rather than software.

What to checkA device that restarts when tapped or knocked is a hardware contact problem, not an iOS bug. It tends to worsen over time and is not fixed by a software update.

Reported oncomputerbase.de

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

iOS 26 on iPhone 15 Pro/Pro Max: performance drop and battery drain (Turkey)

To check

A Turkish tech-news report on iOS 26 complaints lists performance drop, heavy visual effects, and battery consumption as the standout issues on the iPhone 15 Pro and Pro Max.

What to checkAfter a major iOS release, the 15 Pro Max can feel slower and drain battery faster. This is usually temporary software indexing; hold off on hardware repairs until a point update ships.
