Costly or dangerous

Fuse blew within a month; official service blamed grease ingress

Owner review on Onliner.by. The device failed within a month; the official service center said splashes/grease had entered the housing and tripped the fuse, which the owner calls a design flaw for a kitchen appliance.

What to check. A grease-seal failure that kills the fuse is a repair/return event within the first month. If you cook fatty foods, this is the most likely early electrical failure mode.

Reported on: catalog.onliner.by

Worth checking

Basket burst/cracked after two weeks of use

Owner review on DNS-shop listing 'burst after 2 weeks of use' as the only drawback. A structural failure of the basket in the first weeks.

What to check. A basket that cracks in two weeks is a warranty/return event. Check the basket's coating and seams early, before the return window closes.

Reported on: dns-shop.uz

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Manufacturer documents NTC sensor and AD sampling chip error codes

Xiaomi's own FAQ for this exact model lists NTC short-circuit/disconnected and AD sampling chip failure as error states that require after-sales service — confirming sensor and control-board faults are recognized failure modes.

What to check. These error codes mean a board/sensor fault, not user error; the unit will need service. A buyer should know a display error is a service trip, not a reset.

Reported on: mi.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor

Uneven temperature distribution across the stacked baskets

Les Numériques (French professional review) lists 'temperature homogeneity could be better' among the cons, alongside noise and no viewing window. A second owner on DNS-shop reported 'Готовит плохо' (cooks poorly) with uneven results.

What to check. Expect uneven browning between the two baskets; you may need to shake or swap baskets mid-cycle. Not a defect claim, but a performance compromise.

Reported on: lesnumeriques.com
