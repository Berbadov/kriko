Worth checking1

Japan recall of PowerCore 10000 also covers only A1263 variants, not A1246

To check

The Japanese Consumer Affairs Agency recall of PowerCore 10000 (410,000+ units) lists only A1263 variants (A1263N12, A1263016, A1263N22, A1263026, A1263036, A1263096). The A1246 is not among the recalled models.

What to checkConfirms across both US and Japan markets that the PowerCore 10000 recall targets A1263 models only. The A1246 PD Redux is not recalled in either market.

Reported onsmartwatchlife.jp

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor2

Anker PowerCore 10000 PD Redux A1246 is not part of the June 2025 CPSC recall (A1263 only)

Minor

The June 12, 2025 recall covers about 1,158,000 units of the plain PowerCore 10000, model A1263 only.

What to checkA buyer or owner of the A1246 PD Redux should not assume it is covered by this recall; the recall is limited to model A1263.

Reported oncpsc.gov

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

User reports PowerCore 10000 PD Redux 25W ran 6 hours without significant heat

Minor

An Amazon Japan customer review reports using the PowerCore 10000 PD Redux 25W (the A1246 family) continuously for 6 hours while powering a 12V fan, observing almost no heat from the cable, with the power bank itself being the warmer component.

What to checkSingle-user anecdote, not a systematic test. It suggests normal thermal behavior for this model under continuous moderate load, but does not rule out long-term degradation or manufacturing defects.

Reported onamazon.co.jp

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27
