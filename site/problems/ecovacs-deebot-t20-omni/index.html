T20 Omni stops drawing water from clean-water tank within the first year; recurring failure and weak service in Turkey

Serious

A verified buyer reports the robot stopped drawing water from its clean-water tank before the first year was up, the fault recurred after service, and the unit failed twice within warranty with inadequate service support.

What to checkIn Turkey, confirm the current authorized service provider and warranty terms before buying; this owner found the service provider unclear, waited months, and the fault returned. The water-drawing failure is the recurring complaint tied to this model.

Reported onhepsiburada.com

T20 Omni water-delivery pump fails; mop pads stop getting wet despite full clean-water tank

Serious

Owners report the mop pads stop getting wet even with a full clean-water tank; the fix is replacing the pump behind the station, which several report recurring (one owner twice) and requiring sending the unit to service.

What to checkBefore buying, check whether the water-delivery pump is covered by warranty and how long service takes; owners report roughly a month without the robot and recurring pump failures. A full reset and cleaning the strainer/filter sometimes helps, but the pump replacement is the documented repair.

Reported onsav.darty.com

Worth checking1

T20 Omni stops after wetting its mop pads and does not resume the task after returning to base in mopping mode

To check

In mopping mode the robot wets its pads then stops, and after returning to the base to clean the pads it declares the task finished instead of resuming; a factory reset did not fix it.

What to checkExpect that mopping runs may need manual restarts or per-zone selection; owners report a factory reset does not resolve it. Suction-only mode works, so this is specific to the mopping task logic.

Reported onsav.darty.com
