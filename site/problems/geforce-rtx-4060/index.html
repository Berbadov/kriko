Owner reports ASUS Dual RTX 4060 drawing ~52W at idle

To check

Independent owner report corroborates Igor'sLAB's ~52W idle measurement on a different AIB card (ASUS Dual EVO OC).

What to checkConfirms the high-idle-draw behavior is not limited to one brand; a shopper should not be alarmed if their 4060 idles near 50W, but should know it contradicts NVIDIA's power-saving claims.

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

RTX 4060 draws ~52W at idle instead of the rated ~7W (high idle power draw)

To check

Professional review measured ~52W at idle on a Palit RTX 4060, versus ~7W NVIDIA advertises; reviewer attributes it to a driver issue and notes colleagues saw the same.

What to checkExpect many RTX 4060 units to idle near 50W rather than the advertised ~7W, which raises idle electricity cost. Verify your own unit's idle draw; a driver update or older driver may reduce it, but it is not a hardware failure.

Reported onigorslab.de

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

Galax RTX 4060 image distortion/lag appears only when NVIDIA driver is installed

Minor

Single owner report (Portuguese) of a Galax RTX 4060 showing lag and image distortion only after the NVIDIA driver is installed; one reply suspects a physical VRAM/BGA solder fault rather than the driver.

What to checkTreat as an isolated, contested report. If a 4060 shows artifacts only with the driver loaded, rule out a hardware defect (VRAM/solder) before blaming the driver; this is not evidence of a widespread driver bug.

Reported onclubedohardware.com.br

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27
