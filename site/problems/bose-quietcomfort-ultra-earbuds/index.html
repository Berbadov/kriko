Left earbud emits constant hissing/static noise

Serious

A Best Buy owner review reports a constant, ever-present hissing/static noise from the left earbud that could not be resolved, which the reviewer rated as a 2-star defect despite praising sound and ANC.

What to checkA persistent hiss/static in one earbud is a reported unit-level defect that the owner could not eliminate; the buyer may need a replacement unit, and should test both buds in a quiet room before relying on them.

Reported ongoogle.com.tw

Multiple owners report static, crackling and thumping noises plus connectivity faults

Serious

A detailed Amazon UK owner account describes connectivity failures within a week, loud thumping noises during movement, and static in the right earbud after three weeks; the replacement unit reportedly showed the same crackling and connectivity faults.

What to checkThis owner experienced faults on the original and replacement units, requiring multiple exchanges. The buyer should keep proof of purchase and be prepared for possible returns/replacements; the report also notes refunds were refused after the return window passed.

Reported onamazon.co.uk

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking2

Bose confirms crackling/static under ANC in loud environments is 'normal'

To check

Bose's own support bulletin states that crackling or distortion under noise cancellation in loud environments is expected behaviour of the ANC microphones and 'normal', not a defect.

What to checkIf the buyer uses these in loud settings (commutes, transit, construction), they should expect intermittent crackling/static from the ANC microphones. Bose will treat it as normal and not a warranty fault, so it cannot be fixed by replacement.

Reported onsupport.bose.com.au

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Unit broke quickly with connection problems and uneven volume between buds

To check

A Galaxus owner review reports the unit broke quickly, with constant connection problems and a volume imbalance between left and right buds, which the owner attributed to poor quality control and returned.

What to checkReliability complaints include connection drops and a left/right volume imbalance. The buyer should check both buds' volume and connection stability early in the return window, since these faults may appear shortly after purchase.

Reported ongalaxus.de
