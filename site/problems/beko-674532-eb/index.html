674532 EB shows error indicator on first plug-in after installation rest

Serious

Owner complaint on ŞikayetVar (query: 'Beko 674532 EB sorun arıza'). A new 674532 EB Kombi NF showed an error indicator immediately when first plugged in after the required 6-hour rest; Beko only logged the call, and the owner requested a return as a defective unit. This is a first-use electrical/control-board failure on this model.

What to checkTest the unit at first plug-in; if an error indicator appears immediately, document it and insist on an exchange under the defective-product rules rather than accepting a repair.

674532 EB loud noise 2 days after delivery, fixed by adjusting touching pipes

To check

Owner Gülşah reports loud noise starting 2 days after delivery, worsening over time; a later service found the noise was pipes touching and fixed it.

What to checkA loud new unit may be a simple pipe-contact issue that a service visit can fix, but it can take multiple visits to diagnose.

674532 EB runs loudly from day two; traced to refrigerant pipes touching

To check

Owner complaint on ŞikayetVar (query: 'Beko 674532 EB buzdolabı şikayet'). Loud operation began 2 days after delivery, escalated from 10-20 min to 60 min of noise, and was initially dismissed as normal motor sound; a later service visit attributed it to refrigerant pipes touching and fixed it. Buyer should expect possible noise complaints and repeated service visits.

What to checkBefore buying, verify the unit is quiet on delivery and be ready to insist on a service visit that checks pipe contact, not just the motor.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

674532 EB main electronic control board is a real replaceable part (Beko 7281620217)

Minor

Parts catalog confirms the 674532 EB uses a main electronic board (Beko code 7281620217; board 5929763600) that controls compressor, fan, sensors and lighting; the same board is also listed on penguensogutma.com for this model.

What to checkIf the unit shows electrical faults, the control board is the concrete part a repairer will replace; it is a stocked, priced spare for this model.
