Constant black noise in right earbud in hear-through and ANC modes

Serious

Owner of a third pair of Jabra earbuds reports persistent black/static noise in the right earbud in both hear-through and ANC modes; firmware reinstall did not fix it and customer support was unhelpful. The reviewer notes this appears to be a common issue online.

What to checkIf you buy these, test ANC and hear-through modes on both earbuds within the return window; a defective unit may show static noise in one bud that firmware cannot fix.

Reported onamazon.com.au

Loud high-pitched ringing noise in both earbuds after ~4 months

Serious

Best Buy customer reports a loud high-pitched ringing noise from both earbuds starting about 4 months after purchase — beyond the return period. This is a hardware failure that appears after the return window closes.

What to checkThe failure emerged after the return period, so check warranty coverage (Jabra typically offers 2 years with registration) and test thoroughly early; a ringing noise at ~4 months means you may need a warranty claim.

Reported onbestbuy.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Residual hissing/static in treble range under ANC

Minor

CHIP.de's professional test notes that while ANC effectively reduces bass and midrange noise, a residual hissing/static (Rauschen) remains audible in the treble range. This is a professional measurement-based observation, not an owner complaint.

What to checkIf you are sensitive to high-frequency hiss, note that ANC does not fully eliminate treble noise; this is a characteristic of the ANC implementation rather than a defect.

Reported onchip.de
