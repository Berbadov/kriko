EU Safety Gate recall SR/02322/26: Xiaomi e-bike charger electric shock risk

SeriousSafety risk

This is an official EU Safety Gate recall for a Xiaomi e-bike/scooter battery charger with an electric shock hazard. Caveat: the recalled charger is 36V/42V, while the Himo T1 uses a 48V battery, so this specific recall may not cover the T1's charger. However, it shows a pattern of Xiaomi e-bike charger safety issues in the EU market.

What to checkCheck the voltage and model number of your T1 charger against this recall. If you have a 36V/42V Xiaomi e-bike charger, stop using it immediately. For the T1's 48V charger, verify it is not affected by any similar notice.

Reported onrecalleurope.com

Himo T1 battery gauge is inaccurate and causes repeated mid-ride power loss

Serious

The owner describes the battery gauge as severely inaccurate: the last bar frequently triggers power cutout, the bike loses propulsion while riding, and only brief re-engagement of the throttle restores a few meters of movement. This is a recurring pattern tied to the undervoltage protection issue and makes the bike unreliable and potentially dangerous in traffic.

What to checkTreat the battery gauge as unreliable; do not ride near what the gauge shows as empty. This is a known systemic issue, not a one-off defect.

Reported onpost.smzdm.com

Himo T1 early-batch instrument panel undervoltage threshold causes sudden power cutout while riding

Serious

A Chinese owner (smzdm) reports that early production batches of the T1 had the undervoltage protection threshold set too high in the instrument panel firmware, so when the throttle is twisted the battery's instantaneous voltage dips and the controller cuts all power mid-ride. The manufacturer's fix was a replacement instrument panel with a lower threshold. This is a motor-controller/battery-management defect that causes sudden loss of propulsion while riding, which is dangerous in traffic.

What to checkIf buying a used T1, ask whether the instrument panel was replaced under this issue; if riding one, expect sudden power cutouts when battery is not full. Budget for a replacement instrument panel if not already fitted.

Reported onpost.smzdm.com

Himo T1 battery capacity far below claimed range and degrades sharply in cold weather

To check

An owner measured 42km per battery in August versus the claimed 60km, and under 20km at -5°C. The battery degrades faster than expected and range collapses in winter. For a buyer this means the advertised range is not achievable in practice, especially in cold climates like Turkey's winters.

What to checkExpect roughly 70% of claimed range in warm weather and under half in winter. If range matters, consider the 28Ah variant or a different model.

Reported onpost.smzdm.com

Himo T1 frame and chain rust severely within months of purchase

To check

An owner reports widespread rust on screws and components, a fully rusted chain affecting the rear wheel bearing, a bent pedal crank, and a broken kickstand within about a year. This indicates poor corrosion protection and material quality, which shortens the bike's usable life and adds replacement costs.

What to checkExpect rust-related maintenance within the first year, especially in humid or wet climates. Budget for chain replacement and rust treatment.

Reported onpost.smzdm.com

Himo T1 owners report no functional after-sales support from HIMO/Xiaomi

To check

A Zhihu user reports a new unit that would not power on, no after-sales support, and being told to troubleshoot with electrical tools themselves. Another user reports being denied returns and told to self-inspect. This pattern suggests that if something goes wrong, the buyer may be left without recourse.

What to checkBefore buying, verify whether the seller in your market (TR/EU) provides actual warranty service. The manufacturer's own support appears unreliable based on owner reports.

Reported onzhihu.com

Himo T1 assembly defect: pedal crank misaligned, steering lock key stuck

Minor

A formal complaint on Sina's Black Cat platform describes a factory assembly defect (misaligned bottom bracket) causing the pedal crank to catch on the shell, plus a steering lock where the key cannot be removed. The seller blamed shipping and offered no repair. This indicates quality-control gaps at the factory.

What to checkInspect the bike thoroughly on delivery, especially pedal crank alignment and steering lock function. Document defects immediately for warranty claims.

Reported ontousu.sina.com.cn
