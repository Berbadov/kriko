Costly or dangerous

Himo C20 battery stops charging at 31.1V after long storage, shows E002

A 2020 C20 stored 3-4 years will not charge past 31.1V; the display then shows error E002.

What to check. A C20 battery left unused for years may fail to charge properly and trigger error E002; the pack and charger should be checked before relying on the bike.

Reported on: otvet.mail.ru

Worth checking

Himo C20 battery cells report unbalanced, dropping capacity after charge

An owner reports voltage sag from 40V to 35V under load; the reply diagnoses unbalanced cells that withhold full capacity.

What to check. If a C20 battery reads well below ~40V right after a full charge, the pack is likely unbalanced and will not deliver rated range; expect reduced capacity and possible early replacement.

Reported on: electrotransport.ru

Himo C20 battery lock disintegrates within three weeks of use

A buyer review reports the battery lock falls apart in the third week of use.

What to check. The battery lock is a common early-failure point; if it fails the battery is not held securely and may need a frame strap or replacement lock.

Reported on: reviews.yandex.ru

Himo C20 battery lock tab breaks, battery can pop out over curbs

A review site warns the battery lock tab breaks and the battery can eject when riding over curbs.

What to check. The removable battery's locking tab is fragile; if it breaks the battery can detach mid-ride. Owners patch it by strapping the battery to the frame.

Reported on: electropowerbikes.com
