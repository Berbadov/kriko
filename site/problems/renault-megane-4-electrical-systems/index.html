Costly or dangerous

Charging aborts and 'battery charging impossible' fault messages

Found with a diagnostic scan

Multiple reports indicate charging aborts and the error message 'battery charging impossible,' often occurring after OTA updates. This is frequently linked to heat pump defects or software issues.

What to check. Attempt to charge the vehicle and monitor for the 'battery charging impossible' error or an orange LED on the charging cable. Check for recent OTA updates.

Reported on: what-breaks.com

Chronic Throttle Body Valve/Electronic Gas Pedal Module Failures (2023 Renault Mégane E-Tech)

Found with a diagnostic scan

Multiple reports confirm persistent failures in the throttle body valve and electronic gas pedal module, leading to power loss and engine warning lights. This is not a one-off issue but a recurring problem affecting multiple owners, with some requiring full replacement of components at significant cost.

What to check. Check for error codes related to throttle position or gas pedal sensor failures. Inspect the gas pedal module and throttle body valve for physical damage, corrosion, or loose connections. Verify if the issue persists after cleaning or replacing the gas pedal connector (as seen in one case where a paslit connector resolved the issue).

Reported on: mycarly.com

DPF clogging in 1.5 dCi/1.9 dCi engines

Found with a diagnostic scan

Short urban trips prevent the DPF from reaching regeneration temperature, causing soot buildup, restricted exhaust flow, and limp-mode entry, leading to permanent performance degradation if unaddressed.

What to check. Check for DPF warning light activation, increased fuel consumption, and power loss during city driving. Inspect exhaust backpressure if limp mode is triggered.

Reported on: autoguide.top

Defective frein de stationnement (parking brake) sensor recall

Found with a diagnostic scan

A recall campaign (reference 29971) targets Megane E-Tech models produced between April 10, 2024, and January 16, 2025, due to a mispositioned parking brake sensor that can prevent the brake from engaging in 'P' position.

What to check. Verify with the dealership using the vehicle’s serial number whether the recall (29971) has been addressed. Test the parking brake functionality in 'P' position during inspection.

Reported on: autoplus.fr

Electric power steering voltage sensitivity

Found with a diagnostic scan

The Megane’s electric power steering system is highly vulnerable to voltage fluctuations from weak batteries or failing alternators, leading to sudden loss of assist and steering instability.

What to check. Test for heavy steering at low speeds, intermittent steering assist loss, or an active steering warning light. Check battery voltage and alternator output during operation.

Reported on: autoguide.top

Heat pump failure leading to increased energy consumption and charging aborts

Noticed on a test drive

The heat pump in the Mégane E-Tech Electric is a known weak point that can fail around 70,000 km, causing increased energy consumption and triggering charging problems. Repair is lengthy and costly.

What to check. Check for reduced heating output in winter, increased energy consumption, or frequent charging aborts. Verify if the vehicle has undergone heat pump repairs.

Reported on: what-breaks.com

Heat pump insulation fault causing charging aborts

Noticed on a test drive

Early 2022 Renault Mégane E-Tech models suffer from a chronic heat-pump insulation defect that prevents charging entirely, often requiring warranty or goodwill resolution. This defect is linked to a known weakness acknowledged by Renault.

What to check. Check if the vehicle is from early 2022 and verify the heat-pump insulation history via fault memory. Look for signs of reduced heating output in winter or increased energy consumption.

Reported on: what-breaks.com

Infotainment system instability (Megane E-TECH)

Found by looking

The mention of a dedicated 'Notice Megane E-TECH' section implies chronic issues with the vehicle's infotainment or electrical systems, likely tied to software or hardware incompatibilities.

What to check. Test the infotainment system for crashes, unresponsiveness, or graphical glitches. Verify connectivity with OpenR Link (Renault’s telematics system) and check for error messages during operation.

Reported on: github.com

Oil pressure solenoid valve failure (petrol/diesel)

Found with a diagnostic scan

A failing oil pressure solenoid valve causes erratic oil pressure regulation, leading to dangerous pressure spikes that damage seals, gaskets, and internal engine components, especially under heat.

What to check. Look for illuminated check engine light, rough engine operation when hot, or oil pressure warnings. Verify oil pressure consistency under load.

Reported on: autoguide.top

Parking brake sensor misalignment recall (0EWQ)

Found with a diagnostic scan

A factory recall for model years 2024–2025 due to a misaligned sensor in the parking brake actuator, causing the parking brake to fail and risking the vehicle rolling away.

What to check. Check if the vehicle is within the 2024–2025 model years and verify if the parking brake sensor recall has been addressed. Test the parking brake functionality to ensure it engages properly.

Reported on: what-breaks.com

Premature cardan and wheel bearing failures

Noticed on a test drive

Cardan joints and wheel bearings have failed prematurely between 80,000 and 115,000 km, requiring expensive replacements (€2,000–€2,500 for a pair of cardans).

What to check. Listen for unusual clacking or rumbling noises while driving on rough roads or during cornering. Check for excessive play in wheel bearings and inspect cardan joints for wear or damage.

Reported on: autoplus.fr

Premature motor failures (130ch & 220ch)

Some Renault Megane E-Tech electric motors (both 130ch and 220ch variants) have failed after only a few thousand kilometers, requiring costly replacements and prolonged vehicle downtime.

What to check. Check for service records confirming motor replacements or extended warranty coverage for electrical propulsion components. Listen for unusual noises or vibrations during acceleration.

Reported on: autoplus.fr

Steering Assistance Motor Failure (E-Tech)

Found with a diagnostic scan

Chronic issue with the electric steering assistance motor causing heavy steering, likely due to overheating or system faults. This is not a standard wear-and-tear issue but a known defect in the Renault Megane E-Tech model.

What to check. Check for warning lights related to steering assistance and test steering responsiveness under normal driving conditions. Listen for unusual noises or vibrations from the steering column.

Reported on: user-manual.renault.com

System-wide electrical instability (battery/ECU sensitivity)

Found with a diagnostic scan

Insufficient or unstable battery output disrupts critical systems (power steering, windows, start-stop, dashboard lights), often exacerbated by age-related battery degradation or improper replacements.

What to check. Check for intermittent power steering failure, slow/non-functional windows/mirrors, or dashboard warning light flickering. Test battery voltage under load and perform ECU resets post-replacement.

Air Conditioning Compressor/Fan Overheating (E-Tech)

Found with a diagnostic scan

The air conditioning compressor and ventilation fan may run excessively (over 30 minutes) after the ignition is switched off, indicating a chronic electrical or thermal management issue in the system.

What to check. Test the air conditioning system by activating it and monitoring its behavior after turning off the ignition. Check for unusual noises, overheating, or prolonged operation of the fan/compressor.

Reported on: user-manual.renault.com

Card Key Synchronization Issues (E-Tech)

Found with a diagnostic scan

Chronic problem with the card key desynchronization, requiring manual intervention to reset synchronization, which can be inconvenient and indicative of a design flaw in the key system.

What to check. Test the card key by attempting to unlock the door and start the vehicle. If synchronization issues persist, check for error messages on the dashboard and attempt to resynchronize the card.

Reported on: user-manual.renault.com

Charging Impossible error due to AC Charger sensitivity to grid grounding resistance

Found with a diagnostic scan

The Renault Megane E-Tech's AC charging system (Chameleon Charger) is overly sensitive to grid grounding resistance fluctuations, causing false 'Charging Impossible' errors even with adequate infrastructure. This requires a software update to adjust tolerance thresholds.

What to check. Check if the vehicle frequently triggers 'Charging Impossible' errors at home or public charging stations. Verify grid grounding resistance (should be <2 Ohms) and confirm the latest BMS (Battery Management System) tolerance update has been applied via a service visit.

Reported on: elektrikliturkiye.com

Corroded Gas Pedal Connector Causing Power Loss (Renault Mégane E-Tech)

Found by looking

A specific case where corrosion in the gas pedal connector caused power loss and warning lights, resolved by cleaning the connector with an electrical contact cleaner. This suggests a potential design or environmental exposure flaw in the connector placement or material.

What to check. Inspect the gas pedal connector for corrosion or oxidation. Clean the connector with an electrical contact cleaner if necessary. Verify if the issue resolves after cleaning.

Fragile 2022-series climate control compressor

Found with a diagnostic scan

Climate control compressors on 2022-model Megane E-Tech vehicles have shown premature failure, particularly after rapid charging sessions.

What to check. Test the climate control system during inspection, especially if the vehicle is a 2022 model. Listen for unusual noises from the compressor and check for refrigerant leaks or reduced cooling performance.

Heat pump (climate control) vibration/noise in cold weather

Noticed on a test drive

The Renault Megane E-Tech exhibits excessive vibration and noise (described as 'tractör benzeri uğultu') from the heat pump system when operating in temperatures below -5°C. This requires an official acoustic isolation kit to mitigate the issue.

What to check. Test the vehicle in cold conditions (below -5°C) and listen for abnormal noise/vibration from the front of the car when the climate system is activated. Confirm the servis has applied the Renault-provided acoustic isolation kit (e.g., vibration dampeners on compressor mounts).

Reported on: elektrikliturkiye.com

Heater blower resistor failure

Found by looking

Thermal fatigue and electrical resistance buildup cause the heater blower resistor to fail, restricting airflow at higher fan speeds and leading to inconsistent cabin heating.

What to check. Test heater performance at all fan speeds; inconsistent airflow or cold cabin conditions despite high/low settings may indicate resistor failure. Inspect wiring connectors for corrosion.

Reported on: autoguide.top

Ignition coil pack failure (petrol engines)

Found with a diagnostic scan

Individual ignition coil failures disrupt combustion balance, causing misfires, rough idling, and increased fuel consumption. Multiple coils often fail simultaneously due to shared electrical issues.

What to check. Listen for misfires under load, rough idle, or check engine light activation. Scan for live cylinder misfires and replace all coils if one fails.

Reported on: autoguide.top

What to check. Check the range display while driving and compare it with real-world driving conditions. Verify if the displayed range significantly deviates from actual performance over multiple trips.

Potential Misdiagnosis Due to Generic OBD-II Scanner Use

Generic OBD-II scanners designed for internal combustion engine vehicles may incorrectly interpret or trigger P0420 codes in fully electric vehicles, leading to false positives.

What to check. Use an EV-specific OBD-II scanner to avoid misdiagnosis. Confirm the scanner is compatible with Renault’s E-Tech Electric diagnostic protocols.

Reported on: edigital.com.tr

Confusion Between Fully Electric and Hybrid E-Tech Models

Found with a diagnostic scan

Renault’s E-Tech lineup includes both fully electric and hybrid models. Misidentification of the vehicle type could lead to incorrect triggering of P0420 codes, as hybrid models have catalytic converters.

What to check. Verify the vehicle’s powertrain configuration (fully electric vs. hybrid) to rule out confusion between model variants. Check the vehicle identification number (VIN) or consult the manufacturer’s documentation.

Reported on: edigital.com.tr

OpenR Link infotainment system instability (CarPlay/Donma)

Found by looking

Early software versions of the Megane E-Tech's OpenR Link system suffered from Apple CarPlay freezing or screen blackouts during use. This was later resolved via a firmware update (OpenR Link 2.2) and a manual reset procedure.

What to check. Check if the infotainment system exhibits random freezes, black screens, or CarPlay instability. Verify the latest software version (OpenR Link 2.2+) is installed. Test the manual reset procedure (holding the volume button for 10 seconds).

Reported on: elektrikliturkiye.com
