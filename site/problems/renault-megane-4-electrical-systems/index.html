Charging aborts and 'battery charging impossible' fault messages

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

Multiple reports indicate charging aborts and the error message 'battery charging impossible,' often occurring after OTA updates. This is frequently linked to heat pump defects or software issues.

What to checkAttempt to charge the vehicle and monitor for the 'battery charging impossible' error or an orange LED on the charging cable. Check for recent OTA updates.

Reported onwhat-breaks.com

Chronic Throttle Body Valve/Electronic Gas Pedal Module Failures (2023 Renault Mégane E-Tech)

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

Multiple reports confirm persistent failures in the throttle body valve and electronic gas pedal module, leading to power loss and engine warning lights. This is not a one-off issue but a recurring problem affecting multiple owners, with some requiring full replacement of components at significant cost.

What to checkCheck for error codes related to throttle position or gas pedal sensor failures. Inspect the gas pedal module and throttle body valve for physical damage, corrosion, or loose connections. Verify if the issue persists after cleaning or replacing the gas pedal connector (as seen in one case where a paslit connector resolved the issue).

Reported onmycarly.com

DPF clogging in 1.5 dCi/1.9 dCi engines

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

Short urban trips prevent the DPF from reaching regeneration temperature, causing soot buildup, restricted exhaust flow, and limp-mode entry, leading to permanent performance degradation if unaddressed.

What to checkCheck for DPF warning light activation, increased fuel consumption, and power loss during city driving. Inspect exhaust backpressure if limp mode is triggered.

Reported onautoguide.top

Defective frein de stationnement (parking brake) sensor recall

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

A recall campaign (reference 29971) targets Megane E-Tech models produced between April 10, 2024, and January 16, 2025, due to a mispositioned parking brake sensor that can prevent the brake from engaging in 'P' position.

What to checkVerify with the dealership using the vehicle’s serial number whether the recall (29971) has been addressed. Test the parking brake functionality in 'P' position during inspection.

Reported onautoplus.fr

Electric power steering voltage sensitivity

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

The Megane’s electric power steering system is highly vulnerable to voltage fluctuations from weak batteries or failing alternators, leading to sudden loss of assist and steering instability.

What to checkTest for heavy steering at low speeds, intermittent steering assist loss, or an active steering warning light. Check battery voltage and alternator output during operation.

Reported onautoguide.top

Heat pump failure leading to increased energy consumption and charging aborts

SeriousNoticed on a test drive

The heat pump in the Mégane E-Tech Electric is a known weak point that can fail around 70,000 km, causing increased energy consumption and triggering charging problems. Repair is lengthy and costly.

What to checkCheck for reduced heating output in winter, increased energy consumption, or frequent charging aborts. Verify if the vehicle has undergone heat pump repairs.

Reported onwhat-breaks.com

Heat pump insulation fault causing charging aborts

SeriousNoticed on a test drive

Early 2022 Renault Mégane E-Tech models suffer from a chronic heat-pump insulation defect that prevents charging entirely, often requiring warranty or goodwill resolution. This defect is linked to a known weakness acknowledged by Renault.

What to checkCheck if the vehicle is from early 2022 and verify the heat-pump insulation history via fault memory. Look for signs of reduced heating output in winter or increased energy consumption.

Reported onwhat-breaks.com

SeriousFound by looking

The mention of a dedicated 'Notice Megane E-TECH' section implies chronic issues with the vehicle's infotainment or electrical systems, likely tied to software or hardware incompatibilities.

What to checkTest the infotainment system for crashes, unresponsiveness, or graphical glitches. Verify connectivity with OpenR Link (Renault’s telematics system) and check for error messages during operation.

Oil pressure solenoid valve failure (petrol/diesel)

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

A failing oil pressure solenoid valve causes erratic oil pressure regulation, leading to dangerous pressure spikes that damage seals, gaskets, and internal engine components, especially under heat.

What to checkLook for illuminated check engine light, rough engine operation when hot, or oil pressure warnings. Verify oil pressure consistency under load.

Reported onautoguide.top

Parking brake sensor misalignment recall (0EWQ)

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

A factory recall for model years 2024–2025 due to a misaligned sensor in the parking brake actuator, causing the parking brake to fail and risking the vehicle rolling away.

What to checkCheck if the vehicle is within the 2024–2025 model years and verify if the parking brake sensor recall has been addressed. Test the parking brake functionality to ensure it engages properly.

Reported onwhat-breaks.com

Premature cardan and wheel bearing failures

SeriousNoticed on a test drive

Cardan joints and wheel bearings have failed prematurely between 80,000 and 115,000 km, requiring expensive replacements (€2,000–€2,500 for a pair of cardans).

What to checkListen for unusual clacking or rumbling noises while driving on rough roads or during cornering. Check for excessive play in wheel bearings and inspect cardan joints for wear or damage.

What to checkCheck for service records confirming motor replacements or extended warranty coverage for electrical propulsion components. Listen for unusual noises or vibrations during acceleration.

Charging Impossible error due to AC Charger sensitivity to grid grounding resistance

To checkFound with a diagnostic scan

The Renault Megane E-Tech's AC charging system (Chameleon Charger) is overly sensitive to grid grounding resistance fluctuations, causing false 'Charging Impossible' errors even with adequate infrastructure. This requires a software update to adjust tolerance thresholds.

What to checkCheck if the vehicle frequently triggers 'Charging Impossible' errors at home or public charging stations. Verify grid grounding resistance (should be <2 Ohms) and confirm the latest BMS (Battery Management System) tolerance update has been applied via a service visit.

Reported onelektrikliturkiye.com

Heat pump (climate control) vibration/noise in cold weather

To checkNoticed on a test drive

The Renault Megane E-Tech exhibits excessive vibration and noise (described as 'tractör benzeri uğultu') from the heat pump system when operating in temperatures below -5°C. This requires an official acoustic isolation kit to mitigate the issue.

What to checkTest the vehicle in cold conditions (below -5°C) and listen for abnormal noise/vibration from the front of the car when the climate system is activated. Confirm the servis has applied the Renault-provided acoustic isolation kit (e.g., vibration dampeners on compressor mounts).

Reported onelektrikliturkiye.com

Heater blower resistor failure

To checkFound by looking

Thermal fatigue and electrical resistance buildup cause the heater blower resistor to fail, restricting airflow at higher fan speeds and leading to inconsistent cabin heating.

What to checkTest heater performance at all fan speeds; inconsistent airflow or cold cabin conditions despite high/low settings may indicate resistor failure. Inspect wiring connectors for corrosion.

Ignition coil pack failure (petrol engines)

To checkFound with a diagnostic scan

Individual ignition coil failures disrupt combustion balance, causing misfires, rough idling, and increased fuel consumption. Multiple coils often fail simultaneously due to shared electrical issues.

What to checkListen for misfires under load, rough idle, or check engine light activation. Scan for live cylinder misfires and replace all coils if one fails.

Potential Misdiagnosis Due to Generic OBD-II Scanner Use

To check

Generic OBD-II scanners designed for internal combustion engine vehicles may incorrectly interpret or trigger P0420 codes in fully electric vehicles, leading to false positives.

What to checkUse an EV-specific OBD-II scanner to avoid misdiagnosis. Confirm the scanner is compatible with Renault’s E-Tech Electric diagnostic protocols.

OpenR Link infotainment system instability (CarPlay/Donma)

MinorFound by looking

Early software versions of the Megane E-Tech's OpenR Link system suffered from Apple CarPlay freezing or screen blackouts during use. This was later resolved via a firmware update (OpenR Link 2.2) and a manual reset procedure.

What to checkCheck if the infotainment system exhibits random freezes, black screens, or CarPlay instability. Verify the latest software version (OpenR Link 2.2+) is installed. Test the manual reset procedure (holding the volume button for 10 seconds).
