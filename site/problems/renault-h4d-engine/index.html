Recurrent dashboard reset causing temporary blank screen

Serious

The dashboard display resets intermittently while the vehicle is in operation, resulting in a blank screen that disrupts critical driver information. This is a chronic software defect affecting all Tucson variants.

What to checkTest the dashboard display by monitoring for unexpected resets or blank screens while the vehicle is running. Confirm if the display flickers or restarts unexpectedly during driving.

Reported onensonhaber.com

h4d DSG low-speed jerks, clutch and mechatronics failures

SeriousFound with a diagnostic scan

The 7-speed DSG in this Polo can jerk at low speeds and suffer from clutch or mechatronics failures, leading to expensive repairs. It is a known weakness of this gearbox.

What to checkDuring test drive, check for jerky low-speed shifts; inspect service history for DSG oil changes or previous mechatronics replacement.

Reported onautoricambitritella.it

Worth checking1

Electronic defects (infotainment freezing, sensor warnings, Start&Stop failure)

To checkFound by looking

The 1.0 TSI Polo has a higher incidence of electronic issues, including infotainment freezing, random sensor warning lights (ABS, airbag, TPMS), and Start&Stop system failures, often linked to battery issues.

What to checkTest the 12V battery and check for infotainment freezing or sensor warning lights during a demonstration. Ensure the Start&Stop system functions properly.
