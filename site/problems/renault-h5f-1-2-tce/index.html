1.2 TCe (h5f) excessive oil consumption

Found by looking

Owners report this engine burns oil from around 85,000–110,000 km, leading to low oil levels and potential engine damage if not monitored. It's a known issue specific to this engine, not general to all cars.

What to check. Check oil level on the dipstick (should be within marks). Look for blue smoke from exhaust on start-up and during acceleration. Ask for service records showing oil top-ups or frequent oil changes.

H5F (1.2 TCe) wastegate clogging can cause turbo overspeed failure
