Jet Black aluminum case coating peels off around the sensors after months of wear

Serious

Found via 'Apple Watch Series 10 common problems'. heise reports widespread coating peeling on the Jet Black Series 10, confirmed by Apple support forum and Reddit reports.

What to checkIf buying the Jet Black variant, inspect the back under bright light and keep it in warranty; Apple reportedly replaces under warranty but some users are told it is user damage and quoted about 350 euros.

Reported onheise.de

Speaker becomes weak and muffled after one or more months of use

Serious

Found via 'Apple Watch Series 10 common problems'. PhoneArena aggregates Reddit and Apple support forum reports of the speaker going weak; Apple published a support document and offers replacements, though some users report the replacement also fails.

What to checkIf the speaker sounds thin or weak, try cleaning, water ejection, restart and re-pair first (Apple's official steps); otherwise claim warranty replacement, but be aware replacements may repeat the fault.

Reported onphonearena.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking2

Battery drain tied to a WatchOS 11 bug, fixed by updating to 11.0.1

To check

Found via 'Apple Watch Series 10 common problems'. Android Authority attributes much of the Series 10 battery drain to a WatchOS 11 bug fixed in 11.0.1, a software rather than hardware cause.

What to checkIf battery drains fast, first update to WatchOS 11.0.1 or later before assuming a hardware fault; this is a software fix, not a repair cost.

Reported onandroidauthority.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Heart rate sensor fails to display readings at session start and shows implausibly high values

To check

Found via 'Apple Watch Series 10 common problems'. A marathon runner's detailed first-hand account of the heart rate sensor dropping readings at session start and then showing implausibly high values (e.g. 171 bpm early in a slow run).

What to checkIf you rely on heart rate for training, verify readings against a chest strap; the sensor can show gaps and wrong values, which matters for zone-based training.

Reported onbgr.com
