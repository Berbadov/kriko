Green vertical line on Galaxy S23 display after software update, requires display replacement

Serious

German tech outlet Futurezone reports Galaxy S23 owners seeing a persistent green line after a software update with no physical damage, attributed to overheating during updates, and states the only fix is display replacement.

What to checkBefore buying, treat the S23 display as a known weak point: a green line can appear after a routine update with no drop or impact, and the fix is a paid display swap. Keep the phone cool during updates and confirm warranty/replacement coverage.

Reported onfuturezone.de

Permanent green and pink lines on Galaxy S23 display after One UI 8.5 update

Serious

Digit.in reports multiple Galaxy S23 owners seeing permanent green and pink lines after the One UI 8.5 update, believed linked to heat during installation, with no official fix confirmed.

What to checkIf you own or are buying an S23, be cautious installing large updates in hot conditions; the line defect is permanent and the fix is a display replacement. Samsung's free-replacement program for this issue was only confirmed to run until 25 December 2025.

Reported ondigit.in

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking3

Battery drain on Galaxy S23 after One UI 7 update, users charging multiple times a day

To check

Turkish tech site Webtekno reports Galaxy S23 owners on Reddit and Samsung forums seeing a marked rise in battery consumption after the One UI 7 update, with one user needing to charge several times a day even on light use.

What to checkExpect possible battery drain right after a major One UI update; it is often temporary while the phone re-learns usage, but if it persists a factory reset was reported to help. Budget for this when buying a used S23.

Reported onwebtekno.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Camera quality worsened on Galaxy S23 after One UI 7 update

To check

Yahoo Tech reports Galaxy S23 series users complaining that camera quality worsened after the One UI 7 update, with photos described as blurry, washed out and less sharp, and community moderators advising error reports via Samsung Members.

What to checkA software update can degrade the S23 camera output (blurry/washed-out photos). If camera quality matters to you, this is a software regression to watch for and report through Samsung Members rather than a hardware fault.

Reported ontech.yahoo.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Overheating and fast battery drain on Galaxy S23 after Android 8.5 update

To check

A Turkish owner complaint on Sikayetvar states that after the Android 8.5 update the Galaxy S23 heats up noticeably more and drains battery much faster, with a full-day charge now needing frequent recharging; Samsung Turkey responded asking for contact details.

What to checkPost-update overheating and battery drain are a recurring S23 complaint in the Turkish market. If you buy one, be ready for a possible update-related battery regression and check whether a newer update or reset fixes it.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27
