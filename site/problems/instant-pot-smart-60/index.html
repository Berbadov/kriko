Smart-60 lid removable under pressure, scalding burns (Wilder v. Instant Brands, 2023 complaint)

Serious

This is the actual filed federal complaint (Docket 1:23-cv-1964, N.D. Illinois) naming the Smart-60 model and alleging the lid could be opened while the cooker retained pressure, projecting scalding contents.

What to checkThis is an allegation in a court filing, not an adjudicated finding, and it is a US case. It describes a lid-safety failure mode (lid opening under retained pressure) distinct from the electrical recall. Treat it as a documented claim to weigh, not a proven defect.

Smart-60 thermal probe electric-shock recall (CPSC, July 2015)

Serious

This is the official CPSC recall for the Smart and Smart-60, serials 1410-1503, manufacture dates 12/1/2014-6/1/2015, about 1,000 US units plus 140 in Canada, remedy a free replacement base.

What to checkIf you own a Smart-60 within those serial/date ranges, stop using it and contact Instant Pot for the free replacement base; the documented hazard is electric shock from the thermal probe. This is a US/Canada-market recall, so a Turkish-market unit should be checked against its serial/date code rather than assumed covered.

Reported oncpsc.gov

Worth checking2

Burn lawsuit: Smart-60 lid opened while pressure remained, scalding contents

To check

A law-firm page tracking litigation reports a March 29, 2023 federal complaint alleging a Smart-60 lid opened while pressure remained inside, projecting scalding contents and causing burn injuries; a similar April 2025 Florida complaint alleges hot contents burned a child. These are unproven allegations, not adjudicated findings, and they are separate from the electrical recall. This came from the 'Instant Pot Smart-60 recall' search.

What to checkThese are allegations only, not established defects, so treat them as a warning signal rather than a confirmed failure. The claimed failure mode is the lid opening under residual pressure, which is the burn scenario a buyer should be aware of. I could not verify these cases against a court docket within this run's budget.

Reported onschmidtandclark.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Smart-60 owner wiki: float valve steam leak and sealing ring wear

To check

The iFixit Smart-60 troubleshooting wiki (this page is in Chinese) documents float-valve steam leaks from dirt on the float valve/anti-block shield and sealing-ring compression over time. Owner comments on the page also report C6L/C6H error codes.

What to checkOwner-reported failures on this model center on the float valve and sealing ring; cleaning the float valve and anti-block shield and periodically replacing the sealing ring are the recurring fixes. The page is a student-contributed wiki, so treat it as owner evidence rather than an official statement.

Reported onzh.ifixit.com
