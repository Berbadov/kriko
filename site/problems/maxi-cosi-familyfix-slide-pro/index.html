FamilyFix Slide Pro base (Ref. 8065057110) recalled: green connection indicator can show while seat is only partially attached

Serious

The UK Trading Standards recall (PSD 2605-0109) confirms the FamilyFix Slide Pro base, item ref 8065057110, made 6 Sep 2025–24 Mar 2026, is recalled because the green indicator can show while the seat is only partially connected.

What to checkIf you own this base, check the serial number against Maxi-Cosi's recall page. Affected bases are replaced free of charge; until the replacement arrives, do not rely on the green indicator alone — verify the seat clicks in at both front and back on every installation.

FamilyFix Slide Pro base (Ref. 8065057110) voluntary recall: green visual indicator can show locked when car seat is not fully connected

Serious

This finding came from the seed query 'Maxi-Cosi FamilyFix Slide Pro recall safety' (UK regulator GOV.UK recall notice 2605-0109, published 28 May 2026).

What to checkIf you own a FamilyFix Slide Pro base manufactured between 20 August 2025 and 24 March 2026, check the model reference and manufacturing date against Maxi-Cosi's online checker; affected bases are replaced free of charge. Until the replacement arrives, do not rely on the green indicator alone — manually verify the car seat is clicked in at front and back, or use the seat belt installation.

German forum thread (citing ADAC): indicator can stay green when the base has not clicked in, seat could move in a crash

Serious

This finding came from the seed query 'Maxi-Cosi FamilyFix Slide Pro Rückruf Sicherheit' (German forum thread citing ADAC).

What to checkA consumer forum thread citing ADAC describes the practical failure: if the seat is not fully released and then re-placed, the base may not click in but the indicator stays green, so the seat is not firmly locked and could move through the vehicle in a crash. The author urges affected owners to act quickly.
