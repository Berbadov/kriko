3 sourced reports, with what to check before you buy. Each one links to the page it was reported on.

Costly or dangerous

Bosch PUE611BB5E induction hob power imbalance burns pan bases — fire risk

Safety risk

An owner complaint on Şikayetvar names this exact model (PUE611BB5E Serie 4) and reports that after about a month of problems the hob developed marked power imbalance between zones: three separate pans' bottoms burned and one pan's base separated completely, and while cooking the rear-zone kettle suddenly boiled over. This is a documented fire-adjacent incident specific to this product, not a general brand complaint. It came from the Turkish seed query 'Bosch PUE611BB5E yangın riski'.

What to check. If you own or are buying this hob, watch for uneven heating and overheating of cookware; if pans burn or boil unexpectedly, stop using the affected zone and contact Bosch service. The owner's report suggests the power-distribution behaviour is the thing to verify before relying on the hob.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Bosch PUE611BB5E induction hob power imbalance burns pans, launches kettle, and is called a fire risk by service

Safety risk

Owner report on the PUE611BB5E (found via the 'yangın riski' and 'cam patlaması' seed searches): after about a month of use the hob showed marked power imbalance across zones, three pans' bottoms burned, one pan base separated, and a kettle on the rear zone suddenly launched off, burning the owner's hand and face. The first authorized service said the hob had been wired incorrectly, the motherboard may be damaged, and continued use carried a fire risk; a second service disputed this. The owner stopped using the hob and left the home for three days.

What to check. If you own or are buying this model, treat reports of uneven zone power as a safety matter, not a nuisance: stop using the hob and have an authorized service inspect the wiring and motherboard before cooking again. The conflicting service statements mean you should get the diagnosis in writing. This is an owner report, not an official recall.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com

Worth checking

Bosch PUE611BB5 induction hob third zone cycles on/off; service diagnoses motherboard (anakart) fault

Owner report on the close model PUE611BB5 (listed on the PUE611BB5E complaint page, found via the 'glass door shattered forum' and 'cam patlaması' searches): opening the third zone produces a repeated on/off 'tık tık' cycling sound, with heating cutting out and restarting in sync. The authorized service diagnosed a faulty motherboard (anakart) and set about sourcing a replacement.

What to check. Expect that a motherboard fault on this series may present as a single zone cycling on/off rather than a total failure. Have the service confirm the part and warranty coverage before paying; the owner here was told the motherboard was faulty and needed replacing.

Reported on: sikayetvar.com
