Costly or dangerous2

L10s Ultra base station middle nozzle pumps water continuously until the tank is empty

Serious

An owner reports a large puddle around the self-wash base: the middle nozzle keeps pumping water after the robot leaves, overflowing the basin until the clean-water tank is empty; the owner suspects the nozzle shut-off valve is defective.

What to checkA base-station water leak can flood the floor around the dock; this is a high-severity failure that the owner could not fix by cleaning or factory reset and required contacting Dreame support under warranty.

Reported onroboter-forum.com

L10s Ultra leaks water in place and keeps mop pads wet from first use

Serious

A Media Markt buyer in Turkey reports the unit leaks water in place from the first use, leaving the mop pads permanently wet so it cannot clean properly; the fault persisted on a replacement unit and after service visits.

What to checkBefore buying, check the unit's water delivery and mop system in the first weeks; a leak that survives a replacement unit suggests a recurring design fault, and multiple service trips were needed.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Worth checking2

L10s Ultra battery drains to 10-15% after ~70 minutes instead of ~200 minutes

To check

From the same Turkish complaint. The owner reports runtime far below the ~200-minute spec, dropping to 10-15% after about 70 minutes, and says the problem continued on the replacement unit.

What to checkIf you rely on long single-pass runs, verify real runtime early; a battery that falls short of spec and is not fixed by a replacement unit is a costly thing to discover later.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

L10s Ultra scheduled cleaning stops after first cycle and robot fails to make dock contact to recharge

To check

An owner whose unit ran well for two years reports a scheduled cleaning that stopped after the first cycle, with the planned intermediate charge not happening and the robot sitting in the dock without making charging contact; a friend's identical unit showed the same fault, and the owner attributes it to software.

What to checkIf you depend on multi-cycle scheduled cleaning, this failure mode means the robot may stop mid-task and fail to recharge; the owner could not roll back the firmware, so a fix depends on a software update.

Reported oncomputerbase.de

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Minor1

Official troubleshooting page: a wet filter can cause abnormal noise during cleaning

Minor

From the manufacturer's official French troubleshooting page for the L10s Ultra. It documents abnormal cleaning noise and lists a wet filter as a cause, along with side-brush deformation and tangled main-brush ends.

What to checkLow-severity and owner-fixable: if the unit gets noisy, the official guidance is to let a washed filter dry fully and check the side brush and main brush for tangling before contacting service.

Reported ondreametech.zendesk.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27
