Fakir Airfry stops mid-cycle and will not restart; two service visits did not fix it

Serious

Owner reports the unit stops mid-cycle and will not restart, and that two service visits failed to resolve it.

What to checkThis is the most-reported Fakir Airfry fault. If buying, expect that a mid-cycle shutdown may not be fixed by warranty service; keep the receipt and be prepared to push for replacement rather than repair.

Fakir Airfryer interior plastic coating flakes into the food

Serious

Owner of a dual-basket model reports the interior plastic lining crumbling like flour into the food after about 4 years of use.

What to checkA health-relevant surface failure: degraded interior coating contaminating food. This appears after years of use, so it is a longevity concern rather than a first-use fault.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Fakir Mono Airfry repeatedly stops and restarts on its own

Serious

An independent second owner report of the same stop-start behaviour on the Mono model, corroborating the mid-cycle shutdown finding.

What to checkConfirms the shutdown fault is not an isolated unit; it recurs across models in the Airfry line.

Worth checking2

Fakir Airfryer leaves potatoes raw / undercooked

To check

Owner reports potatoes staying raw and not browning across three attempts, and requested a refund/replacement.

What to checkUneven/under-cooking reported on a new unit. If you buy, test with a simple batch (e.g. frozen fries) within the return window, since the seller directed this owner to service rather than a refund.

Reported onsikayetvar.com

Newest linked source retrieved: 2026-10-27

Fakir Airfryer never enters heating mode, gives a constant contact warning

To check

Owner reports the unit refuses to enter heating/cooking mode and repeatedly shows a contact (temassızlık) warning even with the basket seated.

What to checkA sensor/contact fault that stops cooking entirely; likely needs a service visit, and may recur.
