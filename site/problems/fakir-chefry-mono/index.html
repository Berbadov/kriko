Fakir Chefry Mono fails out of the box: ticking sound, cycling on/off on first use

Serious

Owner Selma reports the unit cycled on/off with a ticking sound on the very first day of use, immediately after purchase.

What to checkA brand-new unit exhibiting this behavior indicates a quality-control or design fault in the control circuitry, not user error. Test the unit immediately on delivery and return it if it cycles.

Fakir Chefry Mono mid-cycle shutdowns: unit stops and restarts on its own, never runs continuously

Serious

Owner Erol reports the unit stops and restarts by itself from the first use, never running continuously; authorized service claimed repair but the problem persisted unchanged.

What to checkIf buying, expect a real risk the unit will not run a full cycle. If owning, the failure appears within warranty in this case, but service did not resolve it — push for replacement rather than repair.

Fakir Chefry Mono rapid on/off cycling since day one, then total failure to power on after ~2 years

Serious

Owner reports rapid on/off cycling from the first use, and after roughly 2 years the unit completely stopped powering on — now out of warranty.

What to checkThis pattern suggests the cycling is not a transient defect but a progressive failure of the control/power circuitry. If you own one that cycles, expect it may eventually fail entirely; replacement parts after warranty will likely cost more than a new unit.

Fakir Chefry Mono self-stops repeatedly during use, unusable

Serious

Owner Samet reports the unit repeatedly stops and restarts by itself, making it unusable.

What to checkThis is the same mid-cycle shutdown pattern reported by multiple owners. The unit cannot complete a cooking cycle, so food will be undercooked or the process is interrupted entirely.

Fakir Chefry Mono starts cycling on/off shortly after purchase; authorized service did not resolve it

Serious

Owner Beren reports the unit worked initially but began cycling on/off by itself shortly after purchase, becoming unusable; the complaint title states service did not resolve the problem.

What to checkEven when the unit works at first, the cycling fault can appear within weeks. The complaint title indicates authorized service failed to fix it, so a repair may not be a reliable remedy.
