Fan arızası cihazı çalışmaz hale getiriyor, gıdalar bozuluyor

Serious

Owner bought the 674533 MB No Frost on 17/07/2025; shortly after delivery the fan fault stopped the unit and spoiled the food. Service opened a replacement record.

What to checkIf buying, treat the fan as the weak point: check cooling within the first weeks and keep the warranty/receipt, since a fan failure can stop the fridge and spoil contents. The replacement unit also failed to cool, so the fault may recur across units.

Fan motoru sekiz ayda bozuluyor

Serious

Owner's 674533 MB started making noise in month ~6 and by month ~8 the fan motor (which pushes cold air) failed; service ordered a part without opening the unit. Owner notes Beko gives 10-year warranty on the main compressor but not the fan motor.

What to checkExpect the fan motor to be the first electrical part to fail (around 6–8 months). A noisy fan is the early sign; if it fails, cooling stops and food spoils. Check whether the fan motor is covered before relying on the compressor's 10-year warranty.

Worth checking1

Tahliye hattı tıkanıyor, donma ve terleme tekrarlıyor

To check

Owner's 674533 MB No Frost had a clogged drain in the first 2 months, then a freezing fault in July requiring service, and ongoing intermittent freezing and sweating. Owner also reports rough, burred drain air holes and dirt/yellowing inside, calling the unit defective from production.

What to checkThe defrost drain is prone to clogging, which leads to ice buildup and water leaking/freezing. If you see frost or water, check the drain before assuming a bigger fault. The recurring freezing/sweating suggests a design-level issue rather than a one-off.

Yan duvarda garanti kapsamında sararma

Minor

A 674533 MB bought 10.04.2025 developed a visible yellowing on the side wall within about 3 months, still under warranty, and it does not resolve on its own.

What to checkA cosmetic side-wall yellowing appears within the first months on some units. It does not affect cooling, but if it matters to you, raise it early while under warranty since it does not self-correct.
