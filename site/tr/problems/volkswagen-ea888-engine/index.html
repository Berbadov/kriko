The DSG 7-speed dry-clutch transmission in the Scirocco 2.0 TSI suffers from adaptation loss after battery disconnections, leading to erratic shifting behavior, shuddering, and eventual clutch failure. This is exacerbated by urban stop-and-go driving and infrequent software updates.

Neye bakmalıTest for rough starts, shuddering during acceleration, or delayed engagement. Check for DTCs like P0740 (torque converter clutch circuit malfunction) or P0750 (torque converter clutch solenoid circuit malfunction). Verify if the issue persists after a battery disconnect or software reset.
