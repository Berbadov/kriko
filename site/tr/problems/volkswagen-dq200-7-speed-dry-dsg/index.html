Chronic DSG Transmission Issues Pre-2013

Ciddi

Volkswagen acknowledged chronic reliability issues in DSG transmissions before 2013, which were significantly addressed in later models. However, without regular maintenance, these issues can still occur in newer DSG transmissions.

Neye bakmalıCheck the service history for regular maintenance, especially for the DSG transmission. Look for signs of previous or ongoing transmission issues such as delayed gear shifts, unusual noises, and warning lights.
