Mekatronik Kart (ECU) Failure Leading to Safety Mode Activation

Critical failures in the mekatronik kart (electronic control unit) can cause the transmission to enter safety mode, locking the vehicle in a single gear and preventing further gear shifts. This is often due to hardware or software failures in the ECU.

Neye bakmalı. Check for sudden locking of the transmission in a single gear, inability to shift gears, and error codes such as **P1701 (mekatronik kontrol ünitesi hatası)**. Verify if the vehicle remains stuck in a single gear without warning.
