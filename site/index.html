04 / No mystery here

Good to know.

Still curious? Ask us

Where does my research live?

On your computer. Catalogs, history and settings stay with you. There’s no account to create. Online research sends your question and the pages read to the provider you choose.

€

What's the catch?

Kriko is free and open source under the MIT license. Catalog lookups need no key. If you choose a paid AI provider for research, that provider bills your own account.

Can I check new products too?

Yes. New and used products both have issues that can be easy to miss. Investigate owner experiences, repair findings and variant-specific concerns. Quick look researches beyond your installed catalogs with your selected agent and an internet connection.

↔

What if a concern is not confirmed?

A report can reveal something worth investigating without proving a defect. Follow the supporting pages, compare explanations and see what remains unanswered. You decide what the evidence means for your purchase.
