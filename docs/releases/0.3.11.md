# Kriko 0.3.11 verification - Windows x64

Built locally on 2026-11-08 from `main` at `74b410c6c`. It follows 0.3.10, whose
dossier UI was cut on a release branch and never reached `main`; this release
merges it back and ships it together with what `main` gained since. The
extension keeps its 0.5.0 manifest. The website and the phone app now live in
their own repositories (#335) and release on their own; the phone app's
matching release is 0.15.9.

## What changed for readers

- **The dossier UI and the dust, in one build.** The desktop app and the browser
  extension keep 0.3.10's ruled ledger rows, mono provenance and drafting-paper
  palette, and the home and product screens gain the dust: the product is built
  up in grains above the check, and the home's question sits over a field of
  dust on its own dark ground. The dust no longer follows the cursor. Reduce
  motion stops it like every other animation.
- **The final jack icon.** The app icon is the jack centred on a light grey
  plate, and the small sizes are rendered solid, without the split, below
  64 px.
- **The catalogs ship beside the installer.** 32 `.kpack` catalogs, their
  `packs.json` index and `packs-v0.3.11.zip` are attached; the installer still
  carries none.

## What was and was not observed

See `0.3.11.json`. The portable copy was started and the frozen engine's smoke
check passed. The installer was built and hashed but not run, and no live
research, setup, compare, stop, reconnect or upgrade journey was walked on this
build, so `tools/release_readiness.py` refuses complete stable certification,
as it did for 0.3.10.

The CI recipe (`desktop.yml`) was dispatched on `main` and re-run; its Windows
job failed both times in the pre-packaging test step on intermittent sqlite
"database is locked" and concurrency-count failures, a different set each run,
so the artifacts here come from the documented hand build.
