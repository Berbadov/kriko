# Kriko 0.3.10 verification - Windows x64

Built locally on 2026-11-08 from `release/0.3.10` at `dd3d2663a` in a clean
worktree. It follows 0.3.9 on `main`; the extension keeps its 0.5.0 manifest and
the phone app keeps its 0.15.7 version. This is a presentation release: the
desktop app, extension panel and options page, phone app and public site move to
the dossier system, with no engine or provider change.

## What changed for readers

- **The desktop app and the browser extension read as a dossier.** Findings are
  ruled ledger rows with an evidence gutter instead of floating cards. Source
  hosts, dates and counts are set in mono, the palette is drafting paper in
  light and blue-black graphite in dark, and one navy carries meaning. Severity
  repeats by rule shape, not only by colour. The home screen is a check desk,
  the rail and top bar are a single instrument strip, and settings are ruled
  rows. The decorative dither sky is replaced by a hairline grid.
- **The phone app carries the same language.** All four palettes, the landing,
  chat, findings, history, sheets and settings use the sharper corners,
  evidence-gutter rows and mono provenance. Long chats settle above the footer
  instead of hiding their last actions under the recent-search strip.
- **The public site is rebuilt on the dossier system.** The home is a dossier
  cover with a specimen evidence sheet, section labels are sentence-case mono
  indexes, and the guide, install, privacy and known-problem pages share the
  same ruled structure. The app, panel, guide and phone pictures were recaptured
  from the rebuilt interfaces.
- **The catalogs ship beside the installer.** This release attaches 32 `.kpack`
  catalogs, their `packs.json` index, and `packs-0.3.10.zip` holding the same 33
  entries in one folder. The app still installs and updates one verified
  download at a time from `packs.json`; the installer still carries none.

  and the install smoke refuses to replace it.

  0.3.10 engine.
- The extension against a live listing on the packaged app.

## Packaging notes

- The updater manifest (`engine.json`) reports shell 0.3.10, so an older app is
  offered the installer rather than an engine-only update.
- The extension ZIP is the `extension/` folder without `tests/`; its manifest
  stays 0.5.0.
- The installer is unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run.
