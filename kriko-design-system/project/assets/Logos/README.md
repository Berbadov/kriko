The KRIKO wordmark is condensed, square-shouldered capitals with a chamfered O. Only the first K is special: its stem is cut down to a short pivot post (two thirds of cap height) that the two folding arms hinge on, and a lead screw runs through it from the left, with a wing-nut crank outside the post and a square nut on the right, the way the screw sits in a car jack (kriko).

Files are single-ink SVGs, so `<img>` cannot recolour them: pick by ground.

- `kriko-wordmark-white.svg`: white, for the brand top bar, `ground` and any dark surface.
- `kriko-wordmark-black.svg`: near-black (`#05070f`), for light grounds and print.
- `kriko-mark-white.svg`: the jack K alone in white, for app icons on `brand`.
- `kriko-mark-brand.svg`: the jack K alone in `brand` blue, for `ground` and `surface-1` (3.5:1).

The animated mark is not a file: the asset store strips SVG animation. Copy its inline markup from the Motion preview.

Keep clear space of one stem width (16 of 100 units of cap height) on every side, plus the 18-unit crank on the left. Do not recolour, outline, add effects, or set the name in another typeface; the wordmark is outlined paths, not Barlow Condensed. Minimum height 16px for the mark and 14px for the wordmark; below that the screw disappears into a plain K, which is acceptable.
