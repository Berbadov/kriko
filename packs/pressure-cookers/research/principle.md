# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented lid and pressure failures, burns, fires and recalls for Instant Pot electric pressure cookers and multicookers sold in Turkey. It covers only what official recall notices, court filings and manufacturer manuals state. It does not cover general wear and tear, routine maintenance, or issues that any pressure cooker of any brand shares. Claims are model-specific; where a claim applies to a specific variant, the variant is named in the claim.
