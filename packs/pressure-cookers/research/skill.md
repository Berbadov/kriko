# Identifying a product in Instant Pot electric pressure cookers before researching it

A listing's name is not a subject. Resolve what this catalog tells products apart by *before*
searching, and search on that.

1. **Resolve identity before searching.** This catalog tells its subjects apart by:

   - a **product**: `brand`, `series`

   Read the keys from the installed catalog at run time, never from memory: the list changes
   when the catalog does.
2. **Search the discriminating attribute, not the label.** What goes wrong is reported against
   the model, version or configuration, not against a brand. A brand alone finds marketing,
   not failure reports.
3. **One product versus its family.** A report about a sibling product can inform a search; it
   is never filed as a fact about this one unless the claim says which parts the two share.
4. **Refuse rather than guess.** If the listing does not fix every key above, report what is
   missing and what would settle it, and leave the subject unresearched instead of aiming at a
   neighbour. A confident wrong answer is worse than none.
5. **The bar is `principle.md`, in full.** Read it before filing anything.
