# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented failures of condensing combi boilers sold in Turkey and Europe: ignition and flame lockouts, leaks and pressure faults, burned control boards, and repeated no-heat or no-hot-water faults. It records what owners and installers report, the fault codes manufacturers define, and the component each report points to. It does not cover price, service response times or comfort complaints unless they show a fault in the appliance, and it does not infer a defect from a single unverified report.
