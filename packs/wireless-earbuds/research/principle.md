# What this pack surfaces

markdown: This pack surfaces what owners and repair records report failing on Xiaomi Redmi Buds budget true-wireless earbuds (5-series and 6-series) sold in Turkey and Europe. It covers hardware defects (dead earbuds, one-side sound loss), battery life shortfalls, Bluetooth connectivity/disconnection problems, and known firmware quirks acknowledged by Xiaomi. It does not cover sound quality opinions, fit comfort, or features that work as advertised.
