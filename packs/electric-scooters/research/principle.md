# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces what is known to go wrong with the Xiaomi Electric Scooter 4 / Elite (the 'Elite 4' sold in Turkey): throttle failure (fault code 14), handlebar play, overstated real-world range, water ingress (error code 10), a weak front drum brake from the factory, and an easily scratched display. It does not cover other brands (Segway-Ninebot, Kugoo, Navee) or other Xiaomi models; those are out of scope. Claims are drawn from the manufacturer's own FAQ, verified buyer reviews, and Turkish owner complaints.
