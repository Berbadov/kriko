Kriko surfaces the risks a used-car buyer **cannot cheaply get
from a standard pre-purchase inspection** (ekspertiz) — what to worry
about for *this* car, before booking the expert.

| Keep when it is | Drop when it is |
|---|---|
| **Config-specific** — engine code, gearbox type, fuel, market; not advice for any car | Generic dashboard-light items, or anything true of every car |
| **Predictable from the listing** — mileage, age, fuel, gearbox imply a known weak point, no inspection needed | Anything the routine inspection already catches: fluids/leaks, pads, injector bench tests, compression |
| **Due unless proven otherwise** — cam belt, major service, high-mileage clutch; silence in the ad *is* the signal | |
| **Expensive or dangerous** — structural weak points; emissions, dual-clutch/mechatronics, turbo, timing | |

Test: *would a buyer learn this from a normal inspection anyway?*
If yes, noise. *Is it specific to this engine/gearbox/mileage and
predictable from the ad?* If yes, keep.
