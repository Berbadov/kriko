# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented overheating, fire, shock and recall history for named power strips and surge protectors from Anker, Belkin, APC, Ugreen and Philips. It does not cover every model of each brand, and absence of a claim is not proof a product is safe; the MOV-based surge protectors in this category degrade with use and can fail hot. It does not cover power banks, UPS battery units, or extension cords without surge protection.
