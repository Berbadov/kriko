# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented failure modes that a shopper cannot cheaply discover: power-connector melting on high-end NVIDIA cards (RTX 4090 and the RTX 50 series), high idle power draw and memory-junction temperatures on AMD RDNA 3 cards, and driver-level quirks on Intel Arc. It does not cover performance benchmarks, pricing, availability, or fitment in a specific PC case, and it does not compare AIB partner variants of the same model.
