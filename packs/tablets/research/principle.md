# What this pack surfaces

This pack covers the best-selling entry-tier tablets in Turkey and Europe (Samsung Galaxy Tab A series, Apple iPad 10th gen, Xiaomi Redmi Pad, Lenovo Tab M series, Huawei MatePad SE). It surfaces what owners and repair records report going wrong: Wi-Fi dropouts, severe lag and freezing, short battery life, faulty physical buttons, and mainboard failures. It does not cover flagship S-series or Pro models, and it does not judge screen quality or camera performance beyond what owners complain about.
