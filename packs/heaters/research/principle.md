# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented fire, overheating and shock hazards for electric space heaters, fan heaters and oil radiators sold in Turkey and Europe. It records official recalls (Dyson AM04/AM05, Kobe, Philips HD3477/3478/3479, De'Longhi 1980–1988) and owner-reported incidents (Xiaomi). It does not cover ordinary performance or comfort complaints unless they bear on safety, and it does not infer a hazard where no recall or report exists.
