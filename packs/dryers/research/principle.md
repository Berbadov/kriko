# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented fire risks, recalls, and safety defects for tumble dryers and washer-dryers sold in Turkey and Europe under Bosch, Siemens, Samsung, Beko, Arcelik, Whirlpool, Hotpoint, and Indesit. It does not cover general reliability or repair issues, nor recalls for non-EU markets. Where a finding applies to a specific variant or market, the claim says so.
