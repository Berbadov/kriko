# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces known failure modes and reliability complaints for consumer FDM 3D printers from Bambu Lab, Creality, Prusa, Elegoo and Anycubic. It covers what owners and repair records report going wrong — extruder clogs, thermal runaway errors, firmware bricking, first-layer calibration failures, and part wear. It does not cover print quality tuning tips, slicer settings, or resin printers.
