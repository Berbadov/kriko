# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented battery fires, recalls, and safety defects for electric cars sold in Turkey and Europe. It relies on official recall records (NHTSA, KBA, EU Safety Gate) and reputable automotive press. It does not cover routine maintenance, subjective owner complaints, or speculative failure modes without official or documented evidence. Claims are model-specific; where a recall applies to a particular variant or production window, that is stated in the claim.
