# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces what owners and repairers report going wrong with specific super-automatic and manual espresso machines sold in Turkey and Europe: pump and grinder failures, brew-group blockages, boiler coating flaking, water leaks, and descaling/scale problems. It does not judge taste or value, and it does not cover machines outside the listed brands. Claims are tied to the exact model or variant named; where a fault affects only one variant (e.g. the Gaggia EVO boiler), the claim says so.
