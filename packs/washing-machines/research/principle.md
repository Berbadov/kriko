# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces what owners and repair records report going wrong with the best-selling washing machine models in Turkey and Europe (Arçelik, Beko, Bosch, Samsung, LG, Siemens, Vestel). It does not rank brands, does not cover every model each brand sells, and does not report routine maintenance or user-error items that any inspection answers. Claims are model-specific; where a claim applies to a variant (e.g. a washer-dryer) that is stated in the claim.
