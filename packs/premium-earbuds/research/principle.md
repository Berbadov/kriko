# What this pack surfaces

markdown: This pack surfaces documented faults reported by owners of the Apple AirPods Pro 3 (launched September 2025), chiefly the static/white-noise behaviour under Active Noise Cancellation that two firmware updates have not resolved. It does not cover subjective sound-quality opinion, fit comfort, or feature praise, and it carries no claims for the other premium earbuds brands in the line-up until their own evidence is researched.
