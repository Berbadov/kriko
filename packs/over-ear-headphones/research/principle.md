# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces what is known to go wrong with a specific over-ear active noise-cancelling headphone model: physical failures (hinges, headbands, drivers, cables) and firmware/Bluetooth faults that owners and repairers report. It does not cover sound quality, ANC performance, comfort or battery life as opinions, nor accessories, apps or earpads. A claim is only made where an owner, forum, service bulletin or repair source names a concrete failure mode for that exact model; a model with no solid evidence ships with no claims.
