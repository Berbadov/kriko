# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented, model-specific failure modes for gaming monitors sold in the US and EU: VRR flicker and scanlines, OLED burn-in and the warranty that does or does not cover it, cooling-fan noise, firmware regressions that break VRR or colour modes, port and bandwidth limits that stop the advertised refresh rate or console features, text-rendering problems caused by an unusual subpixel layout, and uniformity or coating defects reported on a named model.

It does not cover subjective picture preferences, price advice, or sample-to-sample failure rates, because no page can establish those. A generic panel-type trait is included only when it costs the buyer something the spec sheet hides: a VA panel's black smearing on a 180 Hz monitor, the absence of local dimming on an HDR-rated model, or an HDMI 2.0 input on a monitor advertised at 240 Hz. A claim is attached to the model it names; an owner report about a sibling model is not extended to this one, and a claim resting on a single page says so in its body.

What it leaves out: models with no well-sourced problem were not given a row. No recall or safety notice for a gaming monitor was found, so the pack holds review findings and owner reports rather than regulatory actions.
