# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces documented safety defects, recalls, and safety notices for child car seats and baby strollers from Cybex, Maxi-Cosi, Chicco, Britax Römer, Joie, Graco, and Inglesina. It does not cover routine wear, subjective comfort, or non-safety quality issues. Claims are limited to what official recall documents, government safety notices, and independent testing organizations have reported.
