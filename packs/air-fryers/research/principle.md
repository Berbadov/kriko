# What this pack surfaces

This pack surfaces owner-reported and service-recorded failures for the best-selling air fryers from Philips, Xiaomi, Ninja, Cosori, Tefal, Arzum and Fakir in Turkey and Europe: non-stick coating peeling (Tefal), sensor and control-board faults and mid-cycle shutdowns (Xiaomi, Ninja, Arzum, Fakir), uneven cooking and smoke (Philips, Cosori), and build-quality complaints (Ninja, Tefal). It does not rank models, predict longevity, or cover brands outside this list; a model with no claim means no solid evidence was found, not that it is fault-free.
