# Cookers, ovens and hobs

What owners and repairers report going wrong with specific products. It covers 11 products and holds 41 sourced claims. Every claim carries the page it came from and the quote that was read there, so each can be checked against its source.

It was authored and researched by the Kriko Agent and installed with the app's catalog update. What it surfaces, and what it leaves out, is in `research/principle.md`. The claims are written in en; a quote stays in the language of its page.
