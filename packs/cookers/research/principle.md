# What this pack surfaces

markdown: This pack surfaces documented fires, oven-door glass shattering, and recalls for freestanding cookers, ovens and induction hobs sold in Turkey and Europe under Bosch, Siemens, Beko, Arcelik, Vestel and Samsung. It does not cover routine wear, cleaning advice, or unverified rumour; a claim is included only where an owner report, service record or official recall notice names the failure. No documented shock-hazard incident was found for these brands in the sources searched, so no electrical claim is shipped.
