Keep a claim when it tells a second-hand buyer something they could not learn by picking the tool up in a shop:

| Keep | Drop (visible in 30s of handling) |
|---|---|
| **Model/platform-specific** — this model or battery platform, not "drills wear out" | Play in the chuck, cracked housing, missing belt clip |
| **Predictable from age/use** — cycles on the pack, hours on the motor | |
| **Expensive relative to the tool** — dead battery platform, stripped gearbox; not a scuffed case | |
| **Consumable and due** — brushes on a brushed motor, at a stated interval | |
