Keep a claim when it tells a second-hand buyer something they could not learn by
picking the tool up in a shop:

- **Specific to this model or battery platform** — not "cordless drills wear out".
- **Predictable from age or use** — cycles on the pack, hours on the motor.
- **Expensive relative to the tool** — a dead battery platform or a stripped
  gearbox matters; a scuffed case does not.
- **Consumable and due** — brushes on a brushed motor, at a stated interval.

Drop anything visible in thirty seconds of handling: play in the chuck, a
cracked housing, a missing belt clip.
